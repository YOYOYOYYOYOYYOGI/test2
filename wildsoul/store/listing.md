**Title:** Vesperglen: The First Light

**Short description (under 80 characters):** Explore a little 3D valley, befriend wildlings, and find your first light.

**Full description:**

The valley remembers every friendship.

Step into Hollow Vale, a handcrafted-feeling 3D world of lantern-lit cottages, winding meadow paths, a rushing river, ancient woods and a cave glowing with quiet mysteries. Follow the trail beyond Thimblewick and meet the wildlings that call this place home.

Choose one of three original companions. Explore on foot with a third-person camera, approach a wildling in its own habitat, and enter a turn-based battle where every move matters. Offer a Wisp Vial to form a bond; a tired wildling is easier to befriend. Build a party, care for your companions, and see how they grow. Bristlebloom can evolve into the luminous Thorncrest as your journey continues.

Your first chapter includes:

• An explorable 3D valley with meadow, village, river, bridge, forest and walk-in cave.
• Twelve original wildling species to discover, with distinct forms and affinities.
• Turn-based encounters, capture vials, healing tonics, party and lodge storage.
• The First Light story quest, helpful village characters and a friendly trainer challenge.
• A field guide, minimap, supplies shop, hidden caches and day-to-dusk lighting.
• Local saves and an original synthesized soundscape. No account, ads or in-app purchases in this demo.

This is an early playable chapter, not a claim that a larger future world is already present. The adventure is offline-first on supported devices. A device with WebGL 2 is required.
