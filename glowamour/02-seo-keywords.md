# 02 — Instagram SEO & Keyword Strategy

## The model you are optimising for (2026, not 2021)

Instagram's own guidance says the text a person types is *"by far the most important signal for Search"* and that it is matched against **usernames, bios, captions, hashtags and places**. On top of that, three things changed the game:

1. **Captions are capped at 5 hashtags** (18 Dec 2025). Instagram says hashtags *"help with search"* but *"don't increase your reach."* Hashtags are now **labelling metadata**, not a distribution lever. Every hashtag set in this file is 5 tags or fewer.
2. **Your public posts are indexable by Google and Bing** (since 10 Jul 2025), and **Google Search Console now reports Instagram posts**. Your caption is a small web page competing on the open internet.
3. **Search reads the image too.** Mosseri (Jul 2026): search uses captions and comments as text signals *and* runs the photo/video through **embedding models** that place visual content in the same mathematical space as the query. A blurry aesthetic shot of a shelf is invisible for "night cream"; a clear, well-lit shot of a night cream jar is not.

**Practical translation:** write like a person describing what they made, put the searchable words early, show the product clearly, and use five honest hashtags. That is the whole of Instagram SEO in 2026.

## PART 4 — The price-transparency decision

Your customers will search `glowamour price` and `night cream price`. You have two options and they lead to different businesses.

**Option 1 — Publish prices openly (🥇 my recommendation).** Put ₹499 in the Reel cover text, the caption, the pinned post, the Highlights and the WhatsApp catalogue.
- *Why:* Indian online skincare shoppers have been burned by "DM for price" accounts. Publishing the price is a **trust signal that costs nothing**, it lets you rank for `night cream under 500` and `night cream price` (high-intent, low-competition), and it filters out non-buyers so your DMs are full of people who already know what it costs. Your existing order tool prices Night Cream at ₹499 — you have nothing to hide.
- *Cost:* competitors see your pricing. At your stage, that is not a real risk.

**Option 2 — "DM for price."** Only defensible if you are doing consultative, personalised pricing or if margins are so thin that public pricing invites undercutting. Neither appears true here.

**Decision: publish prices.** It is the single highest-leverage trust move available to you this week.

## PART 6 — How to build and refresh the keyword list yourself (free, 20 minutes/month)

You do not need a paid tool. Do this monthly:

1. **Instagram autocomplete.** Search bar → type `night cream` and **stop typing**. Read what Instagram suggests. Those are real, ranked, high-volume queries. Repeat with `kesar`, `skincare`, `face cream`, `ahmedabad`, `gujarat`, `night cream for`, `night cream under`.
2. **The Tabs.** For each query, open the **Accounts**, **Tags**, **Places** and **Reels** tabs. Note which Reels rank — read their line-1 captions and on-screen hooks. That is your competition's proven copy.
3. **Comment mining (highest value, most ignored).** Read the comments on your own posts and on 3 competitor accounts. Customers literally type their objections and their search language. Every "does it work for oily skin?" is a keyword and a content idea. Your own dossier's honest-expectation table is the *answer* to most of these — use it.
4. **Google Search Console.** Since ~Jul 2026 it reports Instagram posts and the queries that surfaced them. This is **first-party data on what your Instagram content ranks for on Google** — free, and almost nobody in your category is looking at it. `[VERIFY availability]`
5. **Your own WhatsApp inbox.** The first message a customer sends you *is* their search query. Log the phrasing in a note. It is more accurate than any tool.
6. **Meta Business Suite / Edits "Audience" section** (Aug 2026) shows **what your followers are interested in**. Read it quarterly and adjust the bank.
7. **Google itself.** `site:instagram.com "night cream" ahmedabad` — the closest thing to advanced Instagram search that exists, now that professional accounts are indexable.

Keep the bank in one place (a note, a sheet) and mark each keyword: **P0 (owns now)**, **P1 (targeting)**, **P2 (later)**, **WON** (you appear in results). Reviewing that list monthly is the entire discipline of Instagram SEO.
