# 06 — Instagram Algorithm & Organic Growth

## Part 0 — What I will and won't promise

I will not promise you viral reach, a follower count, a ranking position, or any sales figure. Nobody can, and anyone who does is selling something. What I can do is tell you **which signals Instagram has actually named**, **which of them you control**, and **how to move them deliberately**. Everything else is luck, and luck favours a consistent publishing schedule.

I will also tell you which popular tactics will actively hurt you in 2026.

---

## Part 1 — What Instagram has actually said (first-party, on the record)

There is no single "algorithm." Instagram runs **separate ranking systems** for each surface. Here is what Meta and Adam Mosseri have stated publicly:

### The three signals Mosseri named (January 2025)
> *"The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends. So when looking at your insights, pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach."*

And the distinction that makes it actionable:
> *"Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content."*

**Plain English:** likes help you with people who already follow you. **Sends** — someone forwarding your post in a DM — are what earn you distribution to **strangers**. Since strangers are the entire growth problem, sends are your most important metric.

⚠️ Instagram has **never published numeric weights**. The widely-repeated claim that "sends are worth 3–5× a like" has **no first-party source**. Ignore it; the direction is right, the number is invented.

### Ranking by surface

| Surface | Who it shows your content to | Signals Instagram has named | What that means for GLOWAMOUR |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feed** | Mostly people who follow you, plus some suggested | Your activity (likes/shares/saves/comments) · information about the post (how popular, how *quickly* people engage, when posted, location attached) · information about the author · **your history of interacting with that person** | Feed is a **retention** surface. It rewards relationships. Reply to comments, show up in DMs, post consistently — the ranking is partly a measure of how much your existing audience likes *you* |
| **Stories** | People who follow you | Viewing history · how often they engage with your Stories · **closeness** (likelihood of a real relationship — DMs, mutual follows, interactions) | Stories reward **conversation**. A DM exchange is one of the strongest closeness signals available. Your "DM GLOW" CTA does double duty: it converts *and* it raises your Stories ranking with that person |
| **Explore / Discover** | **Mostly people who do NOT follow you** | Your activity in Explore · information about the post · **popularity — "how many and how quickly other people are liking, commenting, sharing and saving"** which Instagram says matters *"much more in Explore than in Feed or Stories"* · your history with similar topics | Explore rewards **velocity**. Fast, early engagement from people with matching interests is what pushes a post further. This is where saves and shares do the most work |
| **Reels** | Heavily weighted to **non-followers** | Your activity · your history with the author · information about the Reel (**watch time, replays, audio**) · information about the author | Reels are your **only real acquisition engine**. Optimise for average watch time and sends |
| **Search** | Anyone typing a query | **The typed text — "by far the most important signal"** — matched against usernames, bios, captions, hashtags, places · your activity · popularity of the result. Plus (Jul 2026) **embedding models that analyse the photo/video itself** | Search is **pure SEO**. See `02-seo-keywords.md`. Slow, compounding, free, and it keeps working after the post stops getting reach |

## Part 2 — What you control vs what you don't

Being clear about this is what stops you wasting effort on things that feel like work.

### ✅ Fully in your control (spend 90% of your energy here)

| Lever | How to move it | Metric to watch |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook quality (first 1–3 s)** | Keyword on screen + spoken. Payoff stated, not teased. Never "hey guys" | **Average watch time**, skip rate |
| **Watch time & completion** | 15–45 s. Front-load the payoff. Tight editing — cut every pause. Design a reason to watch to the last frame | Average watch time, views-over-time |
| **Rewatchability** | Loops, a reveal at the end, dense info that rewards a second pass | Views ÷ Viewers ratio |
| **Sendability** ⭐ | Make content that is *useful to a specific other person*: "send this to someone who skips moisturiser", checklists, comparison tables, myth-busting. This is the #1 growth lever | **Sends per reach** |
| **Saveability** | Reference content: routines, timelines, ingredient lists, policies, "how to read a label" | Saves per reach |
| **Originality** | Shoot your own product, your own city, your own hands. 100% of posts | Account Status |
| **Keyword placement** | Name field, bio, caption line 1, on-screen text, spoken audio, alt text, comment replies | Search appearances, Google queries in Search Console |
| **Reply speed & depth** | Reply to every comment within a few hours, with a real sentence (not an emoji). Substantive replies are read as text signals for search | Comment volume, DM volume, Stories ranking |
| **Posting consistency** | 3 Reels + 1 carousel + daily Stories. Same time each day. Batch-shoot weekly | Content-you-shared count, follower growth |
| **Profile → follow conversion** | Designed Reel covers, 3 pinned posts, 8 Highlights, published prices | **Profile visits → Follows** ratio |
| **CTA clarity** | One action per post. "DM GLOW" everywhere | DM inquiries, link taps |
| **Comment quality** | Ask a specific answerable question, not engagement bait | Meaningful comment count |

### ❌ Not in your control (stop worrying about these)

- **Which specific test audience** Instagram shows your Reel to first
- **How many impressions** you get from any individual post
- **Whether a post goes to Explore** — you cannot request or force it
- **What your competitors do**
- **Timing/luck** — the same Reel can perform 10× differently on different days
- **The exact weights** of any signal — never published
- **Follower count as a vanity metric** — Instagram does not rank you on it, and bought followers actively hurt you by collapsing your per-reach ratios

### ⚠️ Partially in your control
- **Trending audio** — you can browse and use it, but relevance matters more than trendiness. A forced trending audio on a skincare tutorial signals confusion
- **Time of day** — Edits' Audience section (Aug 2026) and Insights "most active times" tell you when *your* followers are on. Post then. But most of a Reel's lifetime reach comes days later, so this is a minor optimisation, not a lever

## Part 3 — The sends-per-reach system (your growth engine)

If you take one thing from this file, take this. Here is a repeatable system for making content people forward.

### The send test
Before publishing anything, ask: **"Who specifically would forward this to whom, and what would they say in the message?"**

If you cannot answer with a real sentence, the content is not sendable and it will not reach non-followers.

| If the answer is… | The content will… |
|---|---|
| *"My sister, saying 'stop wasting money on this'"* | Travel. This is the format to build |
| *"Nobody, but it looks nice"* | Die at your follower count |

### Six sendable formats that work for a skincare brand

| Format | Example for GLOWAMOUR | Why it gets sent |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The warning** | "3 skincare products that are a waste of money at 21" | People forward warnings to protect someone they care about |
| **2. The checklist** | "5 questions to ask before buying any skincare online" | Utility. Saved *and* sent |
| **3. The comparison** | "Cream vs gel moisturiser for Gujarat humidity" | Resolves an argument. People send it as evidence |
| **4. The counter-intuitive fact** | "Your night cream isn't the problem — hard water is" | Social currency. The sender looks smart |
| **5. The radical honesty** ⭐ | "What our night cream CANNOT do" | **Disbelief.** People send it because they cannot believe a brand said it. This is your highest-potential format |
| **6. The local truth** | "Ahmedabad tap water and your skin — what's actually happening" | Identity. People send local content to local friends |

### The CTA that generates sends without being bait
❌ "Tag 5 friends" — engagement bait, penalised by Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines.
✅ "Send this to someone who skips moisturiser." — specific, useful, socially natural, and it names the exact person the viewer should think of.
✅ "Save this for your next shopping trip."
✅ "Which one do you use — cream or gel? Tell me below." — a real question that generates real comments.

## Part 4 — Reaching non-followers: the practical playbook

### For Reels (your main acquisition channel)
1. **Hook in 1–3 seconds, keyword visible and spoken.** Most viewers are muted and decide instantly.
2. **15–45 seconds.** Under the 3-minute recommendation ceiling with room to spare. Shorter is easier to complete; completion drives watch time.
3. **Cut ruthlessly.** Every pause is a skip. The Edits app's **First Draft** (Aug 2026) auto-trims clips and removes pauses in under 10 seconds — use it as a starting point, then refine.
4. **On-screen text always.** Bilingual Gujarati + English via Edits. Doubles your searchable surface and works muted.
5. **One idea per Reel.** Two ideas = two Reels.
6. **End frame = product + price + CTA**, 2 seconds. Many viewers never open the caption.
7. **Designed cover** with topic text — this converts profile visits into follows.
8. **Post 3× a week minimum.** Reels reward volume more than any other surface because each one is an independent lottery ticket with a real test audience.
9. **Reply to every comment within hours**, substantively. Comments are read as text signals, and reply threads extend the post's active life.
10. **Once you pass 200 followers: use Trial Reels** to test 2–3 hooks on non-followers before committing one to your grid. Insights after ~24 h. Note that Trial Reel scheduling (Apr 2026) has been reported intermittently greyed out since Sep 2026 — do not build a plan that depends on it.

### For carousels (your save engine)
- Slide 1 must be a **claim or question in large type** — it is both the hook and your grid thumbnail.
- **1080×1350 (4:5)** — the grid previews at 3:4, so square designs get cropped.
- One idea per slide, ≤25 words, large font.
- The **swipe-through rate** is the carousel equivalent of watch time. A 7-slide carousel where people reach slide 7 is a strong signal.
- Final slide = CTA.
- Carousels are **indexable by Google** and are your best "reference content" asset — they keep earning search traffic for months.

### For Stories (your conversion engine)
- Stories do not reach non-followers. Their job is to convert the people you already have and to raise your **closeness** signal, which improves your Feed and Stories ranking with each individual.
- **DMs are the highest-value Story outcome.** Every conversation makes Instagram more likely to show that person your next Story. Your "DM GLOW" CTA is therefore a ranking tactic as well as a sales tactic.
- Use interactive stickers daily. Every tap is a cheap engagement signal.
- Use **link stickers** on commercial frames — Insights reports Story **link taps** (since Jun 2026), so you can finally measure Story → WhatsApp conversion.

### For comments and conversations (the underrated multiplier)
- **Reply to every comment** in the first 6 hours. Reply with a real sentence that contains a keyword — comments and replies are read as text signals for search (Mosseri, Jul 2026).
  - ❌ "Thanks! 🙏"
  - ✅ "Yes — this night cream works well in Ahmedabad humidity, and COD is available across Gujarat."
- **Ask a specific answerable question** in the caption. "Which do you use — cream or gel?" outperforms "Thoughts?"
- **DM everyone who comments something substantive**, once, without pitching. "Saw your comment about hard water — that's genuinely the biggest factor in Gujarat. Happy to help if you want." This converts and it builds closeness.
- **Never** do engagement pods, follow-for-follow, or comment groups. They produce interactions from accounts with no interest in skincare, which teaches Instagram to show your content to the wrong people. You will gain followers and lose reach.

### For profile-visit → follow conversion
Reach means nothing if visitors bounce. Check this ratio weekly:
```
Follows ÷ Profile visits
```
If you have good reach but a poor ratio, the problem is **not your content** — it is your profile. Fix in this order: Reel covers → bio line 1 → pinned posts → Highlights → grid consistency. (`03-profile-layout-trust.md`)

## Part 5 — Consistency and interaction: the compounding part

Instagram's Feed ranking includes *"your history of interacting with the person who posted."* That is a **relationship** score, built by repeated contact. It means:

- **Posting consistently matters more than posting brilliantly.** Four adequate Reels a week beat one excellent Reel a month, because each post is a fresh opportunity to be shown, tested, and engaged with.
- **Being active in DMs and comments is a ranking input**, not just customer service. Every reply raises your closeness with that person.
- **Disappearing for two weeks costs you more than the lost posts.** Your relationship scores decay and you have to rebuild distribution.

### The minimum viable schedule (if you are very busy)
- 2 Reels/week (batch-shot in one 90-minute session)
- 1 carousel/week (repurposed from the Reels)
- Stories 3–4 days/week (2 frames each)
- **Reply to every comment and DM, daily** — this is non-negotiable and takes 15 minutes

Do this for 90 days before judging anything.

## Part 6 — A no-budget 90-day growth plan

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Foundation** | 1–2 | Profile per `01`, layout per `03`, 12 posts live, 3 pins, 8 Highlights, WhatsApp Business + catalogue, saved replies, Account Status checked | A stranger can understand the business in 7 seconds |
| **2. Volume** | 3–6 | 3 Reels + 1 carousel + daily Stories, every week, no exceptions. Batch-shoot weekly. Reply to everything | 20+ posts published; you know which 2–3 formats get the best watch time |
| **3. Learn** | 5–8 | Read Insights weekly (`07`). Identify your top 3 posts by **sends per reach**. Make 3 more like each. Kill what does not work | A repeatable format you can produce in 30 minutes |
| **4. Cross 200 followers** | 6–12 | At 200 followers on a professional account, **Trial Reels unlock**. Start testing hooks on non-followers before committing them | 2–3 trial-tested hooks per month |
| **5. Local authority** | 8–12 | Double down on Gujarat content: hard water, humidity, festivals, weddings, Gujarati-language Reels. Tag real locations. Engage genuinely with Ahmedabad/Gujarat accounts (comment usefully, not spam) | Local search appearances; orders from multiple Gujarat cities |
| **6. Conversion** | 10–13 | First small paid test **only if** organic conversion is proven. Boost your single best-performing Reel with a small budget, geo-targeted to Gujarat, for 5 days. Measure cost per WhatsApp conversation | Real data on paid CAC before committing |

### When to spend money (and when not to)
**Do not spend until:** you have 30+ posts, a proven format, a working WhatsApp order path, and at least 10 organic orders. Boosting content that has not proven itself organically is how small brands burn ₹5,000 learning nothing.

**When you do spend, in this order:**
1. **Boost your best organic Reel** (highest sends per reach), 5 days, small budget, Gujarat-only geo. Cheapest possible test.
2. Then a **lead/message campaign** optimised for WhatsApp conversations — that is your actual conversion event.
3. Only then consider catalogue/Advantage+ — which **requires a website**, because Instagram Shopping in India has no in-app checkout.

⚠️ **Meta ad policy note for cosmetics:** Meta's personal-care policy restricts cosmetic ads to 18+, prohibits "statements of inferiority about physical appearance," and prohibits permanent skin whitening/bleaching claims. Your honest positioning keeps you compliant by default — but check ad copy against `11-compliance-claims.md` before submitting.

## Part 7 — Tactics that will hurt you (do not do these)

| ❌ Tactic | What actually happens |
|---|---|
| **Buy followers** | Your follower count rises; your **likes-per-reach and sends-per-reach collapse**. Instagram ranks on ratios, not totals. You will have 10,000 followers and 200 views per Reel — and no way to fix it |
| **Engagement pods / follow-trains** | Interactions come from accounts with no interest in skincare. Instagram learns your content belongs to the wrong interest cluster and shows it to people who will never buy |
| **Follow/unfollow at scale** | Detectable, damages your account standing, and produces followers who immediately disengage |
| **Engagement bait** ("like if you agree", "tag 5 friends") | Explicitly treated as a low-quality signal in Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines. Can cost you recommendation eligibility |
| **Reposting stock/aesthetic skincare content** | **Account-level** loss of recommendations under the Apr 2026 rule, judged over a rolling 30-day window |
| **30 hashtags** | You cannot — capped at 5. Generic tags can actively hurt |
| **Deleting underperforming posts immediately** | You destroy your own data and you churn your 30-day originality window. Archive instead |
| **Reels over 3 minutes for discovery** | Not recommended to non-followers |
| **Buying a "verified badge" from a third party** | Scam. Verification only comes from Meta directly |
| **AI-generated before/afters** | Fabricated evidence. ASCI/CCPA exposure plus loss of every real customer's trust when discovered |
| **Posting 5× a day for a week then vanishing** | Volatility is worse than modest consistency. Relationship scores decay |

## Part 8 — The honest summary

Your growth in 2026 comes from four things, in this order:

1. **Reels under 45 seconds, with your own footage, built to be sent to a friend.**
2. **A profile that converts a visit into a follow** (covers, pins, Highlights, published prices).
3. **Keywords in the right fields** so search and Google keep finding you after the reach dies down.
4. **Staying recommendation-eligible** — original content, no engagement bait, Account Status checked monthly.

None of these require a budget. All of them require you to show up four times a week for three months. That is the entire secret, and it is also why most brands do not do it.
