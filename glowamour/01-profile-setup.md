## 1. Username (handle)

### What Instagram allows
Up to **30 characters**; letters, numbers, **periods** and **underscores** only. No spaces, no hyphens, no symbols. Must be unique across all of Instagram. Changing it later is possible but you lose the old URL and any recognition you built — so choose once, carefully.

### 🥇 Primary recommendation: `glowamour.in`  (12 chars)

**Why this one wins for you specifically:**

| Reason | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Brand-first** | "GLOWAMOUR" is the asset you are building. It leads. People who hear the name on a Reel can type it and find you. |
| **`.in` signals India without costing characters** | `india` costs 5 characters and reads clunkier; `.in` costs 3 and is an established Indian D2C convention. It also quietly tells a Gujarati shopper "this ships to me, this is not a dropshipper from abroad." |
| **Readable aloud** | *"glowamour dot in"* — one clean phrase you can say at the end of every Reel, print on a label, or put on a visiting card. Compare `glowamour_official` which you cannot say cleanly. |
| **Search-friendly** | Instagram search matches typed text against usernames first. A shopper typing "glowamour" gets an exact-prefix match. |
| **Consistency** | `glowamour.in` maps to the domain `glowamour.in`, which is the domain you would actually buy. Same string on WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile, YouTube, Facebook — no mental translation for customers. |

### Ranked alternatives

Try in this order. Stop at the first one that is free.

| # | Handle | Chars | Why / trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `glowamour` | 9 | The prize. Almost certainly taken or squatted, but **check it first** — if free, take it and use nothing else. |
| 2 | **`glowamour.in`** | 12 | 🥇 Best realistic option. India signal + clean + brand-first. |
| 3 | `glowamourindia` | 14 | Solid. Slightly less elegant to say aloud, but zero ambiguity and no punctuation for customers to misremember. |
| 4 | `glowamour.india` | 15 | Same idea with a period. Fine, but `glowamour.in` is shorter. |
| 5 | `shopglowamour` | 13 | **Commercial-intent prefix.** "shop" tells both the algorithm and the visitor this is a store, not a mood board. Strong fallback if you want a transactional identity. |
| 6 | `glowamour.skin` | 14 | Adds a **category keyword** ("skin"). Best if you expect to stay skincare-only forever. |
| 7 | `glowamourskincare` | 17 | Maximum keyword density, maximum length. Acceptable, less memorable. |
| 8 | `glowamourcare` | 13 | Softer, works if you later add haircare/bodycare. |
| 9 | `glowamour.ahmedabad` | 19 | **Only** if you decide to be a hyper-local Ahmedabad brand and never ship nationally. This is a ceiling, not a floor — I do not recommend it given your all-India COD plan. |

### Handles to avoid — and the specific reason for each

| Avoid | Reason |
|---|---|
| `glowamour_official`, `glowamour.real`, `originalglowamour` | Defensive words like "official"/"real"/"original" *signal the opposite* — they make a new brand look like it is fighting impersonators it does not have yet. Only adopt one if you are actively defending against a copycat. They also waste searchable characters. |
| `glowamour123`, `glowamour_2026`, `_glowamour_` | Numbers and trailing/leading underscores are the visual signature of fake and spam accounts. Indian shoppers are trained to distrust them, and it directly undermines the trust objective you set. |
| `bestnightcream`, `glownightcream`, `nightcreamindia` | A generic keyword handle with no brand in it means **no one can find you by name**, and you cannot build a trademark or a recognisable wordmark later. Also: `best`/`no1` are superlative claims ASCI has specifically flagged in personal care. |
| `dermaglowamour`, `glowamourclinic`, `glowamourderma`, `drglowamour` | Implies medical/dermatological authority you do not have. This is a regulatory exposure under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act framing, not just a style issue. |
| `organicglowamour`, `naturalglowamour`, `ayurvedicglowamour`, `herbalglowamour` | These are **substantiation-required claims baked into your permanent URL**. "Ayurvedic" implies an AYUSH manufacturing licence; "organic" implies certification; "natural" is exactly the sort of absolute claim ASCI's greenwashing guidelines and the CCPA target. Only use one if you hold the paperwork. |
| `fairglowamour`, `glowamourfairness`, `whiteningglow` | Fairness/whitening is the single most regulated and most reputationally damaged claim space in Indian cosmetics advertising. ASCI has dedicated guidelines for it; the CCPA has fined brands for it. Never put it in a handle. See `11-compliance-claims.md`. |
| Anything containing `kesari`, `vicco`, `fairandlovely`, or another brand's mark | Trademark infringement risk. |

### How to verify availability (do not assume — check all five)

I cannot see Instagram from here, so **you** must confirm. Five checks, in this order:

1. **Direct URL test (fastest, no login).** In a browser where you are *not* logged into Instagram, open `https://www.instagram.com/glowamour.in/`.
   - "Page not found" / "Sorry, this page isn't available" → **likely free**.
   - A profile loads → **taken**.
   - A login wall → inconclusive, go to step 2.
2. **In-app search.** Instagram → magnifying glass → type the exact handle → tap the **Accounts** tab. No exact match = likely free. An exact match with 0 posts may still be a reserved/abandoned account; you will find out at step 3.
3. **The definitive test — the signup rename flow.** This is the only method that is actually authoritative:
   - If you already have the account: **Profile → Edit profile → Username**. Type the candidate. Instagram tells you instantly whether it is available or not. **Do not press Done until you are sure** — you can back out.
   - If creating new: start the signup flow and enter the username at that step. It validates in real time.
   - ⚠️ **Username change cooldown:** Instagram limits how often you can change your username, and the old handle may become temporarily locked to you or released. Do not "test" by repeatedly renaming a live account. Decide, then set it once.
4. **Trademark clearance (free, 5 minutes, do this before you commit).** GLOWAMOUR is a brand you want to own. Search the **IP India public trademark registry** for the word "GLOWAMOUR" in **Class 3** (cosmetics, skincare, soap). If a live mark exists in Class 3, pick a different brand name now — before you print labels. Also check the **MCA company-name search** if you intend to incorporate. `[VERIFY — registry data changes]`
5. **Cross-platform reservation.** Once the handle is yours on Instagram, immediately take the **same string** on: WhatsApp Business display name, Facebook Page, YouTube handle, Google Business Profile, Pinterest. Consistent naming is itself a trust signal and a minor SEO signal, and it stops squatters.

**If your top choice is taken:** do not append numbers. Move down the ranked list. If `glowamour.in`, `glowamourindia` and `shopglowamour` are all gone, the honest conclusion is that a similar name already trades in your category — which is a brand-strategy problem worth solving *before* you spend money on packaging, not after.

## 2. Display name (the "Name" field)

### Why this is the most valuable free SEO real estate you own
Instagram's own search guidance says results are matched by text and that *"using an Instagram handle or profile name that's related to the content of your posts is your best bet for showing up in relevant searches."* The Name field is:
- **separately searchable** from your username (two independent keyword slots),
- **shown in bold** at the top of your profile and in search results,
- **editable** without losing anything,
- and **limited to 30 characters** `[VERIFY IN-APP — Instagram publishes no official number; 30 is the observed limit]`.

Most brands waste it by repeating the handle. You will not.

### 🥇 Set it to: `GLOWAMOUR | Night Cream India`  (29 / 30 characters)

Counted, verified, and constructed as: **BRAND + separator + PRIMARY KEYWORD + MARKET**.

Why this exact string:
- `GLOWAMOUR` first → brand recognition and brand search still work.
- `Night Cream` → your **main product's highest-volume category keyword**. Someone in Surat typing "night cream" into Instagram search has a real chance of surfacing you; someone typing it has commercial intent, not curiosity.
- `India` → qualifies the market, adds a second searchable term, and reassures on shipping.
- The `|` (pipe) separator reads cleanly and is a widely used convention that does not look like keyword stuffing.

### Rotation plan — because you can change this field
The Name field is one of the few genuinely cheap A/B tests Instagram gives you. Run each for **at least 14 days** and record *Profile visits* and *Follows* from Insights (`07-measurement.md` shows you how).

| Phase | Name field | Chars | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | `GLOWAMOUR | Night Cream India` | 29 | Baseline: product keyword + market |
| Days 31–44 | `GLOWAMOUR | Skincare Ahmedabad` | 30 | **Local intent.** Does "Ahmedabad" pull more Gujarat profile visits and more WhatsApp opens? |
| Days 45–58 | `GLOWAMOUR | Night Cream & Soap` | 30 | **Two products.** Once Kesar Goti Soap launches, does naming both beat naming one? |
| Days 59–72 | `GLOWAMOUR | Kesar Soap & Cream` | 30 | **Gujarati-adjacent keyword.** Does "Kesar" pull a different, higher-intent Gujarati audience? |

⚠️ **Cooldown:** Instagram restricts how frequently the Name field can be edited (commonly reported as **twice per 14 days**). Confirm the message you get in-app before planning tight rotations, and never change it mid-campaign. `[VERIFY IN-APP]`

### Name-field mistakes to avoid
- `GLOWAMOUR Official` — wastes 9 characters on a word nobody searches.
- `GLOWAMOUR ✨🌙💫` — emoji are not searchable text. Zero discovery value.
- `GLOWAMOUR | Best Night Cream in India | Buy Online | COD | Ahmedabad` — over the limit **and** keyword stuffing. Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines explicitly keep low-quality signals (including text unrelated to the content) out of recommendations. Stuffing risks your *eligibility*, not just your aesthetics.
- All-lowercase `glowamour` — you lose the visual authority of a capitalised wordmark.

## 3. Bio (150 characters)

### The job your bio has to do
A visitor arrives from a Reel with roughly **3 seconds** of patience. Your bio must answer, without them scrolling: *What do you sell? Is this real? Will it reach me? How do I buy?* Instagram's own guidance is to *"include relevant keywords and locations in your bio"* and notes that for a small business, *"sharing your location in your bio can make it easier for people in your area to find you."*

**Critical mechanical detail most brands get wrong:** text inside the bio is **not clickable**. Only the dedicated **Links** section is. So your bio must *point* at the links and give a **separate, trackable action** (a DM keyword). Never write "click the link in my bio" as your only CTA — write both.

### 🥇 Option A — The Balanced Seller (recommended to launch with)

```
Night cream + Kesar Goti Soap for Indian skin 🌙
Ships across India 📦 COD available
Ahmedabad, Gujarat
DM “GLOW” to order 👇
```
**125 / 150 units · 4 lines · 25 units of headroom**

| Line | What it does | Keyword value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Names **both products** + the audience ("Indian skin" — a deliberate positioning phrase, not a claim) | `night cream`, `kesar goti soap`, `indian skin` |
| 2 | Kills the #1 objection of an online-only skincare brand: *"will it actually reach me, and can I pay on delivery?"* | `ships across india`, `COD` — buying-intent terms |
| 3 | **Local trust + local SEO.** Instagram explicitly recommends location in the bio for small businesses. "Ahmedabad, Gujarat" makes you a real place, not a dropshipper | `ahmedabad`, `gujarat` |
| 4 | A **single, unambiguous, trackable action.** Every "GLOW" DM is a lead you can count | — |

Why "DM 'GLOW'" and not "DM to order": a **fixed keyword** is countable. You can literally tally how many "GLOW" messages you get per week and attribute them to specific Reels. Later it becomes the trigger for automation. It also feels lower-friction than "message us" because the customer knows exactly what to type.

### Option B — Trust & Provenance (use this once you can prove manufacturing)

```
Made-in-India skincare from Ahmedabad 🇮🇳
Night cream · Kesar Goti Soap
Real ingredients. Real batch numbers.
Order on WhatsApp 👇
```
**131 / 150 units**

Stronger for the ingredient-aware urban segment (`04-audience.md`, Segment 4). "Real batch numbers" is a claim you can only make if batch numbers are genuinely printed on your packs — **do not use this bio until that is true.** It is, however, a devastatingly effective differentiator in a market full of unlabelled product, and your existing label-printing tool means you are unusually well placed to deliver it.

### Option C — Bilingual Gujarati-first (use this if your first 90 days are Gujarat-only)

```
રાત્રિનો ક્રીમ • કેસર ગોટી સાબુ 🌙
Night cream & saffron soap | Ahmedabad
All-India shipping 📦 COD + UPI
DM “GLOW” to order 👇
```
**127 / 150 units**

Gujarati line first is a **signal of who you are for**. A Gujarati-speaking customer in Rajkot or Surat sees their own language in the first line and the account stops feeling like a Mumbai or Delhi brand. Line 2 keeps the English keywords for search indexing — Instagram's search does not need the Gujarati to be the only version.

⚠️ Use "saffron soap" in English **only if saffron is genuinely in the formula** (open question #2 in `00-START-HERE.md`). If not, write `Kesar Goti Soap` in both lines and do not translate it as an ingredient claim.

### Option D — Honest-Positioning (the differentiation play)

```
Skincare that shows its work.
Night cream · Kesar Goti Soap
Full ingredient list on every post
Ahmedabad → all India 📦
DM “GLOW” 👇
```
**132 / 150 units**

This is the bio that matches the positioning insight at the end of `00-START-HERE.md`. *"Full ingredient list on every post"* is a promise you can actually keep, it is verifiable by any visitor in ten seconds, and given that ASCI found ~98% of scrutinised Indian beauty ads needed modification, it reads as a challenge to the entire category. Only use it if you will genuinely publish full INCI lists.

### Option E — Concern-led (best if the night cream targets dullness/dryness)

```
Gentle night cream for dull, tired skin 🌙
+ Kesar Goti Soap
Ahmedabad, Gujarat 📍 COD all India
Tap WhatsApp to order 👇
```
**121 / 150 units**

"dull, tired skin" is a **concern keyword** people actually search, and it is a *cosmetic-appearance* claim (safe) rather than a treatment claim (unsafe). This bio will match more searches than Option A but says less about the brand. Good second-choice.

### Option F — Anti-claim positioning (bold; use only with founder-led content)

```
No fairness promises. Just hydration, glow & honest labels.
Night cream + Kesar Goti Soap
Ahmedabad 📦 COD
DM “GLOW” 👇
```
**119 / 150 units**

Directly invokes the fairness debate. Powerful and *legally safer than the alternative* — ASCI's fairness guidelines are about ads that demean darker skin, and refusing to make fairness claims puts you on the right side. But it spends your first line on what you are *not*, which is weaker for search. Use it once you have an audience that already knows the category.

### Bio rules that apply to every option

**Do**
- Line breaks between every idea. A wall of text is not read on a phone.
- One CTA. Two competing CTAs ("DM us" *and* "call us" *and* "visit website") produce fewer actions than one clear one.
- Front-load. Instagram truncates the bio in some surfaces (search results, suggested profiles, share previews). Your first line must carry the product.
- Keep a searchable noun in line 1 and line 2. Emoji are decoration; nouns are indexing.

**Do not**
- ❌ Write claims you cannot substantiate: `100% natural`, `chemical-free`, `no side effects`, `dermatologist tested` (unless you hold an actual test report), `clinically proven`, `guaranteed results`, `fairness`, `whitening`, `permanent`, `cures acne`, `removes pigmentation in 7 days`. Every one of these is either an ASCI Chapter I substantiation demand, a CCPA misleading-advertisement exposure, or both. Full list in `11-compliance-claims.md`.
- ❌ Put a raw phone number or email in the bio text. It is not clickable there, it wastes characters, and it belongs in the **Contact options** buttons instead.
- ❌ Use a link shortener (`bit.ly`, `tinyurl`) in the Links field. It looks like spam to a wary first-time buyer and destroys your own click attribution.
- ❌ Leave the bio as a tagline with no nouns. *"Chasing the glow ✨"* contains nothing a searcher could ever type.
- ❌ Change the bio every week. Give each version 14 days minimum so Insights data means anything.

## 4. Profile picture (logo)

### The constraint that decides everything
Instagram displays the profile photo as a **circle, at roughly 32–110 pixels** depending on surface — tiny in the feed, in comments, in Stories reply trays, and in the Reels rail. Your logo must survive at **32 px**. Most skincare brands fail this: they upload an ornate wordmark with a thin serif and it becomes grey mush.

### 🥇 Recommended: a monogram mark + wordmark pair
- **Profile photo = monogram only.** A single **"G"** letterform, or **"GA"**, inside a filled circle or rounded square. Thick strokes, high contrast, no fine detail, no gradient thin-lines.
- **Full "GLOWAMOUR" wordmark** lives elsewhere: on packaging, on carousel slides, on your Reel end-card, on the website.

This is how you get brand recognition at small size. A visitor who has seen your "G" thirty times in the Reels rail starts to recognise it before they can read it.

### Technical spec
| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| File format | JPG or PNG (PNG if you need a clean edge) |
| Upload size | **1080 × 1080 px** minimum; 320 × 320 is the stated floor but do not use it |
| Aspect | 1:1 square — Instagram crops to a circle, so keep **all critical content inside the central circle** |
| Safe zone | Nothing important in the outer ~12% of each edge; it will be cropped |
| Contrast | Must pass a "squint test" — shrink the image to 32 px on your screen. If you cannot tell what it is, redesign it |
| Background | **Solid, dark or saturated.** Avoid white backgrounds — on Instagram's light UI a white-background logo looks like an empty circle |
| Faces | No small faces. A face at 32 px is unrecognisable and reads as a stock photo |
| Text in the mark | Maximum one letter or two. Never a full word at this size |

### Colour direction (a hypothesis, not a rule)
Given the brand name and the kesar/saffron product, a **deep saffron-amber + off-black/charcoal** pairing would be distinctive in a category drowning in pastel pink, sage green and clinical white. Saffron-amber also carries immediate cultural resonance for a Gujarati audience (kesar, haldi, festive, auspicious) without needing explanation.

**Test it before committing:** open Instagram, screenshot the Reels feed and your competitor set, drop your logo candidate into the screenshot at real size, and see whether it disappears or stands out. That is a 10-minute test that beats any theory.

### What not to use
- ❌ A **product photo** as the avatar. It reads as a listing, not a brand, and jars at small size.
- ❌ An **AI-generated model's face**. Beyond the trust problem, Instagram's Aug 2026 "AI-generated profile" policy attaches **reach penalties** to profiles featuring undisclosed AI-generated people (`00-START-HERE.md`, fact #15).
- ❌ A **stock logo** bought from a template marketplace. It will appear on other accounts, and duplicate-detection plus sheer customer exposure will make you look generic.
- ❌ Changing it often. The avatar is your primary recognition asset; every change resets recognition.

## 5. Profile category

### What the category actually does
It is the label under your name (`Product/Service`, `Skin Care Service`, `Beauty Brand`, etc.). It (a) tells visitors what kind of thing you are, (b) is used by Instagram as a **classification signal** for who to suggest you to and which interest clusters you belong to, and (c) gates some tools. You can **hide the label** from the profile if you prefer a cleaner look — my recommendation is to **show it** while the account is small, because a stranger needs the category cue more than they need the minimalism.

### 🥇 Choose in this priority order
Instagram's category list is drawn from Facebook's page categories and **varies by account and region**, so search the picker for these in order and take the first that exists:

1. **`Beauty Brand`** — appears in current category pickers and is the most precise description of what you are: a *brand*, not a shop and not a salon. Best signal-to-noise.
2. **`Skin Care Service`** — a real, long-standing option. Slightly service-flavoured (implies treatments/facials) but strongly topical for search clustering around skincare.
3. **`Cosmetics`** / **`Beauty, Cosmetic & Personal Care`** — accurate for a product business; `Beauty, Cosmetic & Personal Care` is the broad Product/Service parent and is a safe default.
4. **`Shopping & Retail`** — acceptable if none of the above appear, and it does reinforce "this is where you buy things."
5. **`Product/Service`** — the generic fallback. Weakest signal. Use only if nothing better is offered.

### Do not choose
- ❌ **`Health/Beauty`** — too broad, clusters you with salons and supplement sellers.
- ❌ **`Skin Care Clinic` / `Dermatologist` / `Medical & Health`** — implies clinical/medical authority. Regulatory exposure, and it sets customer expectations you will disappoint.
- ❌ **`Ayurvedic` / `Holistic` / `Alternative & Holistic Health Service`** — unless you hold an AYUSH manufacturing licence. This is a substantiation claim in a regulated field.
- ❌ **`Personal Blog`** — you will get creator tools but you lose business contact buttons and the commercial classification.
- ❌ **`Entrepreneur`** — clusters you with hustle-content accounts, not skincare shoppers.

**Business vs Creator account interacts with this** — see §9.

## 6. Contact information & business contact buttons

### Where: `Profile → Edit profile → Contact options`

### 🥇 Configure all three of these

| Field | What to enter | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** | Your business WhatsApp number, with country code `+91` | **The highest-value button on your entire profile.** Your order flow already runs on WhatsApp (I read it in your repo). A one-tap WhatsApp button removes every step between "interested" and "talking to you." In Gujarat and across India, WhatsApp is the default commerce conversation layer — a customer who will not fill a web form *will* send a WhatsApp. Set up **WhatsApp Business** (free), not personal WhatsApp: you get a catalogue, quick replies, labels, and away messages. |
| **Email** | A brand address — `orders@glowamour.in` or `hello@glowamour.in` | Not for orders; for **credibility and dispute resolution.** A gmail address is fine on day 1 but a domain-matched email measurably increases perceived legitimacy for a first-time buyer. Also required for some marketplace and payment-gateway KYC flows later. |
| **Phone** | Optional — only if you will actually answer it | If you will not answer calls, **do not add it.** An unanswered business line is worse than no line: it converts curiosity into distrust. If you add it, set expectations in the bio or a highlight ("calls 11am–7pm"). |

### Business address
`Edit profile → Public business information → Business address`.

- **Recommendation: set city and state only — Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.** Leave the street address blank unless you have a real retail location customers can visit.
- **Why city-level:** it feeds local discovery (Instagram matches location text for local queries) and it satisfies the trust need ("this is a real business in a real city") without exposing a home address.
- **Why not a full street address:** if you are operating from home, publishing it is a privacy and safety risk, and it sets a "come to my shop" expectation you cannot meet.
- ⚠️ Note that **cosmetics manufacturing premises must be in a notified industrial area, not a residential home**, under the Cosmetics Rules 2020 (`formulation/day-cream-v1-india-dossier.md` §8A). Your *registered business address* for Instagram is a marketing field and does not have to be your manufacturing address — but if you are manufacturing yourself, the manufacturing address is a licensing question, not a marketing one.
- Turn on **"Show contact info on profile"** so the buttons render. `[VERIFY IN-APP label]`

## 7. Website / ordering link

### The honest situation
Your ordering channel today is **WhatsApp + Google Sheets**, per the tool in this repo. Instagram Shopping in India has **no in-app checkout** — since September 2025 every product tag sends the shopper to *your website*, and India sits in Meta's "open beta" group where some sellers lack full capabilities. So: **do not build your conversion path around Instagram Shop until you have a website worth sending people to.**

### Where: `Profile → Edit profile → Links`
Instagram gives you a dedicated **Links** section (not bio text) that renders as tappable chips above your action buttons. You can add more than one. **Use two, in this order:**

### 🥇 Link 1 — WhatsApp (primary)
`https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%20GLOWAMOUR%2C%20I%20want%20to%20order`

- Replace `XXXXXXXXXX` with your 10-digit number, no `+`, no spaces, no leading `0`.
- The `?text=` parameter **pre-fills the customer's first message**. This is a real conversion lift: the customer taps and a message is already written, so they only press send. It also means every inbound lead arrives tagged with its source, which you can read in your own WhatsApp.
- **Make a separate pre-filled link per campaign** once you are running several: `?text=Hi%2C%20I%20saw%20your%20night%20cream%20Reel`. Now you know which Reel produced the order. This is free attribution that most small Indian brands never set up.

### 🥈 Link 2 — your store / catalogue / payment page
Pick the highest-trust option you can actually stand up, in this order:

| Option | Cost | When to use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp Business Catalogue** | Free | **Now, this week.** Settings → Business tools → Catalogue → Add item (images ≥600×600, title, price, description, code). Customers browse products inside the chat. | No website needed. Native in India. UPI payment inside WhatsApp is live. **This is your single best next move.** |
| **Razorpay Payment Link / Payment Page** | ~2% + GST per transaction | Now, for prepaid orders | Designed explicitly for no-website sellers; shareable over WhatsApp/DM. `[VERIFY current promotional offers — a 90-day fee waiver for new merchants up to ₹5,00,000 GMV was reported for activations on/after 1 Jul 2026]` |
| **Instamojo / Dukaan / WatEase / similar** | ~₹200–500/mo | Within 60–90 days, once you have repeat orders | Gives you a real product page with a URL you can point Instagram at — which is the prerequisite for Shops, catalogue sync and Google Shopping later |
| **Your own domain + Shopify/WooCommerce** | Domain ~₹800/yr + platform | Once monthly revenue justifies it | The endgame. Required for Instagram product tags to be useful in India, required for Google Shopping, and the thing that makes you look like a real brand to a first-time ₹499 buyer |
| **A link-in-bio page (Linktree/Beacons)** | Free–cheap | **Avoid if possible** | Every extra tap loses buyers, and a link-hub reads as "this business does not have a website." If you must, put WhatsApp as button #1 and make it look branded, not templated |

### Rules for the link field
- ❌ **Never** a URL shortener. You lose click data and you inherit a spam aesthetic.
- ❌ Never a raw Google Form or Google Sheet link as your primary. It looks unfinished.
- ✅ Use your **own domain** as soon as you have one (`glowamour.in`) — matching handle, link and domain is a strong legitimacy cue.
- ✅ Instagram Insights reports **website taps** (`profile_links_taps`, broken down by button type via API). Two links means you can see which one people actually use. Check it weekly (`07-measurement.md`).

## 8. Call-to-action strategy

### The problem to solve
A skincare brand's Instagram visitor has three exits: follow, leave, or buy. Most small brands optimise for "follow" and get an audience that never purchases. You said explicitly that your goal is **relevant people → trust → sustainable sales**. So your CTA system has to be layered, not a single "buy now."

### 🥇 The three-tier CTA system

| Tier | CTA | Use it on | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Micro-commitment (lowest friction)** | *"Save this for your night routine"* / *"Send this to someone who skips moisturiser"* | Educational Reels, carousels | **Saves and sends are the strongest distribution signals** (`00-START-HERE.md`, facts #1 and #2). This tier exists to win the algorithm *and* to build a habit of your content being useful. Zero sales pressure. |
| **2. Conversation (medium friction)** | *"DM 'GLOW' and I'll tell you which one suits your skin"* / *"Comment SKIN and I'll DM you the ingredient list"* | Product education, comparison content, FAQ content | A DM is a **relationship**, not a transaction. In India, skincare purchases at ₹499 are frequently decided inside a WhatsApp conversation. This tier converts curiosity into a named lead you can follow up with. Comment-keyword DMs also generate comment volume, which is a visible social-proof signal on the post itself. |
| **3. Purchase (highest friction)** | *"Order on WhatsApp — link in profile"* / *"COD available, ships in 24 hrs"* | Product demos, offers, restock posts, Stories | Reserved for content where the viewer is already warm. Over-using this tier trains your audience to skip your posts. |

**Mix:** roughly **60% Tier 1, 30% Tier 2, 10% Tier 3.** A brand that sells in every post stops being followed. A brand that never sells stops being a business. This ratio is a starting hypothesis — measure it (`07`) and adjust.

### CTA writing rules that matter for you specifically
- **One CTA per post.** Two CTAs split the action and roughly halve both.
- **Put the CTA in the caption's last line AND on-screen in the final 2 seconds of a Reel.** Many viewers never open the caption.
- **Say the friction-killer out loud.** For an online-only skincare brand in India, the two magic phrases are **"COD available"** and **"ships across India."** They belong in your bio, in your pinned post, in your Highlights, and in the caption of any post that drives sales. Cash-on-delivery removes the single biggest reason a first-time buyer in Surat or Rajkot will not pay a stranger online.
- **Never write "link in bio" alone.** Bio text is not clickable; the Links chips are. Write *"WhatsApp link on my profile"* or pair it with the DM keyword so there is always a second path.
- **Never use engagement bait** that Instagram penalises: *"like if you agree"*, *"tag 5 friends"*, *"comment YES to win"*. Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines treat engagement-bait as a low-quality signal and it can cost you recommendation eligibility — the exact thing you need. Ask for a *specific, answerable* response instead: *"Which one do you use — cream or gel? Tell me below."*
