## Never do these

- ❌ Buy followers, join engagement pods, or run follow-trains
- ❌ Repost stock footage, other brands' Reels, or Pinterest-style images
- ❌ Use an AI-generated "brand face" or AI-generated before/after skin images
- ❌ Use engagement bait ("like if you agree", "tag 5 friends")
- ❌ Use more than 5 hashtags, or any generic ones (`#reels`, `#explore`, `#viral`)
- ❌ Make a Reel over 3 minutes expecting discovery
- ❌ Claim: fairness/whitening · cures acne · removes pigmentation · 100% natural · chemical-free · no side effects · dermatologist tested (no report) · clinically proven (no study) · guaranteed results · results in N days · India's No. 1
- ❌ Invent testimonials, reviews, certifications or clinical results
- ❌ Publish a customer's words or screenshot without explicit recorded permission
- ❌ Promise a delivery time you cannot hit
- ❌ Hide or delete legitimate criticism
- ❌ Boost content before it has proven itself organically
