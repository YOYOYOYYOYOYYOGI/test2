# 08 — Step-by-Step Implementation Plan

Twelve phases in strict order. Each step has: **what to do · exactly where in Instagram · why it matters · how to verify it worked.**

Everything in Phases 0–4 costs **₹0**. Phases 5–12 involve either time or optional spend, clearly marked.

> **How to use this:** work top to bottom. Do not skip ahead to content before the profile is finished — driving traffic to an unfinished profile is the most expensive mistake available to you. Tick each box in `10-master-checklist.md` as you go.

## The critical path (if you only do six things)

1. **Claim `glowamour.in`, switch to Business, set the Name field to `GLOWAMOUR | Night Cream India`.** *(30 min)*
2. **Paste Bio Option A and add the WhatsApp link chip with pre-filled text.** *(15 min)*
3. **Publish prices openly, and write your shipping + return policies.** *(1 hour)*
4. **Build Pin 1 (Everything Post) and the 8 Highlights.** *(4 hours)*
5. **Post 3 Reels a week with your own footage, hooks containing a keyword, ≤45 seconds, 5 hashtags.** *(3 hours/week)*
6. **Reply to every comment and DM within hours, and count your inquiries.** *(15 min/day)*

Do those six for 90 days and you will have a professional, discoverable, trustworthy, sales-focused Instagram business. Everything else in this playbook is refinement.
