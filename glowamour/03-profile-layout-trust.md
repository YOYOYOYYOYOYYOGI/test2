# 03 — Profile Layout & Customer Trust

## The 7-second test

A stranger lands on your profile from a Reel. In about 7 seconds, without scrolling, she must be able to answer six questions. If she cannot, she leaves and you have paid for that visitor with a Reel you already made.

| Question | Where the answer must live | Your answer |
|---|---|---|
| **What do they sell?** | Name field + Bio L1 | Night cream + Kesar Goti Soap |
| **Who is it for?** | Bio L1 + Highlights covers | Indian skin / people who want honest skincare |
| **Is this a real business?** | Logo quality + category label + city in bio + Highlights + pinned posts | Ahmedabad, Gujarat · real product photos · real batch numbers |
| **What does it cost?** | Pinned post #1 + Reel covers + Highlights | ₹499, published openly (`02` §4) |
| **How do I buy?** | Bio L4 + Links chips + pinned post | DM "GLOW" or tap WhatsApp · COD across India |
| **What if something goes wrong?** | Highlights (`Shipping`, `Returns`, `Contact`) + pinned post #3 | Written policy, real reply times |

**Everything in this file exists to answer those six questions before she scrolls.**

## PART 1 — The three pinned posts

Pinning puts up to **3 posts** at the top of your grid regardless of date. This is your shop window. A new visitor sees these three thumbnails and reads these three captions first, so they must do three different jobs.

### 📌 Pin 1 — "The Everything Post" (product + price + how to order)
**Format:** Carousel, 6–8 slides, 1080×1350 (4:5)
**Job:** answer every buying question in one place, so you can point all traffic here.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Clear product photo on a clean surface. Text overlay: **"GLOWAMOUR Night Cream · ₹499 · COD across India"** |
| 2 | **What it is.** Texture shot (a swipe on glass or the back of a hand). One honest sentence: what kind of cream it is, who it suits, what it feels like. |
| 3 | **Full ingredient list (INCI), in descending order.** ⭐ This is your differentiator. Do not summarise, do not paraphrase — publish the actual list from your label. |
| 4 | **What it does — and what it does not.** Two columns. "Hydrates · softens · helps skin look brighter / Does NOT treat acne, melasma, or change your skin colour." *This slide will be your most-saved and most-shared asset.* |
| 5 | **How to use it.** Cleanse → pea-sized amount → face and neck → wait 2–3 min. Add the sunscreen note if it is a day product. |
| 6 | **How to order — 4 steps.** 1) Tap WhatsApp / DM "GLOW" 2) We confirm on WhatsApp 3) Ships in 24 hrs 4) Pay the delivery person (COD) or UPI |
| 7 | **Shipping, delivery time, return policy.** Real numbers. Which courier. What happens if it arrives damaged. |
| 8 | **Who we are.** Your name, Ahmedabad, why you started this. One real photo of a real human. |

**Caption:** keyword-first, then the same information in text form (so it is searchable and indexable by Google even if she never swipes). CTA: *"Save this post — it answers everything. DM 'GLOW' to order."*

### 📌 Pin 2 — "The Proof Post" (why you can be trusted)
**Format:** Carousel or a single strong photo post
**Job:** convert a sceptic into a buyer.

Contents — **only what you actually have.** Do not fabricate any of it:
- Real photos of the actual product being made, filled, labelled, packed. Not stock. Not AI.
- The **batch number, manufacturing date and best-before** on a real pack.
- The **manufacturing licence number** — *if you have one* (open question #1). For a category full of unlabelled product, a visible licence number is close to unbeatable proof.
- Your **GST number** — *if registered*.
- **Real customer messages**, screenshotted, with the customer's name and photo blurred/removed and **their explicit permission** (`09-dm-scripts.md` has the permission request script). WhatsApp order confirmations with personal details cropped out are excellent proof.
- A **lab/stability/challenge-test report** — *if you have one*. Even one page with a lab name and date is more than most competitors can show.
- **You.** Founder name, face, one paragraph on why. This is the highest-trust asset a small brand has and the one most founders refuse to use.

⚠️ **What must NOT appear here:** AI-generated people (Instagram's Aug 2026 policy attaches reach penalties to undisclosed AI-generated profiles), AI-generated before/after images (that is fabricated evidence — an ASCI and CCPA problem, not just a platform one), invented testimonials, "dermatologist tested" without a report, star ratings you did not collect, borrowed press mentions, and any before/after that implies a medical outcome.

### 📌 Pin 3 — "The FAQ Post" (objection handling)
**Format:** Carousel, one question per slide
**Job:** answer the six questions that stop a ₹499 first order.

Take these from real DMs as they arrive. Starting set:
1. *"Is it safe for sensitive skin?"* → honest answer + patch-test instructions.
2. *"How long until I see a change?"* → your own dossier's realistic timeline is perfect content: hydration/glow in minutes-to-3-days, softer texture 2–7 days, tone appearance 6–12 weeks **with daily sunscreen**. **Publishing a realistic timeline is a trust weapon.**
3. *"Is COD available? Which cities?"*
4. *"What if it doesn't suit me?"* → return/refund policy, stated plainly.
5. *"Is it Ayurvedic / natural / chemical-free?"* → the honest answer. Your dossier §8.2 argues *"natural does not automatically mean safer"* — that argument is compelling content and it is true.
6. *"Can I use it during pregnancy?"* → your dossier has a specific §9.3. Give the cautious answer and tell them to ask their doctor. **Refusing to over-claim here builds more trust than any glowing review.**
7. *"How is this different from [big brand]?"* → ingredient percentages and price-per-gram, honestly.

## PART 2 — Story Highlights

Highlights sit permanently between your bio and your grid. They are your **website navigation**. Name them with one or two clear nouns — the label truncates at roughly **10–12 visible characters**, so long names get cut off.

⚠️ Highlights are **not indexed by search engines** (Stories and Highlights are excluded). They are for the visitor who is already on your profile — i.e. for **conversion**, not discovery.

### 🥇 The launch set of 8 (build these in week 1)

| # | Highlight | Max ~12 chars? | Exactly what goes inside |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shop** | ✅ 4 | Every product, price, size, and how to order. Start here — it is the first thing anyone taps. |
| 2 | **Night Cream** | ✅ 11 | Texture video, full INCI list, percentages, how to use, who it is *not* for, ₹499, real photos of the jar |
| 3 | **Kesar Soap** | ✅ 10 | Same structure for the soap. Add the honest answer on what "kesar" means in your formula. **Create it now even before launch** — a "coming soon" slide builds anticipation and holds the slot |
| 4 | **Ingredients** | ✅ 11 | One slide per active: what it is, what percentage, what it does, the source of your claim. ⭐ Your highest-value Highlight for the ingredient-aware segment, and your dossier already has the citations |
| 5 | **How to Use** | ✅ 10 | Step-by-step routine, morning vs night, how much to use, what to layer it with, common mistakes, patch-test instructions |
| 6 | **Reviews** | ✅ 7 | **Real** customer messages and photos, with permission. Include your response. **If you have none yet: do not fake it.** Rename this `Orders` and post packed-order photos and delivery confirmations until real feedback exists. An honest "no reviews yet, here are our first 12 orders" is far stronger than an invented quote |
| 7 | **Shipping** | ✅ 8 | Delivery time, courier partner, COD availability, which states, shipping charges (your Gujarat-vs-rest rules), tracking, what to do if a parcel is damaged or late |
| 8 | **FAQ** | ✅ 3 | The Pin 3 questions as Stories, one per slide. Update monthly from real DMs |

### Phase 2 (add at month 2–3)
| Highlight | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Our Story** (9 chars) | Founder, Ahmedabad, why the brand exists, the honest-claims philosophy. People buy from people |
| **Returns** (7) | Refund window, what is eligible, what is not, how to start one, and a real example of a return you handled well |
| **Behind Scenes** (13 — will truncate; use **Making Of**, 9) | Batch-making footage, labelling, packing, quality checks. ⭐ Doubles as your **originality proof** for the Apr 2026 algorithm rule: nobody can mistake this for reposted content |
| **Offers** (6) | Current discounts, combos, festival offers, restock alerts |
| **Contact** (7) | WhatsApp hours, response time, email, what to do if you don't reply |

### Highlight cover design
- **Consistent set.** Same background colour, same icon style, same font. This is the single most visible signal of "professional brand" versus "someone selling from home."
- **Legible at 60 px.** Simple icons or one letter. No detailed illustrations.
- **Use your brand palette** — the deep saffron-amber + charcoal direction from `01` §4 would look strong and distinctive here.
- Free to make in Canva in about 30 minutes for the whole set. Do it once, reuse forever.

## PART 3 — Grid architecture

Two 2026 changes make this newly worth your attention:
- The profile grid now previews posts at **3:4 vertical** instead of 1:1 square → design at **1080×1350 (4:5)**.
- **Grid reordering** rolled out worldwide (Jun 2026): long-press a post → "Reorder grid" → drag. Plus you can now **add tagged posts to your main grid** (Sep 2026), so real customer photos can sit alongside your own content.

### The 3×3 first impression
The top 9 tiles are what a new visitor sees. Arrange them (using reorder) so that a stranger reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom learns the business:

| Row | Tile 1 | Tile 2 | Tile 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 📌 Product hero + price | 📌 Proof / batch / licence | 📌 How to order |
| **2** | Best-performing Reel | Ingredient education | Customer photo (tagged → added to grid) |
| **3** | How-to-use carousel | Behind-the-scenes | Kesar Soap / second product |

### Cover-image discipline
- **Reels need a designed cover**, not a random mid-video frame. Put the topic in 3–5 words of large text: `NIGHT CREAM · ₹499`, `HARD WATER & YOUR SKIN`, `READ AN INCI LIST`.
- Covers are what turn a **profile visit into a follow** — a visitor scans your grid in 2 seconds and decides whether you are useful. A grid of 40 identical beige jars says nothing; a grid of legible topic covers says "this account teaches me things."
- Keep the same font and text position across all covers so the grid reads as one system.

### The mix to aim for on the grid
| Type | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Educational (how-to, ingredients, concerns) | ~40% | Reach + saves + sends + trust |
| Product (demos, texture, price, features) | ~25% | Conversion |
| Proof (customers, batches, behind-the-scenes) | ~20% | Trust |
| Brand/story (founder, Ahmedabad, values) | ~10% | Differentiation + connection |
| Offer/promo | ~5% | Revenue spikes |

A grid that is 100% product photos is a catalogue, and catalogues do not get followed or sent to friends. A grid that is 100% education gets followed but never sells. The mix above is a starting hypothesis — measure and adjust (`07`).

## PART 4 — Trust Evidence Inventory

You told me not to invent testimonials, certifications, clinical results or product claims. I have not. Instead, here is the complete list of trust assets a skincare brand needs, with **status left blank for you to fill in**. This is the gap analysis you asked for.

### Tier 1 — Required before you sell at scale
| Asset | Do you have it? | Where it goes | If missing, how to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Manufacturing licence number** (Form COS-8 own / COS-9 loan licence) | ☐ | Label, Pin 2, Ingredients Highlight | Contract/third-party manufacturer under a **loan licence (COS-6)** — your own dossier §8A calls this *"far cheaper and faster than building a compliant plant"* and the realistic path for a new brand |
| **Full INCI ingredient list, descending order** | ☐ | Pin 1 slide 3, Ingredients Highlight, every product post | You already have this capability — your batch sheet lists INCI names. Print it on the label |
| **Batch number, MFG date, best-before on every pack** | ☐ | Pin 2, Reviews/Orders Highlight | Your existing order-label tool already prints batch/order data — extend it |
| **MRP + Legal Metrology-compliant label** (name, net content, ingredients, batch, MFG, expiry, licence no., manufacturer name & full address, directions, warnings, MRP, country of origin, consumer-care contact) | ☐ | On-pack; photograph for Pin 2 | Required by law, not optional. Photographing your compliant label is free trust content |
| **Real product photography** (not stock, not AI) | ☐ | Everywhere | Phone + daylight near a window + one white/stone surface + one prop. Four hours of work gives you six months of assets |
| **Written shipping policy** (times, courier, charges, COD states) | ☐ | Shipping Highlight, Pin 1 slide 7 | Write it in 15 minutes. Your delivery-rule engine in the repo already encodes the logic |
| **Written return/refund policy** | ☐ | Returns Highlight, Pin 1 | Even a strict "no returns on opened product, full replacement if damaged in transit" is *far* better than silence. Silence reads as "this person will disappear with my money" |
| **A named human** (founder name + face) | ☐ | Our Story Highlight, Pin 2, Reels | The cheapest, highest-yield trust asset in existence, and the one most founders withhold |
| **WhatsApp Business with a catalogue** | ☐ | Links chip #1 | Free, ~1 hour to set up |
| **GST registration** | ☐ | Pin 2, invoices | Required above the threshold; also unlocks marketplace listings and gateway KYC |

### Tier 2 — Strong differentiators (get these in months 2–6)
| Asset | Value | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Stability test report** (3 months at 40 °C/75% RH + 25 °C + 4 °C + freeze-thaw + light) | Proves your shelf-life claim is real | Third-party lab. Your dossier §8D lists exactly what to commission |
| **Challenge test / PET (ISO 11930)** | Proves the preservative system works | Same lab visit |
| **Microbial limits per batch** | Release criterion + a genuine "every batch tested" story | Lab |
| **Heavy metals per BIS IS 4707** | Compliance + a powerful safety story given how many Indian creams have been found contaminated | Lab |
| **HRIPT / dermatologist-supervised patch test** | The **only** thing that lets you legitimately say "dermatologically tested" | 50–200 panelists under supervision |
| **Efficacy substantiation** (corneometer for hydration, chromameter/mexameter for melanin index, 8–12 week consumer study) | Your dossier says it plainly: this is *"the only way you may legally say 'reduces the appearance of dark spots'"* | Contract research organisation |
| **Published active percentages** | The strongest small-brand credibility signal available; what Minimalist and The Derma Co built their positioning on | Free — just state them, if they are true at expiry (HPLC assay confirms) |
| **Real customer reviews with names** | The core of social proof | Ask every delivered customer, 7 days after delivery, using the script in `09`. Expect a low single-digit response rate; that is normal |
| **Retail/marketplace listings** (Amazon, Nykaa, Flipkart) | Third-party validation | Later — margin and ops cost |

### Tier 3 — Do NOT claim these unless they are literally true
`100% natural` · `chemical-free` · `no side effects` · `dermatologist tested` (no report) · `clinically proven` (no study) · `doctor recommended` · `ayurvedic` (no AYUSH licence) · `organic` (no certification) · `cruelty-free`/`vegan` (no documentation) · `fairness`/`whitening` (never) · `cures acne` (never — makes it a drug) · `India's No. 1` (superlative, needs proof) · `guaranteed results` · `visible results in 7 days` · `permanent`

Full regulatory reasoning and safe replacements in **`11-compliance-claims.md`**.

## PART 5 — Making it easy to contact and order

Your existing order flow (WhatsApp → Google Sheets → printed shipping label, ~30–60 seconds per order) is genuinely good infrastructure. The profile just has to feed it.

### The complete order path, end to end
```
Reel / post / search result
        ↓
Profile visit  ← name field, bio, logo, pinned posts, highlights all working here
        ↓
   Two paths, both intentional:
   ├─ "DM 'GLOW'"  → your Instagram inbox → saved reply with price + WhatsApp link
   └─ WhatsApp chip → wa.me link with pre-filled message → chat opens
        ↓
WhatsApp conversation (catalogue visible, quick replies ready, FAQ chips ready)
        ↓
Order confirmed → payment (UPI link) or COD
        ↓
Your tool: order → Google Sheets row → printed shipping label → courier
        ↓
Delivery → Day 3 check-in → Day 7 review request → Day 30 replenishment nudge
```

**Where small brands leak buyers, and your fix for each:**
| Leak | Fix |
|---|---|
| "DM for price" | Publish prices everywhere (`02` §4) |
| No reply for 6 hours | Notifications ON, 10 saved replies written, FAQ chips set, business hours stated in the away message |
| Customer must explain what she wants | Pre-filled `wa.me` link text; FAQ chips; "DM 'GLOW'" keyword |
| No COD | COD available, stated in bio L2, Pin 1, Shipping Highlight, and every sales caption |
| No idea when it arrives | State the number: "ships in 24 hrs, Gujarat 2–3 days, rest of India 4–6" — and then hit it |
| No return policy | Write one, publish it, honour it once visibly |
| Post-purchase silence | Day 3 / Day 7 / Day 30 WhatsApp sequence. This is where repeat purchases come from |

### One structural recommendation
Your tool's README describes a **PWA build** that can be hosted over HTTPS and installed to a home screen. If you host that `dist/` folder on any cheap static host, you have — for near-zero cost — **a real web app on your own domain** that you can put in the Instagram Links field, point product tags at, and use for Google Business Profile verification. That would be a legitimately unusual advantage for a brand at your stage. Worth considering in month 2. `[Your call — I have not assumed you will do this]`

## PART 6 — Pre-launch profile checklist

Complete all of these **before** you drive any traffic to the profile. Sending people to an empty profile is the most common and most expensive launch mistake.

- ☐ 12+ posts live on the grid (mix per Part 3), so the profile does not look abandoned
- ☐ All 3 pinned posts published and pinned
- ☐ All 8 launch Highlights built with real content and consistent covers
- ☐ Reel covers designed with legible topic text
- ☐ Bio, Name field, logo, category, Links, Contact options all set per `01`
- ☐ Prices published openly in at least 3 places
- ☐ Shipping policy and return policy written and posted
- ☐ Founder name and face visible somewhere
- ☐ 10 saved replies + 3–4 FAQ chips configured
- ☐ Blocked-words list set; tag approval ON
- ☐ 2FA ON; backup codes saved offline
- ☐ **Account Status checked → eligible for recommendations**
- ☐ Search-engine indexing confirmed ON
- ☐ Test the whole order path yourself: tap your own WhatsApp link from the profile on a **different phone**, send "GLOW", and confirm the pre-filled text and the reply both work
- ☐ Ask 3 people who have never seen the account to look at it for 7 seconds and tell you what the business sells. If any of them hesitates, fix the bio before you launch
