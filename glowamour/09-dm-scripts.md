# 09 — DM & WhatsApp Scripts

Copy these into **Saved replies** (Instagram: Settings → Business tools and controls → Saved replies) and **Quick replies** (WhatsApp Business: Settings → Business tools → Quick replies). Give each a short shortcut like `/price`, `/cod`, `/safe`.

**Why this matters commercially:** response speed and consistency are two of the few conversion levers entirely in your control. A reply in 5 minutes versus 5 hours is a materially different close rate on a ₹499 COD order. These scripts turn a 10-minute typing job into a 10-second one.

> ⚠️ **Before you deploy:** fill in every `[BRACKET]` with your real information. Never publish a delivery time, price or policy you cannot honour — a broken promise on order #1 costs you that customer permanently and earns you a bad review you cannot remove.

---

## 1. The "GLOW" keyword reply *(your highest-volume message)*

**Shortcut:** `/glow`

```
Hi! Thanks for messaging GLOWAMOUR 🌙

Here's everything at a glance:

• Night Cream — ₹[499] · [50g]
• Kesar Goti Soap — ₹[___] · [___g]
• Combo (both) — ₹[___]

✅ COD available
✅ Ships across India from Ahmedabad
✅ Dispatch within [24 hours] on working days
✅ Delivery: Gujarat [2–3 days] · rest of India [4–6 days]

Full ingredient list is printed on every pack and in our
"Ingredients" highlight — nothing hidden.

To order, send me:
1) Full name
2) Delivery address + pincode
3) Which product, how many
4) COD or UPI

Or tap our WhatsApp link on the profile and I'll take it from there.
Any questions first — ask away, I answer everything 🙂
```

**Why it is structured this way:** it answers the four objections that stop an order (price, COD, timing, safety of ingredients) **before** the customer has to ask, and it ends with a numbered list that makes ordering feel like a small, easy task rather than a decision.

## 2. Price enquiry

```
GLOWAMOUR Night Cream is ₹[499] for [50g].

So your total delivered is ₹[___]. No other charges, ever.

```

**Note:** stating the **total delivered** price, not just the product price, removes the single most common source of cart abandonment in Indian D2C — a surprise charge at the end. Your existing delivery-rule engine already computes this by state and order value; use it.

**Shortcut:** `/upi`
```
For UPI, I'll send you a payment link — it opens in any UPI app
(GPay / PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM). Takes about 10 seconds.

Once it's paid I'll confirm here and dispatch within [24 hours],

⚠️ **Never** ask for payment via a personal UPI ID from an unknown customer without a proper link or invoice, and never send payment requests from a number that does not match your published business number. Fraud awareness is high in Gujarat and one inconsistency will cost you the sale.

## 4. "Is it safe?" / "Will it suit my skin?" *(the most important script you have)*

**Shortcut:** `/safe`

```
Honest answer — it depends on your skin, and anyone who guarantees otherwise
is selling you something.

What I can tell you:
• Full ingredient list is printed on the pack (also in our Ingredients highlight)
• [Key actives and percentages, e.g. Niacinamide 4%, Panthenol 2%]
• No steroids, no hydroquinone, no mercury — those are prescription drugs or
  prohibited in cosmetics in India, and no legitimate cream should contain them
• It's a cosmetic. It improves how your skin looks and feels. It does not treat,
  cure or prevent any medical condition

What I'd ask you to do:
Patch test first. A small amount on your inner arm, leave 24 hours.
No redness, itching or burning → you're fine to use it on your face.

Please tell me if any of these apply, because then I'd suggest you skip it
and see a dermatologist instead:
• You're pregnant or breastfeeding
• You have active eczema, psoriasis, rosacea or an open skin infection
• You've had a reaction to [key ingredients] before
• You're using prescription retinoids or acne medication

I'd rather lose a sale than have your skin react. Genuinely.
```

⭐ **This script is your competitive weapon.** It is slower to send than "yes it's safe for everyone!" and it will lose some impulse buyers. It will also produce the highest trust, the lowest return rate, and the most word-of-mouth of anything in this file — and it is the only version that keeps you clear of ASCI and CCPA exposure.

## 5. Ingredient / technical questions (Segment 2)

**Shortcut:** `/ingredients`

```
Full INCI list, in descending order — exactly as printed on the pack:

[LIST EVERY INGREDIENT. Do not summarise. Do not skip the "boring" ones.]

Actives and percentages:
• [Niacinamide — 4%] — [barrier support, helps even out how tone looks over time]
• [Panthenol — 2%] — [humectant, soothing]
• [...]

pH: [5.0–5.5]
Preservative system: [e.g. Phenoxyethanol + Ethylhexylglycerin]
Fragrance: [0.15% IFRA-certified / or: fragrance-free]

Manufactured by: [name] · Licence no: [___] · Batch: [___]

Happy to go deeper on any single ingredient — ask away.
```

**Rule:** if you cannot answer a technical question accurately, say so and go find out. This segment detects a bluff instantly, and one bluff loses them permanently. Conversely, an accurate answer to a hard question can win a customer for life — and they will tell their skincare group chat.

## 7. Returns, refunds and complaints

**Shortcut:** `/returns`

```
Our policy, plainly:

• Damaged in transit / wrong item → full replacement or full refund.
  Send a photo within [48 hours] of delivery.
• Unopened and unused → returnable within [7 days], you cover return shipping.
• Opened skincare → not returnable for hygiene reasons. This is standard
  across the industry and I'd rather tell you now than after you've opened it.
• If your skin reacts → message me. I'll advise you to stop, and [we'll discuss
  a refund / I'll help you work out what caused it].

I'm a small brand in Ahmedabad. My name is on this. If something goes wrong,
message me here and I'll fix it — you don't need to chase.
```

**Shortcut:** `/complaint`
```
I'm sorry — that's not the experience I want anyone to have with GLOWAMOUR.

Tell me what happened and I'll sort it out today. It would help to have:
• Your order number (or the phone number you ordered with)
• A photo of the product and the batch number on the pack
• What went wrong

I'll come back to you within [2 hours] with a fix. You won't have to chase me.
```

⚠️ **Never** argue with a complaint in public comments. Move it to DM immediately, resolve it, and — if the customer is happy afterwards — ask whether you may share how it was resolved. One visibly well-handled complaint is worth more than ten five-star reviews.

```

Items:
• [Night Cream] × [2] = ₹[998]

TOTAL: ₹[___] (payable on delivery)

Dispatch: [today / tomorrow] · Expected: [date range]

Reply YES and I'll ship it.

**Why the explicit YES/NO:** reported COD RTO is around 26% at lower price points, costing ₹150–300 per failed delivery. A single confirmation message before dispatch is the cheapest RTO reduction available to you, and it also catches address and pincode typos — which your order tool already validates for.

## 11. Day-7 review + permission request ⭐

**Shortcut:** `/review`

```
Hi [Name] — hope the [Night Cream] is working well for you.

Two small asks, and no pressure on either:

1) If you're happy with it, would you send me 2–3 lines about your experience?
   Even "texture is light, doesn't feel sticky in Ahmedabad heat" is genuinely
   useful to other people deciding whether to try it.

2) Can I share your message on our Instagram? I'll only use your first name
   (or no name at all — your choice), and I'll show you the exact post before
   it goes up. If you'd rather not, that's completely fine and it changes nothing.

Also — honest question, which helps me more than a review:
what almost stopped you from ordering?

Thanks for supporting a small brand from Ahmedabad 🙏
```

**Two things this script does that most review requests don't:**
- It asks for **permission explicitly, in writing, and offers anonymity**. Publishing a customer's words or screenshot without permission is a privacy problem and a trust-destroyer if discovered.
- The final question — *"what almost stopped you from ordering?"* — is **free conversion research**. The answers will rewrite your bio, your pinned posts and your Highlights better than any consultant could. Log every answer.

### Log permissions properly
Keep a simple sheet: `Customer (first name only) · Date · Message text · Permission granted? Y/N · Name OK to use? Y/N · Where published · Screenshot stored`. Never publish without a recorded yes.

## 15. Instagram blocked-words list

Settings → How others can interact with you → **Hidden Words → Manage custom words and phrases**. Comments containing these are auto-hidden, protecting the social proof a new visitor reads before buying.

```
fake, fraud, scam, cheat, fake product, copy product, duplicate,
utopia, cheap copy, [competitor brand names],
dm for details, check dm, promote, promotion, collab paid,
followers, likes for, buy followers
```

Also toggle **ON** the built-in offensive-comment filter.

**Do not** block legitimate criticism words like `expensive`, `didn't work`, `late delivery`. Those are real customer feedback and hiding them is how a brand loses the ability to improve — and how it earns a screenshot on someone else's Story.

## 16. Comment-reply templates (public — these are SEO and ranking signals)

Mosseri confirmed in July 2026 that **comments and replies are read as text signals for search**. A substantive reply is simultaneously customer service, SEO, and a Feed-ranking input. Never reply with just an emoji.

| Comment | ❌ Weak reply | ✅ Strong reply |
|---|---|---|
| "Price?" | "DM 📩" | "₹499 for 50g, and COD is available across India. Full ingredient list is in our Ingredients highlight 🙂" |
| "Will it work for oily skin?" | "Yes!" | "It's a lightweight cream, so most oily skin does fine with a pea-sized amount. In Ahmedabad humidity I'd suggest starting every other night. Patch test first — that's the honest answer." |
| "Is it chemical free?" | "100% natural ✅" | "No product is chemical-free — water is a chemical, and our preservative is what keeps it safe from mould. What we can do is list every single ingredient on the pack, which we do. Happy to walk you through any of them." |
| "Results in how many days?" | "7 days guaranteed!" | "Hydration and a fresher look — a few days. Softer texture — 2 to 3 weeks. How your tone looks — 6 to 12 weeks, and only with daily sunscreen. Anything promising faster isn't being honest with you." |
| "Do you deliver to Surat?" | "Yes" | "Yes — Surat is usually 2 to 3 days from Ahmedabad, COD available, delivery ₹[__]. We ship same or next working day." |
| Negative feedback | *(delete or argue)* | "I'm sorry — that's not the experience I want for you. I've DM'd you; I'll sort it out today. Thank you for telling me here so I can fix it properly." |

## 17. Response-time discipline

| Situation | Target |
|---|---|
| Instagram DM | **Under 30 minutes** during business hours; auto-reply otherwise |
| WhatsApp message | **Under 15 minutes** — this is your sales channel |
| Public comment | **Under 2 hours** while the post is still gaining engagement |
| Complaint | **Under 2 hours**, with a specific commitment and a follow-up |
| Post-purchase sequences | Day 0 / Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 7 / Day 30, automated as quick replies |

**Practical setup:** notifications ON for DMs and comments (`01` §10 B14), saved replies loaded, FAQ chips set, away message live, and a fixed 15-minute "inbox sweep" three times a day. That is roughly 45 minutes daily and it will outperform almost every competitor your size.
