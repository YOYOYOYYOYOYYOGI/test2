# 05 — Content Strategy for Reach and Sales

## The governing principle

Instagram ranks on **watch time, likes and sends** — and Adam Mosseri's specific guidance is that *"likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content."* Non-followers are your growth. Therefore:

> **Every piece of GLOWAMOUR content must be worth sending to a friend.**

Not "worth liking." Not "worth commenting." **Worth forwarding to a specific person.** That is the design brief for everything below. A Reel about "5 mistakes with night cream in humid weather" gets sent to a sister. A Reel of a jar rotating on a beige background does not.

Second principle, from the 30 April 2026 originality rule: **everything must be your own footage.** Accounts that primarily post content they did not create lose recommendation eligibility across Reels, photos *and* carousels, judged over a rolling 30-day window. For a skincare brand this is not a constraint — it is permission to make the content that actually sells: your product, your hands, your city, your process.

## PART 1 — Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | Job | Primary signal it wins | Primary CTA tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Honest Education** | 30% | Teach skincare with no pitch. Ingredients, routines, myth-busting, realistic timelines | **Sends + saves** → non-follower reach | Tier 1 (save/send) |
| **2. Product in Action** | 25% | Show the actual product: texture, application, feel, size, price | Watch time + profile visits | Tier 2–3 |
| **3. Radical Transparency** ⭐ | 20% | Full ingredients, percentages, batch numbers, what it *cannot* do, your testing status | **Sends + trust + saves** | Tier 2 |
| **4. Proof & People** | 10% | Real customers, real orders, founder, behind-the-scenes | Trust → conversion | Tier 3 |
| **5. Gujarat & Culture** | 10% | Ahmedabad, Gujarati language, hard water, humidity, kesar rituals, festivals, weddings | Local reach + identity + sends | Tier 1–2 |
| **6. Offers & Ordering** | 5% | Price, COD, combos, restocks, festival sets | Direct revenue | Tier 3 |

**Pillar 3 is your moat.** ASCI's FY26 data shows personal care is a top violation category and that *"visible, rapid or guaranteed results"* is the most-upheld breach; their summer review found 39 of 40 examined ads needed modification. **In that environment, a brand that publishes its limitations is not being modest — it is being unmissable.** Your own dossier's "honest expectation setting" table is a content goldmine.

**Mix discipline:** if you post 4 pieces a week, that is roughly 1 education + 1 product + 1 transparency/culture + 1 rotating. Do not let Pillar 6 exceed ~5–10%; a feed that is always selling stops being watched.

## PART 2 — Weekly rhythm (sustainable on a small budget)

| Day | Format | Pillar | Time budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Reel — educational (15–30 s) | 1 | 45 min |
| **Tue** | Stories — poll/question + a BTS clip | 4/5 | 10 min |
| **Wed** | Reel — product in action (15–30 s) | 2 | 45 min |
| **Thu** | Stories — FAQ answer, one per slide | 3 | 10 min |
| **Fri** | Carousel — 6–8 slides, deep-dive | 3 | 60 min |
| **Sat** | Reel — Gujarat/culture or customer proof | 5/4 | 45 min |
| **Sun** | Rest, or Stories only. **Weekly review** (`07`) | — | 15 min |

**Volume:** 3 Reels + 1 carousel + ~4 Story frames/day. That is ~2.5 hours a day equivalent, batchable into **one 3-hour shoot day per week**.

### The batch-shoot system (how to actually sustain this)
One session, ~3 hours, produces a week or more:

1. **Set up once.** Same spot: window daylight (morning is best in Gujarat), one clean surface (marble/stone/wood), one neutral cloth, a glass slide or the back of your hand for texture shots. Phone on a tripod or propped on books.
2. **Shoot 6–8 "bodies"** — the visual footage with no talking: texture scoops, jar opens, application on hand, water rinse, product on shelf, packing a box, labelling a batch.
3. **Record 4–6 "hooks"** — you speaking the first 3 seconds to camera, separately. Different opening lines for the same footage = different Reels.
4. **Record voiceovers** for the rest. Voiceover + b-roll is faster to shoot, easier to redo, and works for viewers on mute.
5. **Edit in the Edits app** (free). Use: bilingual Gujarati+English captions (2 Jul 2026), the Aug 2026 side-by-side Reel comparison, and First Draft (25 Aug 2026) to auto-trim clips and remove pauses.
6. **Schedule** up to 30 days ahead, up to 25 posts/day, from inside Instagram.

**Reuse rule:** shoot once, publish four ways. One 30-second texture clip becomes → a Reel body, a Story frame, a carousel slide, and a Highlight cover. One good caption becomes → a carousel, a Story text slide, and a WhatsApp broadcast.

### Formats & specs that matter
| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Length for discovery | **15–45 seconds.** Reels up to 3 min are recommended to non-followers; **over 3 minutes they are not.** Stay well under |
| Resolution | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) |
| Hook | **First 1–3 seconds.** The keyword must appear **on screen** and ideally **spoken**. Most viewers are muted and decide instantly |
| On-screen text | Always. Bilingual Gujarati + English via the Edits app |
| Captions/subtitles | Always — most Reels are watched muted, and spoken audio is transcribed and indexed |
| Cover | Designed, with 3–5 words of large topic text (`03` Part 3) |
| Audio | Original audio where you can (it is searchable, and others can reuse it carrying your name). Trending audio only when it genuinely fits — do not force it |
| End frame | 2 seconds: product + price + CTA. Many viewers never open the caption |

## PART 4 — 12 carousel ideas

Carousels are your **save** engine and your Google-indexing engine (they are indexable; Stories are not). Design at **1080×1350 (4:5)** — the grid previews at 3:4.

1. **"The complete GLOWAMOUR guide"** = your Pin 1. Product, ingredients, price, ordering, shipping, policy. 8 slides
2. **"Full ingredient list, explained"** — one slide per active: name, %, what it does, source of the claim
3. **"What a night cream CAN and CANNOT do"** — two-column honesty slide. Highest-save candidate
4. **"How to order from GLOWAMOUR in 4 steps"** — with screenshots of the actual WhatsApp flow
5. **"Shipping, delivery times & charges"** — state-wise table. Answers Segment 1 and 4's biggest question permanently
6. **"Our return policy, in plain language"**
7. **"Skincare for Gujarat weather: month by month"** — 12 slides, one per month. Evergreen, highly saveable
8. **"How to read any cosmetic label in India"** — legally required label fields explained. Public-service content that positions you as the expert
9. **"The realistic timeline"** — day 1 / week 1 / week 4 / week 8 / week 12, with the sunscreen caveat
10. **"Kesar Goti Soap: what's in it and what it's for"**
11. **"Pre-bridal skincare: an honest 8-week plan"** — with the patch-test warning
12. **"5 questions to ask before buying any skincare online"** — where you answer all 5 about yourself

**Carousel structure that works:** Slide 1 = a claim or question in big text (this is the hook and the grid thumbnail) · Slides 2–7 = one idea each, ≤25 words, large type · Final slide = CTA + where to buy.

**Problem / concern**

**Transparency / trust**
14. "This is our full ingredient list. Every single one."
15. "Here's our batch number. Here's why we print it."

18. "If you've never used a night cream, start here."

## PART 6 — Caption structure (and 8 written captions)

### The 5-part structure
```
LINE 1  → The keyword, in a real sentence. Visible before "...more". Your page title.
LINES 2–4 → The useful content. Answer the question you just raised.
LINE 5  → The honest limit / the caveat. ⭐ Your signature move.
LINE 6  → The CTA. One action.
LINE 7  → 5 hashtags.
```
Caption limit is 2,200 characters, but **nobody reads 2,200 characters**. 300–700 is the working range for educational posts; under 150 for product shots.

## PART 7 — Story strategy (daily, 10 minutes)

Stories do not bring non-follower reach. Their job is **relationship and conversion** — they are ranked on whether people watch your Stories and interact with you, and they are the surface where a follower becomes a customer.

**Daily 4-frame template:**
1. **Something real** — today's batch, a packed order, the weather in Ahmedabad, your desk. Unpolished beats produced here.
2. **Something useful** — one tip, one ingredient fact, one myth busted.
3. **Something interactive** — poll, question box, quiz, or slider. ⭐ Interactions are the whole point: they are cheap, they train the Stories ranking, and every answer is audience research.
4. **Something commercial (3–4× a week only)** — product + price + "tap to order" with the WhatsApp link sticker.

**Interactive sticker prompts that work for you:**
- "Night cream કે gel — તમે શું વાપરો છો?" *(cream or gel?)*
- "તમારા શહેરમાં પાણી hard છે?" *(is the water hard in your city?)*
- "COD કે UPI — શું પસંદ કરો છો?"
- "કઈ સ્કિન પ્રોબ્લેમ પર હું આગળ content બનાવું?" *(which problem should I cover next?)* → **this one directly generates your content calendar**
- Question box: "સ્કિનકેર વિશે કંઈ પણ પૂછો" *(ask anything about skincare)* → answer each one as its own Story frame; you now have a week of content from one sticker

**Link stickers:** use them. Stories support link stickers, and since June 2026 Instagram Insights reports **link taps** for Stories — so you can finally measure Story → WhatsApp conversion directly. Put the WhatsApp link on any commercial frame.

**Archive everything to Highlights.** A Story that answers "is COD available?" is a permanent asset once it lives in the `Shipping` Highlight.

## PART 8 — Repurposing system (how one idea becomes eight assets)

| From | To |
|---|---|
| A Reel that performs well | → Carousel of its key points · → Story series · → Highlight · → a WhatsApp broadcast · → the same hook re-shot with a different opening line (a genuinely new video, **not** a re-upload — Trial Reels and the originality rule both detect duplicates) |
| A customer question in DMs | → A Story answering it · → an FAQ Highlight slide · → a Reel · → a saved reply |
| Your formulation dossier | → 20+ educational Reels · → the Ingredients Highlight · → the "what we left out and why" series · → comment replies with real citations |
| A batch-making session | → 6–8 b-roll clips · → a BTS Reel · → a "Making Of" Highlight · → carousel stills · → proof content for Pin 2 |
| A carousel | → each slide as its own Story frame · → slide 1 as a Reel cover/hook |
| Your Google Sheet order data | → "Packing orders for [city]" Reels · → a monthly "where our orders went" map Story (real, aggregate, no personal data) |

**Repurposing rule:** repurpose the *idea*, re-shoot the *asset*. Under the April 2026 originality policy, a re-uploaded clip with a different caption or border does not count as original and can cost you recommendation eligibility at the account level.

## PART 9 — What NOT to do

| ❌ | Why |
|---|---|
| Repost stock skincare footage, Pinterest aesthetics, or other brands' Reels | **Directly risks your recommendation eligibility** under the Apr 2026 rule. Also: it is the fastest way to look like a dropshipper |
