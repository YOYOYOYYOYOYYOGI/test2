# §6 HOOK STRATEGY + HOOK LIBRARY — RANGAT NATURALS

## Why hooks are the whole game

For a zero-followers account, **100% of your reach is earned in the first 2 seconds.** The hook is not a headline — it's the *first spoken line + first on-screen text + first frame*, all saying the same thing in under 2 seconds. Rule: if the hook needs a "welcome" or "so today we will…", it's dead. Start mid-action.

**Hook anatomy (pick the style, fill the blank):**
1. **Who it's for** (segment callout): "Oily skin wale, ruko…"
2. **The tension** (problem/fear/myth): "Yeh galti aap roz karti ho…"
3. **The payoff promise** (what they'll get in 30 sec): "60 second me samjho…"

**Retention engineering (apply to every reel):**
- 0–2s: hook (spoken + text + face close)
- 2–5s: **pattern interrupt** — a cut, a prop, a "wait" ("par ruklo, yeh dekho")
- Every 5–7s: new visual or new micro-promise ("aur 2nd cheez zyada important hai")
- Last 3s: CTA (spoken + text)
- Hard cap: 30–45 sec for product/education; 60 sec max for stories.

**Compliance line for hooks:** hooks may create curiosity and name problems; they must NOT promise cures. "Tan hamesha ke liye khatam" ✗ → "Tan-look fresh dikhane ka tarika" ✓.

## HOOK SELECTION MATRIX (which style when)

| Post job | Best styles | Why |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Reach new strangers (Pillar 1/6) | Problem, Relatable, Curiosity, Story | High CTR on cold feed; emotion first |
| Build authority (Pillar 2) | Myth, Fact, Mistake, Question | Saves + search; "smart brand" signal |
| Product desire (Pillar 3) | Demo, Comparison, Curiosity, Challenge | Sensory + proof; ad-ready |
| Trust (Pillar 4/5) | Social proof, Before/after (disclosed), Story | Converts evaluators; UGC voice |
| Sales (Pillar 7) | Social proof, Problem→offer, Curiosity ("100 me X seat"), Comparison | Direct action; pair with offer |
| Retargeting ads | Before/after, Social proof, Mistake, "X seat left" | They already know you; nudge + proof |

**Rotation rule:** never use the same hook style 2 reels in a row; never reuse a hook in the same month. Keep a spreadsheet: hook used / date / CTR / DMs. Kill bottom-10 hooks, rewrite top-10 with new angles.
