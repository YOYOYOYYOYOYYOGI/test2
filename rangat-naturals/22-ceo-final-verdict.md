## PART A: THE COMPLIANCE RULES (Section 29) — pin this to the wall

### The 8 non-negotiables

1. **No fake before/after.** Only real, documented, disclosed transformations. "Before" never artificially worsened; "after" never filtered.
2. **No fake testimonials/reviews.** No purchased reviews, no "friend's cousin" quotes, no AI-generated customer quotes presented as real. Only people who actually used the product, with written consent.
3. **No guaranteed results.** Never "guaranteed glow," "100% effective," "results in 3 days." Always "4–6 hafte daily use me, results har kisi me alag hote hain."
4. **No medical/cure claims.** Never: "cures acne/pigmentation/tan," "removes tan permanently," "erases dark spots," "reverses aging," "treats skin disease." These cross into drug-claim territory (Drugs & Cosmetics Act) + ASCI violation.
5. **No "100% natural / chemical-free" claims** (and no unverified "dermatologically tested" — only say it if a test exists and is documentable).
6. **Before/after always carries:** "Day X → Day Y · daily use · **Results may vary** · real customer, consent ke saath" — every frame, every platform.
7. **We are a brand, not a manufacturer.** "We formulate, test, and have it manufactured by a certified Indian facility (GMP)." Never "we manufacture in our own factory" (if untrue).
8. **Every ad creative has a written record:** who is in it (consent file), what claims it makes (safe-language check), where it runs. If it can't be defended in a sentence, it doesn't run.

### SAFE vs UNSAFE — the claims table (use this on every caption/ad)

| UNSAFE (never) | SAFE (use) |
|----------------|------------|
| "Tan remove karega / tan hamesha ke liye khatam" | "Tan-look ko fresh dikhane me help karta hai" / "regular use me skin fresh dikhti hai" |
| "Dark spots erase/cure" | "Dark spots ka *dikhna* regular use me kam ho sakta hai — results vary" |
| "Acne cures" | "Gentle, formulated for acne-prone/sensitive skin; patch test recommended" |
| "100% natural, chemical-free" | "Simple ingredient list — label par sab likha hai" |
| "3 din me full glow" | "4–6 hafte ka honest timeline" |
| "Reverses aging" | "Age-look se related care — hydration + protection" |
| "Dermatologist recommended" (unless true+documented) | "Formulated for Indian skin" / "Certified facility me banaya" (if true) |
| "90% OFF" from a never-sold MRP | Launch price + free item (real value) |
| "SOLD OUT!!!" when stock exists | Real batch count: "X/100 seat bachi" |
| "Everyone's glowing" (implied universal) | "20 testers me se X ne kaha… — aapka experience alag ho sakta hai" |

**The one-litmus test for any line:** *"If this exact sentence appears on the packaging, can I show it to the manufacturer and a consumer-protection officer without flinching?"* If no → reword.

**Final note:** this plan is business strategy, not legal advice. Before scaling ad spend (₹2k+/day) and before Diwali, have a lawyer review the final ad copy + website claims against the Drugs & Cosmetics Rules and ASCI Code. (Budget: ₹5–15k. It is the cheapest insurance in this entire document.)

## PART B: THE CEO FINAL VERDICT (Section 30)

*If I owned RANGAT NATURALS — my money, my name on the door — here is exactly what I would do.*

### 1. First product to promote
**Vitamin C Serum 10% — the hero.** It has the strongest visual story (glow-look), the best UGC/before-after compatibility, the cleanest "4–6 weeks" honest timeline, the biggest search demand in the category, and the right price point (₹599–799). **Face Wash launches alongside as the ₹249 entry ticket** (and the free mini in the launch box), **Moisturizer joins at Day 31 as the kit-completer.** Nothing else until Day 45 (Sunscreen). Every rupee of attention in Month 1 goes to one serum.

### 2. First customer segment
**Women 20–35, Ahmedabad + Gujarat metros** (Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot), urban/semi-urban, skin concerns = dullness, tan-look, oily/monsoon skin, will pay ₹399–₹999 for honest products, buy on Instagram/WhatsApp, COD-first, family-influenced decisions. Men (face wash/sunscreen) = Month 3+ side stream, never the lead.

### 3. First platform
**Instagram (Reels) for attention + WhatsApp Business for the close.** Not equal weight — IG is the stage, WhatsApp is the till. Facebook = free crosspost + ads reach. YouTube Shorts = 10 min/week search compounding. Pinterest = zero until 10k followers.

### 4. Posting frequency
**6 Reels/week + 2 Carousels/week + 3–5 Stories daily + 3 Shorts/week.** Batch-shot on 2 days (Tue/Thu), scripted every Sunday. That's ~12–15 hrs/week of content ops total. More is waste; less is death.

### 7. Daily ad budget
**₹500/day from Day 21.** Day 15–20: optional ₹200/day on one organic-validated reel (followers only). Scale rule: +30% every 48h on winners while CPA ≤ 1.2× target. Day 35: +₹500 (LAL campaign). Day 60: +₹1,000 (scale campaign). **Total path: ₹500 → ₹1,000 → ₹2,000 → ₹3,000/day by Day 90 — every step gated on CPA/ROAS, not on ambition.** (Full math: `15-meta-ads-campaigns-budgets.md`.)

### 8. First 30-day sales strategy (the one paragraph)
Weeks 1–2: zero selling — build the 20-tester program, the founder's face, and the "GLOW" DM waitlist (300+ by Day 14). Week 3: drop the Day-28 disclosed before/after + one creator collab; announce the batch. Day 21: **Founding 100 launch** — launch price + FREE Face Wash mini + FREE shipping + COD + "your review on our page," real 100-unit constraint, triple touch (reel + Stories + WhatsApp broadcast), ads on. Days 22–30: objection killer, 60-sec routine (kit logic), unboxing, testimonial, real seat-count close. **Target: 100 orders, 600+ GLOW DMs, 15+ UGC assets, WhatsApp list 300+.** Every order: 5-touch shipping comms + Day-1/7/14 WhatsApp sequence starting the review flywheel.

### 9. First 100-customer strategy (the one paragraph)
**DMs close 60–70% of them.** GLOW keyword everywhere; 15-minute human reply SLA; the 5-step GLOW script; auto-reply with a time promise; every comment gets a 2-line real reply. **Creators close 15–25%:** 10 micro-creators (Gujarat-first), product + ₹500–1,500 + 20% affiliate, 1 collab pre-launch, 2 in launch week, consent + ad rights in writing. **Ads close 30–50% from Day 21** at ₹500/day on proof-carrying creatives. **Referral starts Day 29** ("Do ₹100, Lo ₹100") and the Day-14 review message carries the ask. The offer (Founding 100) removes every psychological blocker at once — price, trial risk, waste fear, identity. If behind at Day 30, diagnose the DM pipeline before spending a rupee more.

### 10. The biggest mistake to avoid
**Selling before you're believable.** The exact failure sequence of most new Indian D2C brands: launch 10 products on Day 1, run ads on Day 2, chase "10k followers in a month" with engagement bait, fake a "SOLD OUT," use one AI-glow before/after — and then *wonder why* nobody orders, because the profile shows a brand with no proof, no face, no reviews, and three "70% OFF" stickers. **Trust is a sequence, not a campaign.** Proof (20 testers, 4 weeks, real photos) → offer (real, once) → scale (only what CPA/ROAS allows). The second-biggest mistake is its twin: **chasing likes instead of DMs** — a 5k-follower account with 5 DMs/week is dead; a 500-follower account with 50 DMs/week is a business.
