## Cross-product rule (all 10 products)

1. **Every product launch = same 3-week mini-arc:** (Wk 1) problem/education seed → (Wk 2) product demo + how-to + waitlist → (Wk 3) launch offer + proof + close. Reuse the Founding-100 playbook, scale it.
2. **Cross-sell via WhatsApp broadcast** to existing buyers before public launch (they're the warmest audience — 10% of new product orders should come from the existing list in week 1).
3. **One hero at a time** — the secondary-6 never compete with the current hero for ad budget.
4. **Claims discipline per category:** face = safe brightening/hydration language; hair = "feel/comfort/lighter," never "growth guarantee"; body = "soft/hydrated feel," never medical; lips = "comfort/softness," never "heals."
