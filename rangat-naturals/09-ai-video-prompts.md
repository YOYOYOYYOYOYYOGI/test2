# §9 AI-GENERATED CREATOR IMAGES + UGC VIDEO PROMPTS — RANGAT NATURALS

## How this fits (and where it can't)

AI UGC is your **creative factory for testing** — fast, cheap variants to find hooks/angles that humans then shoot for scale. Rules of the road:

1. **Never pass AI video off as a real customer.** In the UGC slot, AI-generated "creator" videos must be labeled or used only as *concept/teaser* assets (e.g., "aapki kahani bhi yahan ho sakti hai" style). Real testimonials, real before/after = humans only. (Also protects you under ASCI/Meta policies.)
2. **AI video models butcher brand text.** Do NOT ask the model to render "RANGAT NATURALS" on a label or on-screen text. Generate the video WITHOUT readable label text (plain label or blurred), then add: the real label (product insert or real bottle), on-screen hook text, captions, and CTA **in CapCut/Premiere**. This is the workflow — every prompt below assumes it.
3. **Consistency lock:** define ONE virtual character sheet and reuse it across prompts (same face, hair, outfit) so a "character" appears in 5–10 videos — a recurring face builds parasocial trust, even if AI.
4. **Skin realism is the whole point:** no beauty filters, no smooth skin — the prompts enforce "natural skin texture, visible pores, no retouch."
5. **Use AI images for:** profile placeholder shots, carousel visuals, flat-lays, "in a real Indian home" backgrounds, ad statics (labeled as brand visuals).

## GENERATION WORKFLOW (per batch)

1. Lock character sheet → generate 6–10 images (pick 2 consistent "faces" if the model drifts; keep the better one as THE character).
2. Generate 2 videos per length tier (5/8/10/15s) per hook idea → pick best 1 per hook.
3. CapCut: cut to exact length, add hook text (frame 1), captions, product-label insert (real bottle overlay), RANGAT bottom-corner logo, CTA card, voiceover (real voice, recorded — never TTS for UGC slot), trending audio at 15%.
4. Export 9:16 1080x1920. Name files: `RANGAT_AI_[hook]_[len]s_v1.mp4`.
5. **Labeling:** if used in the UGC/testimonial position → use only as "concept/story-format" assets or as ad creative clearly not claiming to be a real customer. Real testimonial slots = real humans only.
6. Track in the same sheet as human creatives (hook / format / CTR / ATC) so AI vs human performance is comparable.
