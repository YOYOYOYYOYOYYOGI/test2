## PART A: BEFORE/AFTER — SAFE, REAL, CONVEYING (Section 7)

### The 3 laws (non-negotiable)

1. **Only real, documented, disclosed.** If you can't produce the original date-stamped photos on request, you don't post them.
2. **No editing that changes skin.** Same light, same angle, same crop. Allowed: identical light/brightness on both frames, crop, date stamp. Not allowed: beauty filters, whitening, spot-removal, "before" darkened to look worse.
3. **Mandatory on-screen disclosure, always, both frames:**
   > "Day 1 → Day 28 · 4 hafte daily use · **Results may vary** · Real customer, consent ke saath"

### What types of before/after you CAN use (safe list)

| Type | What it shows | Safe because |
|------|---------------|--------------|
| **4-week program grid** | Day 1 / 7 / 14 / 28, same conditions, 2–3 real faces | Time documented, no single "miracle" claim |
| **Week-by-week series** | One tester, one week per post ("Week 3 update") | Serial = believable, builds a show |
| **Texture/finish comparison** | Same hand/back-of-hand: skin feel, glow-look, absorption | No face-claim; sensory proof |
| **Tester's own words** | "Fresh lagta hai," "dull-look kam" (her phrase, not ours) | Customer's language, attributed |
| **Numbers we can defend** | "20 testers me se 17 ne kaha: continue karungi" | Survey of our own program, stated as such |
| **Routine consistency proof** | "Roz subah 6:45 — 28 din ka habit" (habit, not miracle) | Honest framing |

### What you must NEVER do (the kill list)

- ✗ Fake/staged before/after (stock photos, AI "transformations" of real faces)
- ✗ "Before" photos taken in bad light / "after" in ring light (that's a fake)
- ✗ Claims: "tan removed," "pigmentation gone," "spots erased," "acne cured," "100% results"
- ✗ Before/after of a *different person* or a different skin area
- ✗ "3 days me result" framing for anything that needs 4+ weeks
- ✗ AI-enhanced "glow" on the after frame
- ✗ Implying medical outcomes (anti-aging = "age-look," never "reverses aging")

### Transformation period: the honest timeline

- **Minimum real program: 28 days daily use.** Skin turnover ~28 days — it's also a great educational point (Hook #49).
- **Show:** Day 1, Day 7 (honest: "abhi kuch nahi, bas habit ban raha"), Day 14 (early feel: freshness), Day 28 (look: even tone-look, fresh-look). **Showing Day 7's "nothing yet" is what makes Day 28 believable** — most fake content skips it.
- **Batch the program:** 20 testers at launch, then a new cohort of 10 every month (recruit from Founding-100 buyers who agree — ₹200 off next order as incentive + their consent form).

### How to make it convincing without fakes

1. **Show the unglamorous middle** (Day 7 "kuch nahi dikha" line) — fakes can't do this.
2. **Date stamps + location** on every frame.
3. **One honest negative per 3 positives** (dry patch, irritation week, "mujhe nahi pasand" tester).
4. **Let the customer say it, not you** — her words, her face, her room.
5. **Consent form** (1 page: name, city, photos for IG/ads, "I used RANGAT for X days, results are my own experience"). Store with order.
6. **Same-frame discipline:** shoot Day-1 photos the day product is delivered, with a printed "Day 1" card held in frame (the card in-frame = instant credibility).
7. **Repeat the disclaimer without shame.** "Results may vary" on every frame, every time. Brands that over-disclose are trusted more.

### The 4-week documentation SOP (per tester)

| Day | Shoot | Ask |
|-----|-------|-----|
| 1 (delivery) | Full face, same window light, "Day 1" card in frame, back-of-hand | "Kya expect karti ho?" |
| 7 | Same setup | "Kya change feel hua? (honest, even 'nothing')" |
| 14 | Same setup | "Fresh/dull me kya farq?" |
| 28 | Same setup + short 30-sec video to camera | "Kya continue karogi? Kisko recommend karogi?" |

Photography rules: no makeup, front window light (or fixed lamp position marked with tape), phone at same height (mark on wall), 20-min gap from product use, 3 photos each time (pick the sharpest, never retouch).
