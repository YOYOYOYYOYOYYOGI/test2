## PART A: THE CREATIVE TESTING SYSTEM (Section 18)

### How many creatives to test (and why)

- **Cold prospecting (C1):** **4–8 creatives per campaign, 2–3 per ad set, 7-day test.** Fewer than 4 = no data; more than 8 at ₹500/day = each creative starves (<1,000 impressions/day each = noise).
- **Retargeting (C3):** 1–2 creatives per audience (they're small — 4 creatives would burn out a 2k-person audience in a week).
- **Always have 2x your live count in the queue** (6 live → 6 in queue). Creative fatigue in Indian D2C hits in **2–4 weeks** (CPM up, CTR down, CPA up) — the brand that keeps a fresh cut ready wins the scale game.

### The 8 creative archetypes to test (in priority order for a new brand)

| # | Archetype | Why first/late | RANGAT example |
|---|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | **UGC testimonial (story)** | FIRST — highest-converting format for unknown brands; the "4 baar delete karke order kiya" story | D26 customer story (30s) |
| 2 | **Disclosed before/after** | FIRST — the #1 ROAS driver in Indian skincare; must be real + disclosed | D17 Day-1→28 (30s) |
| 3 | **Problem → solution** | FIRST — stops the scroll on the pain | D11 "Bahar ka main, andar ka dull" |
| 4 | **Product demonstration** | 2nd week — texture/ASMR builds desire once curiosity exists | D16 "10 second, 1 drop" |
| 5 | **Founder video** | 2nd week — "who are you?" answer, trust | D20 "Kal se live, 100 boxes" |
| 6 | **Testimonial (quote + product)** | Retargeting workhorse — short, social-proof | D26 cut 15s + quote card |
| 7 | **Educational Reel** | Retargeting + search — works as a "second look" nudge | D9 how-to (30s) |
| 8 | **Offer Reel** | Launch week only (then rotates) — "Founding 100" | D21 launch |

**Test matrix (C1, first 7 days):** 4 creatives × 2 ad sets (broad + interest), all same CTA/offer, different HOOK and ARCHETYPE. That isolates what's working: hook, format, or audience.

### Reading the data: the 4-day and 7-day reviews

**Day 4 (look, don't touch unless something is broken):**
- CTR (link) by creative → the **hook quality** signal. < 0.8% = hook failed (people saw it, didn't care).
- 3-sec/15-sec hold rate (from video insights) → the **first 2 seconds** signal. 3-sec hold < 30% = thumbnail/hook frame failed.
- ATC count by creative → the **message** signal.

**Day 7 (decide):**
- Rank creatives by **ATC cost** (not views, not likes). Top 2 survive; bottom 2 paused.
- Compare ad sets: if broad CPA < interest CPA → shift budget 70/30 to broad.
- **New creative in:** queue 2 new cuts (new hook, same proven archetype) → they test in the next 7-day window.

### Scaling a winner (the safe ladder)
1. Winner found (ATC ≤ target, CPA trending down) → **+30% budget every 48 hours** while CPA stays ≤ 1.2× target.
2. CPA rises > 1.3× target for 3 days → stop scaling, add a **new cut of the same script** (new hook, same body) — often the creative, not the audience, is the limit.
3. Fatigue signature (any 2 of): CPM +30% vs week 1, CTR −25%, CPA +50%, comment sentiment flat/down → rotate to the queued cut, keep the winner's CTA/offer.
4. A creative that survives 4+ weeks = **retire it to retargeting** (it still works for warm people; cold is saturated).

### The metrics, defined for a new cosmetics brand (what each one tells you)

| Metric | What it is | Healthy range (India D2C skincare, 2025–26 data) | What it means for RANGAT |
|--------|-----------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| **CPM** | Cost per 1,000 impressions | ₹100–260 (cold, skincare) | Rising CPM = fatigue or competition (Diwali spike Nov — expect +30–50%) |
| **CTR (link)** | % of people who clicked | Cold 1.0–1.8% · Warm/retarget 2–4% | Your hook score. < 0.8% cold = new hook, not new budget |
| **CPC** | Cost per click | ₹8–20 cold | Watch only as CPM/CTR derivative — don't chase it |
| **ATC** (Add To Cart / conversation) | Cart adds or WA chats started | ATC cost ≤ ₹120 cold (site) · WA chat ≤ ₹60 | **Your leading indicator.** ATC cost 3 days before CPA tells you tomorrow's CPA |
| **CVR** | % of clicks that buy | 1.5–3.5% (site) · 25–40% of WA chats (new brand, COD) | If CTR fine but CVR low → landing page/price/trust issue, NOT creative |
| **CPA** | Cost per order | Month 1: ₹300–450 (accept) · Month 3: ≤ ₹250 · Mature: ≤ ₹200 | The number you manage daily |
| **ROAS** | Revenue ÷ ad spend | Month 1: 1.5–2x (learning) · Month 3: 2.5–3.5x · Mature: 3.5x+ | **DO NOT judge a new brand on ROAS alone** — at ₹500/day, 2 lost orders = ROAS noise. Judge ATC + CPA for 30 days, then ROAS |

**What matters MOST for a new brand (priority order):**
1. **ATC cost / WA conversation cost** (leading indicator, earliest signal)
2. **CPA** (the money number)
3. **CTR** (the hook quality number)
4. **ROAS** (the lagging verdict — only meaningful after ~₹15–20k spend or ~50 orders)
5. CPM/CPC (context only)

**The math you must know (AOV = ₹600 serum, ₹950 kit):**
- At ₹500/day = ₹15,000/month. At CPA ₹350 → ~43 orders → ₹34–41k revenue → ROAS ~2.3–2.7x. Month 1 is a **buying data** month; profit comes from Month 3 (lower CPA) + repeat purchases (which ads don't pay for).
- **LTV math:** a serum buyer who repeats once at 60 days (refill) = LTV ₹1,100–1,200 with COGS ~₹250–300/order. CAC ₹300 is only "expensive" if you ignore the repeat. **This is why the Day-14/28 WhatsApp sequences (§21) are part of the ad strategy — they're where ROAS actually becomes 4x+.**

## PART B: RETARGETING — THE SEQUENCE (Section 20)

### The audiences (build once, Day 25+)

| # | Audience | Window | Size check |
|---|----------|--------|-----------|
| R1 | Video viewers (IG/FB/YT Short views) 25–75% | 90d | expect 2k–20k by Day 30 |
| R2 | IG profile visitors + FB page engagers | 90d | expect 1k–8k |
| R3 | Website visitors (exclude purchasers, exclude ATC) | 180d | grows from Day 21 |
| R4 | Added to Cart (exclude purchasers) | 30d | |
| R5 | Initiated Checkout (exclude purchasers) | 30d | |
| R6 | Purchasers (exclude from ALL above) | 180d | → used for upsell/cross-sell + LAL seed |
| R7 (later) | LAL 1% of R6 | 30d refresh | once 50+ purchasers |

**Exclusions are the system:** every prospecting audience excludes R6 (never show "buy now" to a buyer) and R4/R5 (let retargeting own them). Every retargeting audience excludes R6.

### The correct sequence (who sees what, in what order)

```
R1  video viewers 25–75%   → "second look" proof content (B/A, testimonial)
R2  profile/engagers       → trust content (founder, "how it works", reviews)
R3  website visitors       → objection killer (COD, returns, timeline) + offer
R4  ATC                    → urgency-real ("X seat bachi") + testimonial
R5  checkout               → trust + risk reversal (COD, "aapka number save hai")
R6  purchasers             → NOT "buy again now" — Day-28 "kya chal raha hai?" + cross-sell (kit) + review ask (WhatsApp-first, ads second)
```

**Why this order:** a video viewer needs *proof* (they've never seen the product). A website visitor needs *risk reversal* (they priced it and left). An ATC user needs *urgency*. A checkout user needs *reassurance*. A buyer needs *relationship* (not another sales ad — that's the #1 retargeting mistake: napping buyers with more sales ads).

### Copy per audience (paste-ready)

- **R1 (viewers):** "Aapne 2 din pehle hamari video dekhi thi — yeh 4 hafte ke asli photos hain. Same light, same angle, filter nahi. Results har kisi me alag hote hain — isliye COD, isliye return bhi. ⬇️"
- **R2 (engagers):** "Aap humari page par aaye the. Ek sach baat: hum 20 testers se 4 hafte tak daily photos lete hain — aap bhi 4 hafte baad yahan ho sakti ho. DM 'GLOW' — 15 minute me reply."
- **R3 (site visitors):** "Price dekha? Ab risk nahi: COD, parcel khol ke dekho, return bhi hai. Aur 4–6 hafte ka honest timeline — 3-din wale wada nahi karte. ⬇️"
- **R4 (ATC):** "Aapka item cart me hai. Batch 1 me se ab X seat bachi hain — phir agla batch agle mahine. COD hai, aaj ka order kal pack."
- **R5 (checkout):** "Aap 1 step door thi. Koi bhi sawaal — WhatsApp par insaan hai, bot nahi. COD. Return. Bas."
- **R6 (purchasers — cross-sell only, after Day 21 post-purchase):** "Aapka 28 din ka hisa baaki hai — aur sunscreen agle mahine aa raha hai. Early list me rahi? (link)" · Later: "Kit upgrade: 3-step complete, ₹X off aapke liye (existing customer)."

### Budget & frequency rules
- Retargeting total = **15–25% of ad budget** (at ₹500/day → ₹100–125; at ₹2,000 → ₹400).
- Frequency cap discipline: ≤ 3–4 impressions/person/week. (Meta doesn't expose a hard cap for custom audiences in most setups — manage by **rotating 2 creatives every 10 days** and **shrinking windows** (90d → 60d) when frequency creeps up.)
- Retargeting should hit **CPA ≤ ₹150 and ROAS ≥ 3x** by Day 45. If not → the issue is usually the *creative* (sales-ads on warm people) — swap to proof/objection creative.
- **WhatsApp is the real retargeting channel:** for a COD-first Indian brand, a human WhatsApp nudge ("Kya aapne socha? COD hai") out-converts a 5th Facebook ad. Retargeting ads should *push to WhatsApp*, not just to the site.

### The full funnel order (ads ↔ organic ↔ WhatsApp together)

```
COLD  : C1 prospect (Reels/Feed)          → attention
WARM  : C2 LAL + organic Reels            → trust
WARM2 : C3 R1/R2 (proof) + Stories        → consideration
HOT   : C3 R3/R4 (risk + urgency)         → intent
CLOSE : WhatsApp chat (human, 15-min SLA) → order (COD)
POST  : Day-1/7/14/28 WhatsApp sequences  → review + repeat
REPEAT: R6 cross-sell + referral + kit    → LTV
```
