## THE 10 DECISIONS THAT DEFINE THIS ENTIRE PLAN

Read these first. Every other document in this folder is an execution of these decisions.

| # | Decision | Choice | Why (one line) |
|---|----------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | **Hero product** | **Vitamin C Serum (10%)** — launch with **Face Wash** as low-ticket entry, **Moisturizer** as the "complete the routine" item. Launch NOTHING else for 60 days. | Serum = visible "glow" results in 2–4 weeks, strong search demand, UGC-friendly, ideal price point. |

Willing to pay ₹399–₹799 for honest products. | Concentrated geographic + demographic = faster word of mouth, creator density, cheap local proof. |
| 3 | **First platform** | **Instagram (Reels = attention engine)**. WhatsApp Business = the **actual sales engine**. Not equal weight — different jobs. | IG creates desire + trust at scale; WhatsApp closes Indian orders (COD, human chat, family trust). |
| 4 | **Posting cadence** | 5–7 Reels/week + 3–5 Stories/day + 2 Carousels/week. Batch-shot on 2 days. | Consistency without burning out a 1–3 person team. |
| 5 | **Language** | 50% Hinglish, 30% Gujarati, 20% English (per post varies). | Matches how Gujarat/India actually talks on social media. |
| 6 | **Ads start** | **NO ads before launch (Day 21).** Optional ₹200/day boost on 1–2 best organic Reels from Day 15. Ads start at launch with ₹500/day prospecting. | Ads without social proof = expensive failure. Proof first, spend second. |
| 7 | **Offer** | **"Founding 100" launch offer:** launch price + FREE Face Wash mini + FREE shipping + COD + your review featured on the page. Hard limit: 100 units. | Real urgency, no fake discount games, builds the review flywheel from order #1. |
| 8 | **Proof policy** | Only REAL results: 4-week tester program, real customer photos (written permission), "results may vary" always on screen. No fake before/after, no fake reviews, ever. | One fake photo found = brand dead + ASCI/legal risk. Trust is the whole business. |
| 9 | **Funnel** | Reel → Profile → DM keyword ("GLOW") → WhatsApp → COD order → Day 1/7/14/28 WhatsApp sequence → Review → Referral (₹100 give/₹100 get) → Repeat. | Every reel is an entry point into a machine, not a vanity post. |
| 10 | **North-star metric** | **Orders + Repeat rate**, NOT followers or likes. Followers are only counted if they DM. | You run a business, not a content channel. |

## MARKET CONTEXT (why this plan is built this way)

- India's skincare market ≈ **$2.98B (2026) → $5.1B by 2032, ~9.4% CAGR** (Vyansa Intelligence, 2026). Facial care = ~85% of it.
- **Vitamin C serum** sub-segment: 10–20% concentration dominates ~60% share; category growing ~9.2% CAGR (MarkNtel Advisors, 2026).
- **Sun care** is the fastest-growing category (~12.5% CAGR) — this is RANGAT's Month 2 product, timed before Gujarat's summer and wedding season.
- Indian D2C skincare ad benchmarks (2025–26 data): CPM ₹100–260, CTR 1.2–2.5%, prospecting ROAS 1.8–3.2x, retargeting ROAS 2.8–4.5x, UGC video outperforms brand creative ~2x. Plan targets in `15-meta-ads` are derived from these.
- Structural realities we exploit: **COD is mandatory** in India for an unknown brand; **fake-product fear** is real (our trust content attacks it directly); **Gujarat is value-conscious but business-culture-driven** (respect for honest trade, family recommendations, "aamno che" pride).

## COMPLIANCE — NON-NEGOTIABLES (applies to every file)

1. **No fake before/after, fake testimonials, fake reviews — ever.** Only real customers, with written photo consent.
2. **No medical claims.** No "cures," no "100% removes tan," no "permanent," no "dermatologist recommended" unless a dermatologist actually recommends it.
3. **Safe language bank** (full table in `22-ceo-final-verdict.md`): "helps brighten the look of skin," "may reduce the appearance of dark spots over consistent use," "formulated for Indian skin," "results may vary."
4. Before/after always on-screen: **"4 weeks of daily use · Results may vary · Individual experiences differ."**
5. We are a **brand, not a manufacturer.** Language: "crafted with certified Indian manufacturers," "we formulate & test, expert Indian facilities manufacture."
6. Any "test/clinical" claim must be true and documentable. When unsure → don't say it.
7. This plan is business strategy, not legal advice. Have your final ad copy checked against the Drugs & Cosmetics Act / ASCI code once you scale.
