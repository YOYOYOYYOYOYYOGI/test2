# §1 PLATFORM STRATEGY + §2 BRAND POSITIONING — RANGAT NATURALS

## PART A: PLATFORM RANKING (do NOT manage all equally)

### The ranking

| Rank | Platform | Role in the machine | % of effort |
|------|----------|--------------------|-------------|
| **#1** | **Instagram** (Reels + Profile + Stories + DM) | **Attention + Trust + Funnel entry.** Where an unknown Indian skincare brand gets discovered, judged in 3 seconds, and converted into a DM conversation. | **60%** |
| **#2** | **WhatsApp Business** | **The sales engine.** Where Indian customers actually close: COD, family-trust channel, human support, broadcast, post-purchase relationship. Zero ad cost, highest intent. | **25%** |
| **#3** | **Facebook** | **Cheap reach + older audience (25–50, mothers, tier-2/3).** Crosspost everything; run the same Meta ads here (same ad account — no extra work). | **10%** |
| **#4** | **YouTube Shorts** | **Search longevity.** A Short titled "Vitamin C serum kaise use kare" is found by search for months. Repurpose your top 3 Reels/week — near-zero extra cost. | **5%** |
| **#5** | **Pinterest** | **Skip for now.** Pinterest's beauty traffic in India is thin vs. the effort. Revisit at 10k followers + 100+ images. (Keep it on the map for 6–12 months later.) | **0%** |

### WHY this ranking (the reasoning, so you can defend it)

1. **Instagram is the only platform where all 5 funnel jobs happen in one place** for your target (women 20–35, Gujarat): discovery (Reels feed + Explore), judgment (profile in 3 seconds), trust (highlights, pinned posts, comments), intent capture (DM/WhatsApp link), and community (Stories). No other platform does discovery + trust + DM commerce this efficiently for D2C skincare in India.
2. **WhatsApp Business is where India closes.** Your buyer is price-sensitive (unknown brand → trust gap), wants to ask questions ("COD chalega?", "4 hafte me dikhega?"), and in Gujarat the household decision often involves family. WhatsApp is where that conversation happens. Meta ads also have a native **Click-to-WhatsApp** objective — ads can deliver customers straight into your chat. This combination (Meta ads → WhatsApp → COD) is the highest-converting path for a new Indian cosmetics brand and almost none of your competitors use it well.
3. **Facebook is free reach, not a workload.** You post to a Meta Business Suite that publishes to FB + IG simultaneously. Your 25–40 audience (and their mothers/aunts — high purchase power for family gifting in Gujarat) lives there. No separate content.
4. **YouTube Shorts = compounding search.** Reels die in 48 hours; a Short with a search-optimized title ("Best sunscreen for oily skin India") keeps delivering for months. Cost = 10 min/day of uploading.
5. **Pinterest** is a strong beauty platform in the US, not yet in India for a new brand. Your time is worth more on IG+WhatsApp.

### Platform operating rules (so nothing gets "managed equally")

- **Instagram:** Original creative. Everything is made here first.
- **Facebook:** Auto-crosspost + 1 extra action/month (post to 2–3 local Ahmedabad/Facebook skincare groups where brand posting is allowed — answer questions, don't spam).
- **YouTube Shorts:** Upload top-performing Reels with search-optimized titles/descriptions (templates in `14-seo-and-hashtags.md`).
- **WhatsApp Business:** This is a **system**, not a social channel: catalog, quick replies, labels (New Lead / Order / D7 Follow-up / Buyer), broadcast lists, status updates (post 3x/day from your Stories).

## PART B: BRAND POSITIONING — How RANGAT NATURALS looks and sounds online

### 1. Brand personality (4 words)

**Honest. Desi-smart. Calm. Slightly playful.**

- **Honest:** no fake discounts, no miracle claims, shows real texture, real prices, real faces.
- **Desi-smart:** understands Indian skin, Indian weather, Indian budgets. "Totka-level samajh, science-level transparency."
- **Calm:** never shouts "BUY NOW!!!" — explains, demonstrates, invites.
- **Slightly playful:** knows Indian humor (relatable reels, light Gujarati banter) but never at the customer's expense.

### 2. Tone of voice rules (write every caption against this)

| Situation | We say | We never say |
|-----------|--------|--------------|
| Selling | "Launch me pehli 100 orders — aapki review page par feature hogi" | "LAST CHANCE!!! ORDER NOW!!! 90% OFF!!!" |
| Results | "4 hafte daily use me, dikhne me fresh aur bright skin — results har kisi me alag hote hain" | "4 hafte me tan hamesha ke liye khatam" |
| Price | "2000 wali imported cream ke hisaab, 1/3 daam — kyunki hum middleman nahi" | "Mehngi cream mat lagao, hum saste ho" |
| Objection | "Pehli baar kisi naye brand se lete ho? COD hai, parcel kholo, pasand nahi toh return." | "Sb log khareed rahe hain, aap kyon soch rahe ho" |
| Language | Hinglish by default, Gujarati when it adds warmth ("Aamee skin, aamno liye") | Pure corporate English; broken Hindi |
| Education | "Yeh ingredient yeh karta hai — aur yeh nahi karta" | "Vitamin C = magic" |

**Signature phrase system** (repeat until it owns the space):
- **English anchor:** "Skincare that understands Indian skin."
- **Gujarati flavor (use in 1 in 3 posts):** "Aamee skin, aamno liye."
- **Trust phrase (in every product post):** "Asli result, asli photo, koi filter nahi."
- **CTA phrase (the DM keyword):** **"DM karo 'GLOW'"** — this one keyword is the whole funnel (see §21).

### 3. Content style (the format rules)

- **Reels first, always vertical 9:16, first 2 seconds = hook on screen + hook spoken.** No logo intros, no "welcome to our page." The hook IS the opening frame.
- **Founder-led 40% / Product-led 30% / UGC-creator-led 30%** (see §22 ratio). The founder's face = the trust anchor of a zero-awareness brand.
- **Every Reel ends with a 3-second CTA** (spoken + on-screen): "GLOW likho DM me" / "Follow karo — kal next part" / "Save karo, baad me zaroor chahiye."
- **Captions:** 1–2 line hook + 3–5 value lines + 1 CTA + 3–5 relevant hashtags (framework in §15). First line of caption = keyword (IG search reads it).
- **Audio:** trending audio at 10–20% volume UNDER voiceover, or clean voiceover with soft lo-fi. Voice is always human (founder or creator), never pure TTS.
- **Posting windows (Gujarat/India):** Mon–Fri **7:30–9:00 PM** (peak), alternate slots 12:30–2:00 PM (lunch scroll) and 7–8 AM (morning routine content). Weekend: 10–11 AM (Sunday planning scroll).

### 4. Visual style (what the feed looks like)

- **Palette:** warm cream + terracotta/rangat-red accent + soft sage. "Indian warmth," not clinical blue, not neon.
- **Photography:** natural daylight (window light), real textures (cream swirl on hand, serum drop in palm), **real skin with pores** — no heavy retouching. A light filter max.
- **Product shots:** product always shot with a hand or in a real routine context (bathroom counter, bedroom table) — never floating on white.
- **Typography:** one bold sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat/Poppins) for hooks; one warm serif for brand moments. On-screen text = **max 8 words per frame**, big, centered, with safe margins.
- **Feed grid:** alternating rhythm — face (person) / product texture / text-hook carousel — so the grid reads as "human brand," not "catalog."
- **Logo placement:** small, bottom corner, or on the product label. Never covering the face/hook.

### 5. Educational style

- **"1 Reel = 1 idea"** rule. One myth, one ingredient, one mistake. Never a 10-point lecture.
- Format: **Hook (problem) → Why it happens (30 sec science, simple words) → What to do (2 steps) → CTA.**
- Always end education with a **soft product nod, not a pitch**: "Yehi kaam humara Vitamin C serum karta hai — kal iske baare me detail me."
- Vocabulary for Indian audiences: say "dark spots/pigmentation ka dikhna," not "ameliorate hyperpigmentation." Say "subah-saandh 2 ghante," not "consistent twice-daily application."

### 6. UGC style (what we ask creators to do)

- **Selfie-camera, front light (window), no studio.** The imperfection is the point.
- Structure: **real first line → real problem → product in routine → honest take → "link bio / DM GLOW."**
- Creator says ONE honest thing, not a script: "Maine 3 hafte lagaya, mujhe lagta hai chehra zyada fresh dikhta hai — par meri dry patch abhi bhi hoti hai." Honest negatives build trust more than 10/10 praise.
- Full 20 UGC scripts: `08-before-after-and-ugc.md`. AI-UGC generation prompts: `09-ai-video-prompts.md`.

### 7. Trust-building style (how a ZERO brand earns belief)

1. **Founder face on everything for 90 days.** A real person is the only instant proof a new brand has.
2. **Transparency reels:** "Hum manufacturer nahi hain — hum formula chunte hain, test karte hain, aur certified Indian facilities me banwa dete hain." (Shows the process, the batch card, the label check.)
3. **Anti-fake positioning:** "Fake cream dikhane ka sabse bhaari game khele jaate hain — hum sirf wohi photo dikhate hain jo asli customer ne bheji hai."
4. **Numbers out loud:** "20 aahaiyon ne 4 hafte test kiya" (not "thousands love us").
5. **COD + return on every sales post.** The risk is removed in one line.
6. **Slow proof over loud claims:** 4-week testers, not 3-day miracles.
7. **Every claim on screen has a source line or a humble line:** "Yehi wajah hai" / "Humari opinion, aapka experience alag ho sakta hai."

### 8. Sales style (when we sell, how we sell)

- **Sell in 1-in-5 posts directly** (see §22). The other 4 sell indirectly by making the profile a place you'd trust with money.
- **Offer always has 3 parts:** price anchor (honest) + extra value (free mini/sample) + risk reversal (COD/return) + real constraint (Founding 100 / stock batch).
- **Never:** fake countdowns, fake "92% OFF" from an MRP nobody ever paid, pressure on body image.
- **Always:** what's in the box, what to expect in week 1/2/4 (expectation setting = fewer returns + better reviews), and the one action: **DM "GLOW"** or **click WhatsApp link**.
- Sales post anatomy: **Hook (who it's for) → proof (real photo/review) → what you get → price + offer → risk reversal → CTA.**

### 9. The 3-second test (every asset passes or dies)

A new visitor lands on the profile. In 3 seconds they must know:
1. **Indian skincare brand** ← bio line 1 + product visuals
2. **What problem we solve** ← bio line 2 ("Glow, tan-look, dullness — Indian skin ke liye")
3. **We're real & trustworthy** ← founder photo/avatar, pinned social-proof post
4. **How to buy** ← bio line 4 ("DM 'GLOW' ya WhatsApp link")

If any of the 4 fails, fix the profile before making more content.
