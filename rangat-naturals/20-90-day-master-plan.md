# §26 THE 90-DAY SOCIAL MEDIA + SALES MASTER PLAN — RANGAT NATURALS

**Context (why 90 days is the right frame):** You're launching in mid-September 2026. Days 31–90 run straight into **Gujarat's wedding season (Oct–Feb) and Diwali (Nov 2026)** — the two highest-intent skincare windows of the year in Gujarat. This plan is built to be *sold out of proof* by Day 30 and *sold out of season* by Day 90.

**The 30/60/90 logic:**
- **Days 1–30: FOUNDATION + PROOF** → make the brand believable, sell the first 100.
- **Days 31–60: TRUST → CONVERSION** → scale what works, add Sunscreen, wedding-season content.
- **Days 61–90: SYSTEM + SCALE** → engine running, Diwali wave, Night Cream, repeat machine.
