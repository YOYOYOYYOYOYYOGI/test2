## PART A: THE DAILY STORY SYSTEM

### Why Stories are the #1 conversion surface
Reels bring strangers; **Stories convert the 10–30% who follow** (your actual buyers) because Stories are where a small account feels like a relationship. For a zero brand, Stories = your DM warm-up machine.

### The 3-slot daily formula (15 min/day, batchable)

| Slot | Time | Job | Format |
|------|------|-----|--------|
| **Morning (7:00–9:00 AM)** | "Roz ka 1 sach" | Value + habit | 1 educational tip OR routine shot (1–2 frames) + 1 question box/poll |
| **Afternoon (12:30–2:30 PM)** | "Peeche ki kahani" | Trust/behind-the-scenes | 1 BTS frame (packing, testing, founder, lab visit, a mistake) + 1 poll or quiz |
| **Evening (7:30–9:00 PM)** | "Aaj ka CTA" | Conversion | 1 product/offer/review frame + **DM CTA** ("GLOW likho") or link sticker (WhatsApp) |

**Story rules:**
- 3–5 frames max per slot. Frame 1 = hook (text or face). Frame 2–4 = value. Last frame = engagement (poll/question) OR CTA.
- **Always end the evening slot with a DM CTA or link sticker** — this is the silent order engine (many buyers DM from Stories, not Reels).
- Reply to EVERY poll/question answer within 2 hours (DM conversation = pipeline).
- Story highlights updated weekly (see Part B).
- **WhatsApp Status = same 3 slots, same day** (zero extra work; Gujarat buyers are heavy WhatsApp-Status users).

### Story asset types (use in rotation)
Polls · Question boxes · Quizzes (with answer reveal next story) · This-or-that (product A/B) · Countdown (launch/offer) · BTS (packing, lab, founder fail) · UGC repost · Review quote card · "Ask us anything" box · "Day X of 30" progress · Flashback ("1 mahine pehle hum yahan the")

## PART B: THE INSTAGRAM PROFILE (built Day 0, updated weekly)

> Why: Instagram search matches the name field first. "Indian Skincare" + brand = discoverable. Never put promo text here.

```
🇮🇳 Indian skincare — Aamee skin, aamno liye
✨ Vitamin C 10% · Face Wash · SPF — 4–6 hafte ka honest timeline
📦 COD · Free shipping (launch) · WhatsApp par support
⬇️ DM "GLOW" ya WhatsApp par order karo
```
**Keyword load in bio:** Indian skincare, Vitamin C, Face Wash, SPF, COD, WhatsApp — all real search/decision words.

### Bio link
Linktree/Stan Store with 3 max items: **① WhatsApp order (click-to-chat) ② Website (if live) ③ Waitlist/offer page.** Never 8 links — 3, ordered by conversion.

### Avatar
Founder's face (warm, real, no heavy filters) — a face converts a zero brand better than a logo. (Logo goes on the product/pinned posts; avatar = the human.)
