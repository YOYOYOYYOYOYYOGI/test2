# §27 CONTENT PRODUCTION SYSTEM — RANGAT NATURALS (batch, fast, repeatable)

## The design rules

1. **2 shoot days/week, not 5 tiny days.** Batching cuts setup time ~60% (lighting, phone rig, props, outfits all set once) and forces a script pipeline (no "what should I post today").
2. **Scripts written, never improvised** (founder lines can be natural, but beats are written: hook → 3 beats → CTA).
3. **Every shoot day produces: 8–10 reels + 4 ad cuts + 10–12 Story frames + carousel images.** One day feeds ~1.5 weeks of posting.
4. **AI UGC runs on its own 2 half-days/month** (creative testing only — see `09-ai-video-prompts.md`).
5. **A 3-week content buffer** at all times (if the buffer drops below 10 posts, shoot days become 3/week for a fortnight — no exceptions).

## VOLUMES (per week, at steady state)

| Asset | Volume | Where it's made |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| Reels (founder/product) | 6–7 | Shoot days (8–10 shot, edit to 6–7 best) |
| Reels (UGC/creator/AI) | 2–4 | Creators (their days) + AI batches |
| Carousels | 2 | Shoot day 2 (photos) + design block (90 min) |
| Stories | 21–35 (3–5/day) | 60% batched on shoot days (10–12 frames) + 15 min/day fresh |
| Product photos | 40–60 (1 session/month + 10/week on shoot days) | Shoot day 2 + monthly session |
| AI UGC videos | 6–10/month (2 batch days) | AI batch days |
| AI images | 8–12/month | AI batch days |
| Scripts written | 14–18 (for 2 weeks ahead) | Sunday planning block |
| YT Shorts uploads | 3 | Friday (from top Reels, 10 min each) |

## THE SHOOT DAY RUN SHEET (4–5 hours, same every time)

**Setup (45 min, non-negotiable):** window-light wall (or 1 key light + 1 fill), phone tripod at chest height + 1 hand-held for B-roll, lapel mic (₹1,500 — worth it), prop table (products, jar of water, towel, chai glass, wooden board), 3 outfit options (same 3, rotate — visual consistency), whiteboard for "numbers" shots.

| Block | Time | Output |
|-------|------|--------|
| 1. Founder-to-camera reels (hook → beat → CTA), 2–3 takes each, straight | 100 min | 5–6 reels |
| 2. Product demos (texture, ASMR, how-to hands) — macro close-ups | 60 min | 3–4 reels + 4 ad cuts (15s/30s) |
| 3. B-roll bank: 10 cuts (pouring, drops, hands, face, counter, packaging) | 20 min | editing fuel for 2 weeks |
| 4. Carousel photo set: 12–16 product/flat-lay/context shots | 30 min | 2 carousels' worth |
| 5. Story batch: 10–12 frames (BTS, behind-the-scene, polls as text cards, quiz cards) | 25 min | ~1.5 weeks of Stories |
| 6. Founder "talking head" spare: 5 unscripted 30-sec answers (next week's Q&A) | 15 min | Q&A feed |
| Buffer / re-shoot weak hooks | 20 min | — |

**The hook re-shoot rule:** after editing (next day), if a reel's first 2 seconds feel slow → re-shoot the hook from the B-roll bank + new talking-head line. **The hook is never "good enough, we'll live with it"** — it's 90% of the reel's life.

## THE EDIT BLOCK (60–90 min/day, same day or next)

1. Cut to length (30–45s cap) · 2. Hook text on frame 1 (8 words max) · 3. Captions throughout (95% watch muted) · 4. On-screen beats · 5. CTA card 3s · 6. Product label insert (real) · 7. RANGAT bottom-corner logo (small) · 8. Audio: VO clean + trending track at 15% · 9. Export 1080×1920.
10. **Ad cuts (4/week):** from the week's best 2 reels → 15s + 30s versions (hook-first, CTA-end) → named `RANGAT_AD_[hook]_[len]` → into the ad queue.

## SUNDAY PLANNING BLOCK (2 hrs, the brain of the system)

1. **Data in (30 min):** last week's board (from `20-90-day`): best 3 reels (why?), worst 3 (why?), top 5 DM objections, ad CPA by creative.
2. **Next 14 days scripted (60 min):** 14 posts: type, pillar, hook (from library, marked with style — no 2 same styles back-to-back), script beats, CTA, hashtags set (12 ready sets), story plan. **Rule: 30% of next week's topics come from DM objections + comment questions** (content follows the market, not the mood).
3. **Queue loaded (30 min):** 7 posts scheduled (Meta Business Suite), 7 staged (assets ready, publish Mon–Sun), buffer updated.

## AI UGC BATCH DAY (2 half-days/month, 3 hrs each — see `09` for prompts)

1. Pick 4–5 hook concepts from the week's top organic hooks (test AI variants of proven angles).
2. Generate: 8–10 videos (2 per concept, best kept) + 8–12 images (carousel/ads).
3. CapCut pass (2 hrs): text, captions, product insert, VO (real voice), CTA.
4. **Use:** ad A/B testing (C1/C4), teaser/concept posts, carousel visuals. **Never** as "customer testimonial" (that slot = humans only).
5. Log in creative sheet like human assets — AI vs human CTR is a real learning (expect humans to win on face-trust; AI wins on volume/iteration speed).

## MONTHLY PRODUCT PHOTO SESSION (half-day, 3–4 hrs, first week of month)

- 60–80 photos: hero product (5 angles) · texture macro (palm, hand, drop) · in-context (bathroom, table, hand-held) · flat-lays (cream palette, terracotta accents) · box + card · "with the routine" (3 products in row).
- Output feeds: next month's carousels, ad statics, website, WhatsApp catalog, gift-box content.
- Lighting: window + 1 softbox (or single softbox), cream/terracotta props constant (visual brand consistency).

## THE WEEKLY CALENDAR (steady state)

| Day | Content ops | Notes |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| Mon | Planning check (30 min) · publish 2 (reel + carousel) | |
| Tue | **SHOOT DAY #1** (4.5 hr) | 8–10 reels, 4 ad cuts |
| Wed | Edit block (75 min) · publish 2 | Ad review 60 min (last Sun's data) |
| Thu | **SHOOT DAY #2** (3 hr: lighter) | Photos, Story batch |
| Fri | Edit + **YT Shorts ×3** (30 min) · publish 2 | Hashtag rotation applied |
| Sat | UGC/creator post (1) · publish 1–2 | Creator deliveries due |
| Sun | **Planning block (2 hr)** + KPI review (45 min) · publish 1–2 (Q&A/community) | Next 14 days scripted |
| Daily | 3–5 Stories (15 min: 11 AM + 1 PM + 8 PM slots) · DM SLA (ongoing, 15-min) · reply all comments (within 2 hr) | The human layer — never batch the replies |

**Buffer rule:** at all times, queue holds ≥ 7 posts + buffer holds ≥ 10. Miss a day → pull from buffer, log the reason, protect the week's rhythm. The system's real output is **consistency under chaos** — a launch week with 80 orders and 200 DMs must not stop the reels.

## MINIMUM TEAM / TOOLING (the cheap-but-right stack)

- **People:** founder (content face + strategy) + 1 part-time help (DMs 4–6 hr/day, shoot help, WhatsApp sequences) + 1 editor (₹8k–15k/month, 2 days/week — or self-edit via the run sheet for month 1).
- **Gear:** phone (any modern), tripod ₹2k, lapel mic ₹1.5k, 1 softbox ₹2–3k, ring light optional. Total < ₹10k.
- **Tools:** CapCut (free) · Meta Business Suite (scheduling) · Canva (carousels/cards, brand kit: cream + terracotta, 2 fonts) · Google Sheet (the single source of truth: hooks log, DM log, orders, creatives, UGC consents) · WhatsApp Business.
- **The one sheet to run the company** (tabs): HOOKS (hook/style/post/CTR/DMs) · CALENDAR (14-day) · ORDERS (order# / city / source: organic-DM / ad / creator / referral / COD status) · CREATIVE (asset / type / live dates / CPA / status) · UGC (creator / consent file / assets / ad permission) · DM-OBJECTIONS (date / question / answer / "became content?").
