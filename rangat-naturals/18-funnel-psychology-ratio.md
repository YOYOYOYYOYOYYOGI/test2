## PART A: THE CONTENT → SALES SYSTEM (Section 21)

The machine. Every asset you make belongs to exactly one stage. If a post doesn't know its stage, it doesn't ship.

```
REEL  →  PROFILE  →  TRUST  →  PRODUCT  →  WEBSITE/WHATSAPP  →  PURCHASE  →  REVIEW  →  REPEAT  →  REFERRAL
```

### STAGE 1 — REEL (Attention): "Stop scrolling"
- **Content that belongs here:** Problem hooks, myth-busts, relatable/Gujarati humor, texture ASMR, creator drops, founder stories. 1-in-5 is a direct sales reel.
- **Job done when:** 3-sec hold ≥ 30%, saves/comments, and — the real metric — **profile visits** from the reel.
- **Rules:** hook in 2 sec; one idea per reel; last 3 sec = CTA (follow / save / DM GLOW). The CTA decides what stage-2 sees.
- **Metric:** reach, 3-sec hold, saves, profile visits per reel.

### STAGE 2 — PROFILE (Judgment): "Who are you? 3 seconds."
- **Content that belongs here:** bio (category + products + COD + one action), name field with keywords, founder avatar, 3 pinned posts (buy / proof / who), 6 highlights (RESULTS first).
- **Job done when:** a stranger who came from a reel can say in 10 seconds: "Indian skincare, VC serum, COD, DM GLOW."
- **Rules:** 3-second test (§2) audited weekly. Pinned = always current offer + newest proof + founder.
- **Metric:** profile→bio-link clicks; profile→DM rate.

### STAGE 3 — TRUST (Conviction): "Is this real? Will it work on ME?"
- **Content that belongs here:** Day-1→28 disclosed program, "4 darr 4 jawaab" objection reel, founder story #2 (factory, batch card), tester honesty reels (with negatives), anti-fake positioning, real review clips, unboxings.
- **Job done when:** the DM starts with "kaise order karna hai" instead of "yeh sachcha hai kya."
- **Rules:** one real negative per 3 positives; consent forms on file; "results may vary" always on disclosed B/A.
- **Metric:** share of DM conversations that are purchase-intent vs trust-questions (target: trust-questions drop from ~60% week 1 to <30% by week 6).

### STAGE 4 — PRODUCT (Desire + clarity): "What exactly am I getting?"
- **Content that belongs here:** how-to (5-step VC), texture demos, "box me kya hai," kit routines (60-sec), price-value breakdown, seasonal relevance (monsoon/wedding/winter).
- **Job done when:** the DM question becomes "COD chalega? / kitne din me aayega?"
- **Rules:** every product post states: what it does (safe language) · what it won't do · who it's for · price · COD/return · one CTA.
- **Metric:** DMs that mention the product name; kit share of orders.

### STAGE 5 — WEBSITE / WHATSAPP (The close): human, fast, COD
- **Content that belongs here:** the **GLOW DM script** (§25), WhatsApp Business: catalog with photos + price + "COD available" in every description; quick replies (price, delivery time, returns, results timeline); labels (NEW / ORDER / D7 / D14 / BUYER); broadcast lists; **status = your Stories**; 15-minute reply SLA (7 AM–11 PM); auto-reply outside hours.
- **Job done when:** a warm DM → order within 1 conversation (target ≥ 40% of warm DMs convert; COD pre-confirmation call for first-time buyers).
- **The DM script (GLOW):**
  1. Auto: "Namaste! 🙏 GLOW = Vitamin C Serum 10%, launch price ₹X, FREE Face Wash mini, FREE shipping, COD. Kya aap bhi join karna chahti ho? (city batao — delivery time batate hain)"
  2. They reply city → "Ahmedabad 2–3 din me aayega. COD me paise parcel ke baad. Koi sawaal?"
  3. Objections → price: "Launch me ₹X + free mini — phir regular. Aur COD hai, pehle dekho phir paise." / doubt: "4 hafte ka 20-tester program hai — photos 'RESULTS' highlight me. COD hai isliye risk aapka nahi." / timeline: "Honest: 4–6 hafte daily use. 3-din wada karne wale se door rahiye."
  4. Close: "Theek hai, aapka number save kar leti hoon. [name, city, product] — sahi hai? Parcel nikalte hi WhatsApp pe number milta hai."
  5. Post-order: instant confirmation + "Din 7 pe hum khud message kar rahe hain — use ka tarah bhejenge."
- **Metric:** DM→order % (target 35–45% warm), reply time (<15 min), COD failure rate (<15%).

### STAGE 6 — PURCHASE (Fulfillment as marketing)
- **Content that belongs here:** the **box itself**: thank-you card (handwritten feel), usage guide (day 1/7/14/28 expectations — sets honest timeline = fewer returns + better reviews), a "review card" (QR/WhatsApp: "photo bhejo, ₹100 off next order"), referral card ("do ₹100 lo ₹100" + their code), tissue/finish = unboxing-worthiness.
- **Shipping comms:** order confirmed → packed (photo of THEIR box!) → shipped (tracking) → out for delivery → delivered. 5 WhatsApp touches pre-delivery. (The "photo of your actual box" is a 10-second trust rocket — do it for every order in month 1.)
- **Metric:** COD success (≥85% month 1 → 92%+ by month 3), delivery time, returns (<5%).

### STAGE 7 — REVIEW (The flywheel ignition)
- **The WhatsApp sequence (the whole repeat/review engine):**
  - **Day 1 (delivery):** "Aaya? Din 1 ka tarika: [2 lines]. Koi bhi cheez — main yahan hoon."
  - **Day 7:** "1 hafte ho gaya. Aaj kya feel? (honest, 1 line me)" + the "day-7 photo" ask: "Same window light me 1 photo bhejo? Aapke jaise ka program me dikhate hain (permission ke saath)."
  - **Day 14:** "Aadha tarika. Fresh-look aaya hai? 1 line review bhejo — ₹100 off next order. (Photo optional, sirf word bhi chalega)"
  - **Day 28:** "4 hafte! Day-28 photo + 2 line review? (Consent: page/ads par dikhana?) — yehi hamari sabse badi baat hai: asli photos."
  - **Day 45:** "Kya chal raha hai? Kuch add karna ho? (SPF wave: 'sunscreen agle mahine — existing customer list pehli priority')"
  - **Day 60 (refill window for 30-day serum):** "Aapki bottle ~80% ho gayi hogi. Refill/next: ₹X off, COD. 1 message me order."
- **Metric:** review-request → review rate (target 25–35% by Day 14), photo-review rate (10–15%), consent rate.

### STAGE 8 — REPEAT (The profit line)
- **Content that belongs here:** the Day-45/60 broadcasts above; seasonal cross-sell (Oct: "wedding glow kit existing customers ko pehle"); refill reminders tied to actual bottle size (30-day = Day 28–35 nudge); "kit upgrade" (serum buyer → 3-step kit); loyalty: every 3rd order = free mini of next product (not discount — discount trains price-chasing).
- **Rules:** first repeat must be a *cross-sell* (they already trust you — sell the routine, not the same product cheaper); never open a conversation with "sale."
- **Metric:** repeat rate by Day 60 (target 10–15% of cohort), 30-day kit share, AOV trend (target: ₹600 → ₹900 by Day 60).

### STAGE 9 — REFERRAL (The cheapest growth)
- **The system:** every buyer gets a code in the box + WhatsApp (Day 2). "Do ₹100, lo ₹100" — friend gets ₹100 off first order, referrer gets ₹100 off next (both real, both tracked in WhatsApp labels/Sheets).
- **The ask moments (when people actually share):** (a) Day 14 after a good review ("aapko pasand aaya — 1 dost ko batao, dono ko ₹100"); (b) at unboxing (shareable moment); (c) at review-feature ("aapki review page par hai — share karo, code bhi").
- **Measurement:** every referred order tagged in the sheet (code) → referral % of orders (target: 10% by Day 45, 20% by Day 90).
- **Metric:** referral orders / month, CAC of referred orders (target ~₹100 vs ₹300+ ad CAC).

### The one-page map (print this)
```
REEL (hook)          → attention          KPI: 3-sec hold, profile visits
PROFILE (3-sec test) → judgment           KPI: bio→link, profile→DM
TRUST (proof)        → conviction         KPI: trust-questions ↓ in DMs
PRODUCT (clarity)    → desire             KPI: product-name DMs, kit share
WHATSAPP (GLOW)      → the close          KPI: DM→order 40%+, COD 85%+
PURCHASE (box+comms) → wow                KPI: returns <5%, COD fail <15%

REFERRAL (₹100/₹100)→ cheap growth       KPI: 10–20% orders referred
```

## PART B: VIRAL CONTENT vs SALES CONTENT (Section 22)

### The difference (one line each)
- **Viral/awareness content** = made for *strangers* to stop, feel, and share. Optimized for reach. It should feel like it's about *them*, not about you. (Mistake reels, maa skits, monsoon problem, "girl math.")
- **Sales content** = made for *evaluators* to buy. Optimized for conversion. It's about the product, the proof, and the one action. (Founding 100, objection killer, offer carousel, testimonial ads.)

**The mistake that kills new brands:** all-viral (big views, zero orders — "par paisa kahan?") or all-sales (nobody follows, nobody trusts — "brand hai kya?"). The ratio below is the balance.

### The weekly ratio (5–7 Reels + 2 Carousels = ~9–10 posts)

| Bucket | Share | Weekly count | What counts in it |
|--------|-------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Awareness** (stranger-stoppers, humor, problems) | 20% | 2 | Mistake reels, relatable/Gujarati, problem reels, maa skit, "girl math" |
| **Education** (saves, authority) | 25% | 2 | Myth vs reality, how-to, ingredient science, SPF/layering education |
| **Trust** (proof, people) | 30% | 2–3 | Disclosed B/A, tester stories, unboxings, founder story, creator collabs |
| **Sales** (direct) | 20% | 2 | Offer posts, kit/bundle, X-seats-left, referral, launch/close |
| (Overlap note: a founder story with an offer CTA counts as Trust+Sales — assign ONE primary bucket) | | | |

### How much should directly sell?

- **1 in 5 posts sells directly — and only after Day 15** (when proof exists). Weeks 1–2: 0–1 direct. Week 3: 1. Week 4+: 2.
- **The other 4 in 5 sell indirectly:** every awareness reel ends with a soft CTA into the funnel (follow / save / DM GLOW), every education reel ends with a product nod, every trust post reminds of the offer in one line. So the *effective* selling pressure is ~100% — but the *explicit* sell is 1 in 5.
- **Seasonal override:** launch weeks, Diwali week, and "X seat left" weeks can run 3-in-9 direct (max 33%) — never above.
- **Ads change the math:** organic stays 1-in-5; paid media carries the heavier sell (that's what ads are for).

## PART C: INDIAN BUYER PSYCHOLOGY — why a stranger buys an unknown skincare brand (Section 23)

The unknown-brand gap in India is not a price problem. It's a **trust gap shaped by 10 forces.** Here's each force + the exact RANGAT play:

1. **Fakery fear (the #1 silent blocker).** India's fake-product history makes "naya brand" = "shayad fake." → *Play:* batch card on every bottle + "verify on website" + the "anti-fake" content line + founder face + COD. We market *genuineness* as a feature, not an afterthought.
2. **Trust = face + consistency.** An Indian buyer trusts a human face > a logo, and consistency > one big post. → *Play:* founder face on 40% of content for 90 days; daily Stories; 15-min DM SLA.
3. **Price = respect, not cheapness.** "Sasta" triggers the fake association; "aam daam + zyada value" triggers the smart-buyer identity (very Gujarati: value psychology without the discount circus). → *Play:* honest price breakdown reel (D14); never fake %; free *items* (mini) instead of fake % (a free face-wash mini is tangible, "90% OFF" is not).
4. **Results = visible, slow, and honest.** Buyers know "3-din glow" is fake. The credible brand is the one that says 4–6 weeks and shows Day-7 "kuch nahi" honestly. → *Play:* 28-day program with the honest middle; "honest timeline" as a brand line.
5. **Ingredients = the new "brand."** 25–40 buyers now read labels; "10% Vitamin C, simple list, batch card" is a differentiator against both totka and big-brand opacity. → *Play:* label close-ups in every product reel; "label padho saath me" content (UGC #6).
6. **Reviews = other people's courage.** A buyer needs proof that *someone like them* already took the risk. → *Play:* the Day-14 review sequence, testimonial stories ("4 baar delete karke"), review-on-page for Founding 100.
7. **Fear of waste (not fear of the product).** The real fear: "₹600 kachra ho jayega." → *Play:* COD (risk moves to us), return policy stated in 1 line, 4–6 week expectation (so "no change in 3 days" ≠ "waste"), Day-1 usage guide (so the product works *properly* — half of "no result" is wrong usage).
8. **Curiosity = the local edge.** "Gujarat se brand" is novel in a feed full of Mumbai/Delhi brands; novelty stops the scroll in Gujarat. → *Play:* founder origin story, city callouts, "Aamee skin, aamno liye."
9. **Convenience = the COD+WhatsApp stack.** The Indian first order is a phone conversation, not a checkout flow. → *Play:* Click-to-WhatsApp ads, 15-min human reply, COD, "photo of your box," tracking updates. Every friction point removed in writing.
10. **Local identity + family.** Gujarat is family-purchase: the daughter orders, the mother approves, the sister gets a "gift." → *Play:* gifting content (Diwali box), "maa ke liye" Q&A, family-pack offers; language warmth (Gujarati in relationships, Hinglish in facts).
11. **(Bonus) Value = the math that justifies.** "600 = 20 chai, 4 hafte chalta hai" — give buyers the arithmetic to defend the purchase to themselves/family. → *Play:* "girl math" reel (UGC #17), per-use cost on sales posts (₹20/day).

**The psychological order of a first purchase (match your funnel to it):**
```
Stop (curiosity/hook) → Relate ("yeh meri baat hai") → Believe (face + proof + anti-fake)
→ Understand (what/who-for/price) → De-risk (COD + return + timeline) → Convenience (1 DM, 15 min)
→ Buy → (box wow) → Review → Repeat → Refer
```
Every stage in the funnel (§21) maps to one line of this chain. If orders stall, find *which* line is broken — that's where the next 30 days of content goes.
