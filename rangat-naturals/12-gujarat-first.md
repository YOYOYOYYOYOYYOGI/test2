# §11 GUJARAT-FIRST STRATEGY — RANGAT NATURALS

**Thesis:** Gujarat is not a "starting limitation" — it's a **strategic wedge**. A brand that owns one state (word of mouth, creators, local events, local weather content) earns trust faster than a brand trying to be "pan-India" from day one. Then India opens *because of* the Gujarat proof, not despite it.

**Positioning line:** "Gujarat ma pehle, India ma baad me — aur dono jagah aamee skin, aamno liye."
(Use "Gujarat first" as a proud claim in content — "Hum Gujarat se shuru kar rahe hain, kyunki yahan ki skin, yahan ka mausam, yahan ki samajh.")

## 1. Language strategy (per-post matrix)

| Format | Gujarati | Hinglish | English | When |
|--------|----------|----------|---------|------|
| Founder reels | 40% | 50% | 10% | Founder speaks in warm Gujarati-Hindi mix — authentic, not translated |
| Problem/relatable reels | 60% | 40% | 0% | Max shareability — local humor |
| Product/education reels | 20% | 70% | 10% | Clear Hinglish; key terms on-screen in English (search) |
| UGC creators | creator's native mix | | | Gujarati creators → Gujarati-first; Hindi creators → Hinglish |
| Sales/offer posts | 30% | 60% | 10% | Offer clarity in Hinglish, warmth in Gujarati |
| Captions/keywords | — | 70% | 30% | Captions carry the SEO keywords (English/Hindi search terms) |

**The 3 signature lines (rotate):**
- "Aamee skin, aamno liye." (Gujarati)
- "Asli skin, asli result." (Hinglish)
- "Skincare that understands Indian skin." (English — for the India-next phase)

**Gujarati flavor rules (natural, not forced):**
- Use Gujarati for *warmth* (relationships, home, chai, nani, maa) and Hinglish for *product facts*.
- Never make English the default voice — it signals "imported brand" to the first market.
- Local references that land: chai + thepla mornings, 40-degree summers, monsoon humidity, SG Highway, "dhool," shaadi season, Diwali gifting, college life in Amdavad, "vasadi" chats, ladi-vasem-style gifting boxes (use "gift box" framing), value psychology ("dhamaka daam" energy without cheapening the brand).
- **Do NOT** use Gujarati as a punchline or stereotype; the founders/creators use it because it's their language.

## 2. City-by-city content & distribution

### Ahmedabad (Home base — 60% of launch energy)
- **Content angles:** SG Highway dhool + office AC combo; old city vs new city skin stories; 40° summers + AC winters; Maninagar/Satellite/SG Highway geotags on posts; "Amdavad wale" callouts; local college life (MS/SD, NIZRD area).
- **On-ground:** Founder physically in Ahmedabad — film in real locations (local café, market, park) for BTS reels. 3–4 local events/micro-launches (see §creators).
- **Ads:** Launch geo = Ahmedabad + 40km. Retargeting same.
- **Local hashtags (rotate 2–3 per post):** #Ahmedabad #Amdavad #AhmedabadFood (adjacent reach) #Gujarat

### Surat (Wave 2 — young, fast, aspirational)
- **Angles:** "Diamond city hustle" (9-to-9 + glow); Surat is young (big 18–28 population) → student budget content lands hard; diamond-polishing dust/pollution angle (careful, respectful framing: "sheher ka dhool"); fast-city life, less time → "2-minute routine" content.
- **Creators:** 2–3 Surat micro-creators (fashion/food/lifestyle, 10k–50k).
- **Ads:** Add Surat to prospecting in Week 4–6 (after Ahmedabad CPA is known).

### Vadodara (Wave 2 — culture, colleges)
- **Angles:** Cultural festival + wedding-glow season; college hubs; "Vadu" humor (light); art/culture lifestyle = aesthetic content plays well.
- **Creators:** 1–2 college/culture creators.
- **Ads:** Add in Week 6–8.

### Rajkot (Wave 3 — value + family)
- **Angles:** Dry winters (face cream wave), value-for-money psychology, family purchasing (gifts for maa/nani), "Rajkot walo" direct callouts.
- **Creators:** 1–2 family/lifestyle creators.
- **Ads:** Week 8+, with Face Cream + gifting content.

**Expansion rule (India-next, Day 90+):** Keep 40–50% of content Gujarat-anchored (it's the identity), shift 30% to "all-India" problems (monsoon, office, college, wedding season), 20% to new-city creator content (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Indore). **Never drop Gujarat — it stays the brand's home, like the "Pune" of Indian D2C.**

## 4. Local creator strategy (Gujarat)

### Who to find (5k–50k followers = the sweet spot for a new brand)
- **Categories (in order of fit):** food/lifestyle Gujarati creators → college/influencer (Ahmedabad/Surat) → beauty/skincare local → fashion (wedding season) → male lifestyle (for Face Wash wave).
- **How to find (2 hours, weekly):** Instagram search "#Amdavad #Ahmedabad #Gujaratfood #Gujaraticreator #Amdavadi" + explore-page following from 5 known local accounts + YouTube search "Ahmedabad vlog" + local wedding vendors' tagged photos.
- **Vetting (15 min each):** last 20 posts (engagement = likes+comments vs followers; target >4%), comment quality (real people commenting in Gujarati = real local audience), no brand-damage flags, audience location check (commenters' handles/language).

### The outreach template (DM/email — personalize the first line with their actual post)

> "Hi [Name]! [Specific post reference — e.g., 'aapka aajak ka reel dekha, Amdavad ka vibe perfect tha']. Main [Name], RANGAT NATURALS ki founder — Gujarat ka ek naya skincare brand. Humne [city] me 20 testers ke saath 4-hafte ka program chala hai. Aapko hamara Vitamin C Serum bhejna chahte hain — bilkul free, koi script nahi. Aapki honest baat (achhi bhi, kami bhi) — aur agar pasand aaye toh ek chhota sa compensation + affiliate code. Kya aap try karne ke liye sochenge? — [Name], RANGAT NATURALS"

### Terms (standard, in writing)
- Product free (₹0 cost to creator) + flat fee by tier: 5–10k → ₹500; 10–25k → ₹1,000; 25–50k → ₹1,500–2,000.
- Deliverable: 1 Reel (15–45s, UGC structure from `08-before-after-and-ugc.md`) + permission to use as Meta ad (₹100 per ad-use, stated).
- **4 hard rules in the brief:** no medical claims, no "cure/remove/permanent," "results may vary" on any B/A, no fake urgency/discounts.
- Consent message template (save with the video): *"Main RANGAT ke liye [product] use kar raha/hoon [dates], apni asli experience ke saath. Video ko IG + Meta ads me use kar sakte ho. — [Name, city, date]"*
- **Cadence:** Week 3: 1 collab (UGC #19). Week 4–8: 5 more (1/week). Day 90: 10–15 total. Each creator = 1 post + 1 ad cut + 3 quote cards.

### Local on-ground (Ahmedabad, low-budget trust builders)
1. **Micro "launch day" meetup (Day 21):** 20 tester + creator + local press/blog — 1.5 hrs, chai, product demo, 10 min founder talk. Cost: venue (a local café, often free for 20 people) + chai + boxes. Output: 30+ UGC photos, 5+ reels, 2 blog mentions.
2. **College "skin 101" 20-min sessions (2/month, months 2–3):** not a sale — a free 20-min talk (routine basics) at 2 colleges; RANGAT as the "local brand that made this." Permission from student councils; leave 10 sample minis (cost-controlled).
3. **Local beauty page collabs:** 2–3 Ahmedabad lifestyle/beauty Instagram pages — pay ₹1,000–3,000 for 1 post + 3 stories (cheaper and more local than any "national" micro-influencer).

## 5. "Gujarat first" without capping the brand

1. **Bio & pinned:** say "Gujarat se shuru, India ke liye bana" — the origin is a badge, not a limit.
2. **Content ratio:** Month 1: 70% Gujarat / 30% all-India. Month 2: 50/50. Month 3: 40/60 (Gujarat stays the home base forever).
3. **Creator map:** 60% Gujarat / 40% other cities from Day 60.
4. **Ads:** Geo starts Ahmedabad → +Surat/Vadodara (Wk 4) → +Rajkot (Wk 8) → top-6 India cities (Day 90+), always with "Indian skin" creative (never "Gujarat only" creative nationally).
5. **Language:** Keep Gujarati in the brand's voice (signature lines, founder reels) even in national phase — it's a differentiator, not a barrier.
6. **The proof story travels:** "Gujarat me 20 testers, 100 pehli customers — ab India ke saath" is a *stronger* national launch story than a brand that never had a home.
