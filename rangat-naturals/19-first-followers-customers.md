# §24 FIRST 1,000 FOLLOWERS + §25 FIRST 100 CUSTOMERS — RANGAT NATURALS

## PART A: THE FIRST FOLLOWERS (Section 24)

**The north star:** followers who will DM "GLOW" — not vanity counts. A 500-follower account with 40 DMs/week beats a 5,000-follower account with 0. So every growth tactic below is a *buyer-acquisition* tactic.

### 0 → 100 (Days 1–7): the warm foundation
- **Founder's network (legitimate, high-converting):** founder posts Day-1 reel on personal IG/FB/WhatsApp with a genuine "yeh humara naya kaam hai, genuine hai, aapki family/friends ke liye bhi useful" note. Expect 50–80 of the first 100 from here. (These are buyers' mothers/sisters/college friends — the *best* first 100.)
- **20 testers = 20 micro-ambassadors:** each tester posts their Day-1 photo/story tagging RANGAT (their story = their network).
- **Every comment answered** (personalized, not "thank you!"): a real 2-line reply to a stranger = 30% follow-back rate.
- **Local tag + local hashtag on every post** (Ahmedabad geo + #Amdavad): the local Explore feed is where a new city brand lives.
- **Target: 100 by Day 7. Metric that matters: first 10 DM conversations.**

### 100 → 500 (Days 8–21): proof-led growth
- **The share machine:** 2 relatable/Gujarati reels per week (D5-style) — shares are how a 100-follower account reaches 10,000 people.
- **The save machine:** 2 education carousels/reels per week (saves = re-delivery to the same user for weeks + ranking boost).
- **First creator collab (Day 19):** 10k–30k Ahmedabad creator = instant 5k–30k *impressions* from her audience; 1–3% follow = 50–300 followers.
- **Day-28 before/after drop (Day 17):** the proof post is the follow-magnet for skeptics; pin it.
- **Local engagement loop (30 min/day):** comment genuinely (3–10 words, useful) on 20 posts from Ahmedabad/Gujarat food/lifestyle/wedding accounts — not begging, adding value. 5–15 profile visits/day from this.
- **Target: 500 by Day 21. Metric: 30 DMs/week, 2 reels >5k views.**

### 500 → 1,000 (Days 22–45): the launch wave
- **Launch-day triple touch (reel + Stories + WA broadcast + optional ad boost)** = the single biggest follower spike.
- **Founding 100 = 100 new buyers = 100 followers who *buy*** (every order confirmation asks: "follow karo — day 7/14/28 updates yahan aayenge" — buyers follow because updates are *for them*).
- **Creator #2–4 drops** (1/week).
- **YouTube Shorts repurposing** starts compounding (3/week) — slow, but search-driven followers are high-intent.
- **Target: 1,000 by Day 45. Metric: 60–100 orders done; DMs 50+/week.**

### 1,000 → 5,000 (Days 45–120): the system phase
- **Weekly rhythm holds:** 5–7 reels + 2 carousels + daily Stories; 1 creator/week; 2 UGC drops/week.
- **Ads start feeding the profile:** prospecting ads (C1) at ₹500–1,000/day with strong creatives = 200–500 profile visits/day; 2–4% follow = 10–60 followers/day *on top of* organic. (Ad followers are cheap here precisely because the creative carries proof.)
- **The "show":** UGC #20 (30-day challenge series) + weekly "4-hafte program" episodes = serialized content people *follow to finish* (follow-for-continuation is the strongest follow motivation).
- **Local events (2/month):** college sessions + micro-meetups → 100–300 followers each.
- **Target: 5,000 by ~Day 120. Metric: 500+ orders, 15%+ of followers engaged (30d reach > followers is the health signal).**

### 5,000 → 10,000 (Days 120–240): scale without selling the soul
- **National creator wave** (3–5/week, 20k–100k, Gujarat 40% / other cities 60%).
- **Ads at ₹2,000–5,000/day** with 6–10 live creatives (C4 scale) → profile visits 500–1,500/day.
- **One "campaign" per season:** e.g., "Wedding Glow 6-Hafte" (Oct–Dec), "Sardi ka Dhyan" (Jan–Feb) — a named, hashtagged, creator-amplified campaign is how 5k → 10k jumps happen (campaigns create shareable moments; single posts don't).
- **Collab posts with 2–3 established Indian skincare accounts** (barter → paid as budget allows): their followers, your proof.
- **Target: 10,000 by ~Day 240. Metric: 2,000+ orders, referral 20%+, 25%+ repeat.**

**Anti-vanity rules (always on):**
- No follow-for-follow, no engagement pods, no bought followers (they poison ad delivery and the DM ratio).
- Never optimize a post for "views only" — every awareness post still ends with a funnel CTA.
- Weekly, the 1 number = **new buyers from social** (not followers). Followers are the fuel gauge; orders are the destination.

## PART B: THE FIRST 100 CUSTOMERS (Section 25)

**The plan in one line:** *Proof before offer, offer before scale — 100 real customers in 30–45 days via DM pipeline + Founding 100 offer + micro-creators, all fed by one WhatsApp machine.*

### 1. WHAT CONTENT (the 30-day content job, mapped to buyers)
- Weeks 1–2: 100% proof-building (founder, problems, education, tester program start) — **no selling** (see `03-launch-4-weeks.md`).
- Weeks 3–4: proof drops (D17 B/A, D19 creator) + launch (D21) + objection killer (D22) + unboxing (D24) + testimonial (D26) + close (D30).
- Every buyer-stage asset from §21 Stages 2–6 is live by Day 21 (profile 3-second test, GLOW script, box, sequences).
- **The 5 "buyer" posts that will actually sell the first 100:** D8 (intro+waitlist), D17 (proof), D21 (launch), D22 (objection), D24 (unboxing). Everything else warms the pool.

### 2. WHAT OFFER — "FOUNDING 100"
- **Offer stack:** launch price (e.g., ₹599 where regular will be ₹799 — real future price, stated honestly) + **FREE Face Wash mini** (tangible, not fake %) + **FREE shipping** + **COD + return** + **review featured on page** (the social-pricing: your face on our wall).
- **Real constraint:** exactly 100 units of batch 1 (state the batch logic — "har order ka hum khud dhyan le sakte hain").
- **Why this offer works:** every element removes a *different* psychological blocker (§23): price (launch) + trial risk (free mini + COD) + waste fear (timeline + guide) + identity (founder-100 status + review feature).
- **Anti-patterns avoided:** no fake MRP strike, no "50% OFF" (nobody ever paid the struck price), no fake countdown (the batch is the countdown).

### 3. WHAT CREATOR STRATEGY (first 100)
- **5–10 micro-creators (5k–30k, Gujarat):** product free + ₹500–1,500 + affiliate 20% on 20 sales + ad permission.
- **Sequence:** creator #1 collab Day 19 (before launch — her audience sees the proof) → #2–3 in launch week (their reels = ad creatives) → #4–10 in weeks 4–6 (Sunrise for wave 2).
- **Each creator = 6 assets** (their reel, our repost, 2 ad cuts, 3 quote cards) — 10 creators = 60 assets = the content bank for Month 2.
- **The 30-day challenge creator (UGC #20)** runs from Day 21: 30 daily stories/posts from one real person = continuous social proof the whole month.

### 4. WHAT ADS
- **Days 1–14: none.** Day 15–20: optional ₹200/day on 1 validated organic reel (followers/waitlist only).
- **Day 21+: ₹500/day** (C1: broad Ahmedabad + interest Gujarat, 4 creatives, WA objective primary). Day 25+: +₹200/day retargeting (C3). Day 35+: scale winners.
- **Expected contribution:** at ₹500–700/day, ads = 30–50% of first-100 orders (organic+DM+creators = 50–70%). **Organic-first is the point — ads amplify proof, they don't replace it.**

### 5. WHAT CTA (one keyword, everywhere)
- **"GLOW"** — DM keyword, on-screen text, Stories, WhatsApp greeting, ad primary text, box card, referral card. One keyword = the whole funnel is trackable (count GLOW DMs = count top-of-funnel).
- Secondary CTA by stage: follow (strangers) → save (education) → GLOW (intent) → "apna code" (referral).

### 6. WHAT DM STRATEGY (the engine — 60–70% of first 100 close here)
- **Speed:** 15-minute SLA, 7 AM–11 PM (2 people: founder + 1 help, or founder + family). Auto-reply outside hours with a time promise.
- **The GLOW script** (5 steps, full version in §21 Stage 5): greet with the full offer → city → delivery time → objection handling (3 canned: price/doubt/timeline) → confirm name+city+product → instant confirmation + "Din 7 pe hum khud message karenge."
- **Every comment = 2-line real reply** (not emoji). Every question box answer = DM follow-up ("aapne pucha — detail WhatsApp pe bhej du?").
- **Weekly DM audit (20 min):** DM→order %; top 3 objections heard → next week's content answers them (content is made from DMs, not the other way).
- **Target:** 600–1,000 GLOW DMs by Day 30 → 40% conversion = 240–400 conversations → **80–100 orders.**

### 7. WHAT WHATSAPP STRATEGY
- **Business setup (Day 0):** catalog (3 products + kit), profile (real photo, address city, hours), quick replies (price/delivery/return/timeline), labels (NEW/ORDER/D7/D14/BUYER/REFER), auto-greeting, away message.
- **The lists:** WAITLIST (GLOW DMs, pre-launch broadcast Day 20: "Kal launch — reply GLOW, aapka number save") → BUYERS (all orders) → REVIEWS (photo consenters) → REFERRERS (code users).
- **Status = Stories** (3 slots/day) — Gujarat WhatsApp-Status usage is huge; zero extra work.
- **Broadcast rules:** max 2/week to WAITLIST (pre-launch), 2/week to BUYERS (sequences only, no spam), 1/week to all (milestone/offer). Every broadcast = one value + one CTA, signed by name.
- **The box comms:** 5 touches pre-delivery (including the *photo of their actual box*) — this single habit drives the unboxing UGC and the "look, mera parcel aaya" stories.

### 8. WHAT REFERRAL STRATEGY
- Launch Day 29 (after first testimonials exist): **"Do ₹100, Lo ₹100"** — friend ₹100 off first order, referrer ₹100 off next.
- **The ask moment:** Day-14 review message ends with: "Agar pasand aaya — 1 dost ko bata do. Aapko dono ko ₹100 milega. Code: [code]." (Asking at Day 14, when the product is working, is 3x more effective than Day 1.)
- **Targets:** 15–20% of orders referred by Day 45; referred orders tagged in the sheet (CAC ≈ ₹100 vs ₹300 ads).
- **The wall:** a pinned "Founding 100 ki kahani" post/Story showing real customer faces + cities (consented) — "aapki city me bhi koi hai" is a silent referral nudge for everyone scrolling.

**If behind at Day 30 (below 60):** do NOT add fake urgency. Diagnose in order: (1) DM reply speed > 15 min? → fix staffing. (2) GLOW DM count low? → hook problem (test 5 new hooks from `07-hooks-library.md`). (3) DM→order < 30%? → COD clarity / price / timeline objection script.
