**How to use:** This is the shoot bible. Each day = 1 primary asset (posted) + that day's Stories (in `13-stories-and-profile.md`). Languages: Hinglish default, Gujarati where marked (G). - **Script (VO/live):** "Hi, main [Name]. Main Gujarat se hoon, aur maine RANGAT NATURALS shuru kiya hai — Indian skin ke liye skincare. Is account par is chand me aap dikhoge: humne formula kaise choose kiya, 20 aahaiyon ne 4 hafte test kaise kiya, aur asli results kya aaye — filter nahi, fake photo nahi. Ek cheez promise karti hoon: jo dikhaayenge, wohi rahega. 30 din ke saath chalo. Pehla episode: hum kaun hain, aur kyun."
- **On-screen text:** [0-2s] "Zero followers. Ek sachchi shuruwat." → [mid] "20 testers · 4 hafte · 1 sachcha result" → [end] "RANGAT NATURALS — Asli skin. Asli result."
- **Caption:** "Ek naya Indian skincare brand. Ek honest promise: jo dikhaayenge, wohi rahega. Pehla episode: hum kaun hain. #RangatNaturals #IndianSkincare #Ahmedabad"
- **CTA:** "Follow karo — kal: 40 degree me aapki skin kar rahi hai kya?"
- **Content purpose:** Identity + trust anchor (founder face) + series promise.
- **Target customer:** All — awareness cast. Especially 20–35 Gujarat.

## DAY 2 (Tue) — REEL · 25–35s · Pillar 1: PROBLEM (no product)
- **Platform:** IG Reel → FB
- **Idea:** The 40°/monsoon/AC trinity problem — the single most relatable Gujarat skin complaint.
- **Hook:** "Gujarat me mausam dekho. Aapki skin abhi kya kar rahi hai — aapko pata hai?"
- **Concept:** 3 quick location cuts (or 3 outfit/lighting changes to simulate): (1) outside/bright — sweat, shine; (2) AC room — face tight/dull; (3) mirror selfie — "thaka hua" look. Founder talks over B-roll.
- **Script:** "Bahar 40 degree — oil, shine, pores wide. Ghar ka AC — tightness, dry patches. Aur dono ke beech ek look jo har mirror me dikhta hai — thoda dull, thoda 'brown'. Yeh teeno cheezein aapki skin roz saamne aati hain. Aur yeh teeno fixable hain — sahi routine se. Agle din se hum ise step by step samjhogi. Aapka sabse bada skin problem kya hai? Comment me likho — hum padh rahe hain."
- **On-screen text:** [hook] "40° bahar. AC andar. Skin beech me." → "Oil · Tightness · Dullness" → "Fixable hai. Kal se."
- **Caption:** "Indian skin ko Indian mausam samajhna padta hai. Aapka #1 skin issue kya hai — oily, dull, ya dono? Comment karo. #Gujarat #IndianSkin #MonsoonSkincare"
- **CTA:** "Comment karo apna skin type: oily / dry / normal / confused 😅"
- **Content purpose:** Attention + comment velocity (algorithm) + pain-point harvesting.
- **Target customer:** Women 20–35, Gujarat, oily/dull skin.
- **Sales purpose:** Collect real pain points → tomorrow's DM follow-ups + future content. No selling.

## DAY 4 (Thu) — REEL · 30–40s · Pillar 2: MYTH VS REALITY
- **Platform:** IG Reel → FB → (save for YT Shorts later: title "100% herbal cream kya safe hai?")
- **Idea:** "100% herbal = safe?" — the myth every Indian buyer has, attacked honestly.
- **Hook:** "'100% herbal' likha hai toh lagao? Pehle yeh sach jano."
- **Concept:** Founder to camera; B-roll: generic herbal jar (no brand visible), ingredient card, hand with a drop of serum. Split-screen "MYTH / REALITY" graphic for 2 sec.
- **Script:** "Myth: herbal matlab safe, chemical matlab nuksaan. Reality: haldi, amla, koi bhi natural ingredient — galat concentration me ya galat skin par — irritation de sakta hai. Aur 'no chemicals' possible nahi bhi hai — water bhi chemical hai. Asli baat yeh hai: ingredient + concentration + aapka skin type. Teeno match kare toh herbal bhi kaam karta hai, scientific bhi. Yehi soch ke humne apna formula choose kiya. Kal: kyun exactly wahi choose kiya."
- **On-screen text:** "MYTH: Herbal = Safe" → "REALITY: Concentration + Skin Type = Key" → "H2O bhi chemical hai 😄"
- **Caption:** "Aap kya check karti ho — brand ka naam ya ingredient list? Hum ingredient se shuru karte hain. #SkincareMyths #SkincareEducation"
- **CTA:** "Follow karo — kal: humara 'Gujarati ghar' reel aayega."
- **Content purpose:** Authority + differentiation (neither "totka-only" nor "chemical-scare").
- **Target customer:** 25–45, ingredient-curious, skeptical of both extremes.
- **Sales purpose:** Pre-frames Pillar 3 transparency (tomorrow's brand story).
