# §4 NEW ACCOUNT LAUNCH STRATEGY — RANGAT NATURALS (Day 1 → Week 4)

**The launch is a 30-day story, not a day.** The story arc:
- **Week 1 — "Who is this?"** (identity + problems, zero selling)
- **Week 2 — "What do they know?"** (education + hero product introduced)
- **Week 3 — "Is it real?"** (proof + launch countdown)
- **Week 4 — "How do I buy?"** (launch + Founding 100 + conversion)

Before Day 1 (Day 0 — do this, non-negotiable):
1. Profile fully built per `13-stories-and-profile.md` (bio, name field, 5 highlights, 3 placeholder-quality pinned posts: founder intro, "what is RANGAT," one problem reel).
2. WhatsApp Business set up: catalog (3 products), quick replies, labels, greeting message, business profile photo = same as IG.
3. 20-person **tester list** assembled (friends/family/college, Ahmedabad, women 20–40, mix of skin types) — the 4-week proof program starts Day 1 with the actual product batch.
4. 10 micro-creator shortlist (5k–50k, Gujarat) with email/DM template ready (see `12-gujarat-first.md` §creators).
5. Batch-shoot Day 1–7 content in one 4-hour session (see `21-production-system.md`).

## WEEK 1: DAY 1 → DAY 7 ("WHO IS THIS?")

Goal: ~150–300 genuine followers, 20 testers started, 10+ DM conversations, zero sales pressure.

### DAY 1 (Mon) — REEL · Pillar 6 (Founder)
**Idea:** The honest intro. No logo animation. Founder on camera, plain background, natural light.
**Hook (spoken + on-screen):** "Main ek naye skincare brand ki shuruwat kar rahi hu — bina kisi bade budget ke. Is series me main sab dikhau gi, asli me."
**Script:** "Hi, main [Name], RANGAT NATURALS ki founder. Gujarat se. Humne Indian skin ke liye skincare banayi hai — aur is account par main weekly dikhau gi: formula kaise chuna, 20 aahaiyon ne kaise test kiya, aur asli results kya aaye. Koi filter nahi, koi fake result nahi. Agli 30 din ke saath raho."
**On-screen text:** "RANGAT NATURALS — Asli skin. Asli result." → "30 din ka safar. Follow karo."
**Caption:** "Ek naya Indian skincare brand. Ek honest promise: jo dikhaayenge, wohi rahega. Pehla episode: hum kaun hain. #RangatNaturals #IndianSkincare"
**CTA:** "Follow karo — kal: Gujarat me 40 degree aapki skin kar rahi hai kya?"

## WEEK 2 ("WHAT DO THEY KNOW?") — Days 8–14, one-line plan per day

- **D8 (Mon) — REEL, Pillar 3: HERO PRODUCT INTRO.** "Mila karo: RANGAT Vitamin C Serum 10%." Texture drop, 3 honest benefits (brightens look, evens tone-look over time, fresh feel), price anchor, "launch coming — DM GLOW to be first in line." **This is the first product post.** CTA: comment "GLOW."
- **D9 (Tue) — REEL, Pillar 2+3: HOW-TO.** "Vitamin C serum 99% log galat tarah se lagate hain." (Dry face, 3–4 drops, morning, SPF after, not with strong acids same time.) CTA: save.
- **D10 (Wed) — CAROUSEL, Pillar 2: INGREDIENT DEEP DIVE.** 7 slides: "Vitamin C serum actually kya karta hai? (60-second science)." Slides: what VC does → 10% why (sweet spot) → who benefits most (dull/tan-look/pigmentation-look) → who should be careful (irritation-prone: patch test) → what it WON'T do (no overnight miracles) → how to store (dark, cool) → CTA save.
- **D11 (Thu) — REEL, Pillar 1+3: PROBLEM→SOLUTION.** "Bahar ka main, andar ka dull — dono same cheez." (Outdoor UV + indoor screen/window UV both add up.) Soft product nod. CTA: comment GLOW.
- **D12 (Fri) — REEL, Pillar 4: FIRST TESTER REACTION.** Real tester (friend/relative, disclosed "humari tester"), week-2 honest reaction: "Fresh lagta hai, par meri dry patch abhi bhi hai — honest hai." CTA: "Day 28 wala update is page par aayega — follow karo."
- **D13 (Sat) — REEL, Pillar 6: FOUNDER STORY #2.** "Brand banana kitna mushkil tha" — rejected first formula, 3 packaging samples, why we chose this manufacturer (certified facility, GMP line). Transparency = trust. CTA: share.
- **D14 (Sun) — REEL, Pillar 7 (soft) + Q&A stories.** "Achhi skincare mehngi honi chahiye? Nahi." Price-value reel: honest breakdown (formula + testing + certified manufacturing + no middleman = your price). Still no hard offer. CTA: "Launch is hafte — GLOW likho." Stories: countdown to launch (3 days), tester day-14 photo (with consent + disclaimer), quiz "kitna SPF chahiye?"

**Week 2 exit criteria:** 500+ followers, 30+ DMs collected ("GLOW" list), tester program halfway (14 days), 10 creators contacted.

- **D17 (Wed) — REEL, Pillar 5: FIRST REAL BEFORE/AFTER.** Tester program **day-28** results (real, disclosed, 2–3 faces max, same light/angle, "4 hafte daily use · results may vary" on screen). This is the most important trust asset of the launch. CTA: "DM GLOW — launch kal se."
- **D18 (Thu) — REEL, Pillar 2: EDUCATION (future product seed).** "Ghar par baithe bhi sunscreen kyun?" (UV through windows, cumulative exposure.) Sets up Month-2 product without selling it. CTA: share with a friend.
- **D19 (Fri) — REEL, Pillar 4: FIRST MICRO-CREATOR DROP.** Ahmedabad creator (10k–30k) posts her honest "2 hafte RANGAT" video; we repost + collab. CTA: follow both.
- **D20 (Sat) — REEL, Pillar 6: LAUNCH COUNTDOWN + BTS.** "Kal se RANGAT NATURALS live." Packing the first 100 boxes (real footage), "100 units, phir agla batch." CTA: notifications on.
