# SECTION 8: ORGANIC SALES PLAYBOOK

## 8.1 Why Organic First

Paid ads are the easiest way to spend money. Organic is the hardest way to make money. That is exactly why we **start organic and only move to paid when organic is exhausted.**

A brand that can't sell organically will fail the day its ad budget runs out. A brand that sells organically first will scale profitably when it does turn on ads.

Organic also gives us **customer learnings** — what hooks work, what objections come up, what language customers use, what makes them trust us. We then feed this learning into our paid ads.

## 8.3 WHATSAPP ORGANIC

WhatsApp is **the most underused channel in Indian D2C.** We make it a core channel.

### 8.3.1 WhatsApp Tools
- **WhatsApp Business App** (free for first 1,000 customers)
- **Wati / Interakt** (₹1,500/month for automation when customer count >500)

### 8.3.2 WhatsApp Funnels

**1. Lead Capture**
- Instagram bio links to WhatsApp ("DM us 'GLOW' on WhatsApp")
- Website has WhatsApp click-to-chat button
- Salon staff sends customers to WhatsApp
- Founder's personal number on every box

**2. Lead Nurture**
- Auto-reply with welcome message + routine guide
- Send ingredient breakdown (image)
- Send founder's story video (1 min)
- Send offer: "First order ₹449 with code WELCOME15"

**3. Conversion**
- Personalized product recommendation
- Direct payment link (Razorpay)
- Order confirmation + tracking

**4. Retention**
- Day 7: "How is your skin feeling?" + ask for review
- Day 30: "Time to re-order?" + reorder link
- Day 45: "Try the moisturizer for ₹100 off"
- Day 60: "Tell a friend, get ₹100"
- Day 90: "Your skin journey so far"

### 8.3.3 WhatsApp Broadcast Lists
- Build **5 broadcast lists** of 256 each (1,280 max)
- Customers, leads, influencers, salon partners, friends/family
- Broadcast offers once a week max (don't spam)

### 8.3.4 WhatsApp Status
- Daily status update: behind-the-scenes, packing, founder life
- 5-10 stories per day on personal + business WhatsApp
- Highest organic reach for WhatsApp status

## 8.6 MICRO-INFLUENCER ORGANIC

### 8.6.1 The 1,000-Micro-Influencer Plan

| Phase | Quantity | Type | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1-2 | 30 | Ahmedabad micro (5-25K followers) | Gifting only |
| Month 3-4 | 100 | Gujarat + Mumbai + Bangalore micro (10-50K) | Gifting + ₹1K-3K fee |
| Month 5-6 | 300 | Pan-India micro (5-100K) | Gifting + ₹1K-5K fee |
| Month 6-12 | 1,000 | National creator network | Mostly gifting + revenue share |

### 8.6.2 Outreach Template

```
Hi [Name]! I'm [Founder], founder of RANGAT NATURALS, a new skincare 
brand from Gujarat. We've just launched our 10% Vitamin C Serum 
(₹499) and I'd love to send you a free bottle to try for 2 weeks.

If you love it, I'd be honoured if you shared your honest review 
with your audience. No script — just your real experience.

Would you be open to this?
[Calendar link]
```

**Response rate:** 5-10% of outreach converts to a gifting deal. Of those, 30-50% actually post content.

### 8.6.3 Influencer Gifting Kit

Every gifting package includes:
- 1 full-size Glow Serum
- 1 mini moisturizer (cross-sell preview)
- Personal handwritten note from founder
- Ingredient card + brand story insert
- Unique code (e.g., "PRIYA15" for 15% off) — track conversions

**Cost per kit:** ₹700-900

## 8.7 UGC (USER-GENERATED CONTENT)

### 8.7.1 UGC Collection Tactics

1. **Always ask permission** + offer ₹100-off next order for a video review.
2. **WhatsApp community** of 100 early customers — they share their skin journey weekly.
3. **Hashtag:** #RangatGlow #RangatRoutine — repost customer content.
4. **"RANGAT Babe of the Month"** — feature one customer on Instagram each month.
5. **UGC creator network** — pay 5-10 creators ₹500-1,500/month for authentic content (not paid promotion).

### 8.7.2 UGC Usage Rights

Always get written consent (even WhatsApp message is OK):
> "Can we use your photo/video on our Instagram, website, and Amazon listing? We'll tag you."

## 8.8 REFERRALS

### 8.8.1 The Referral Program

> **"Give ₹100, Get ₹100"**
> - Customer shares unique code.
> - Friend uses code at checkout → both get ₹100 off next order.
> - Codes are single-use, valid 30 days.

### 8.8.2 Where to Promote Referral

- On every box (insert card)
- In post-purchase email
- In WhatsApp follow-up message
- In Instagram bio link
- On Amazon "thank you" card (post-delivery message)

### 8.8.3 Expected Referral Rate

Top quartile brands achieve 10-15% of customers referring someone. Realistic target: 5-8% in Year 1.

### 8.9.3 Society Commerce

- Founder joins **5-10 apartment complex WhatsApp groups** in own area.
- Posts a "skincare demo at clubhouse" event.
- Hosts 1-hour demo + Q&A + samples.
- Takes orders on the spot.
- Converts 15-25 customers per society event.

### 8.9.4 Office Commerce

- Approach HR teams of 5-10 IT companies in Ahmedabad.
- Offer "wellness Wednesday" demo at office.
- 30-60 employees per office, 10-15% conversion = 3-9 customers per office.
- Repeat every 2 months.

### 8.9.5 Wedding Commerce

- Approach 10 wedding planners.
- Offer "Bridesmaid Gift Box" (3 mini products + branded pouch) at ₹999.
- Planners buy 10-30 boxes per wedding.
- Each box introduces 1-3 new customers.
