# SECTION 3: FIRST PRODUCT STRATEGY

## 3.1 Why This Section Is the Most Important

Most new Indian brands die because they launch 8 products at once with no clear hero. Then they have 8 SKUs with no sales. Then their money runs out. Then they shut down.

We will launch **ONE product first**. Then a second. Then a third. Each one is built on the customer base of the previous one.

This is the strategy of Mamaearth (aloe vera gel first), Minimalist (vitamin C serum first), The Derma Co. (aha serum first), mCaffeine (under-eye cream first). **Single hero SKU → prove demand → expand.**

### Why this is the FIRST product

**1. Massive search demand, low brand loyalty**
"Vitamin C serum" is the #2 searched skincare term in India after "sunscreen". 6.2 lakh monthly Google searches in India. Existing brands are crowded but none own it.

**2. Solves a real, visible, emotional problem**
Every Indian woman from 19 to 35 wants brighter, more even skin tone. Vitamin C is the most-requested active. It's a problem people will pay to solve.

**3. Repeat purchase built in**
A 30ml serum used at 4-5 drops a day lasts ~50-60 days. So the natural reorder cycle is 2 months. Perfect for our LTV math.

**4. Easy to manufacture**
Every contract manufacturer in India (Personal Care Pvt Ltd, Ozone, Vedic, Marico's contract arms) can make a 10% vitamin C serum. We are not asking for a custom molecule.

**5. Content-rich**
We can make 60+ pieces of content (reels, posts, carousels) about a single vitamin C serum. Educational, demo, before/after, myth-busting, ingredient-deep-dive. Content is the cheapest marketing channel.

**6. Testable in 4-6 weeks**
Vitamin C results show in 4-6 weeks (brighter tone, reduced pigmentation). This means we can collect real testimonials in time for ads.

**7. Founder-friendly price point**
₹499 is the sweet spot for "first-time online skincare buyer" in India. Below the impulse-buy threshold but above the "is this legit?" ₹199-₹299 zone where customers assume it's cheap junk.

**8. Founder has personal experience**
The founder (and their circle) has personally used vitamin C serums. They can recommend it authentically, not from a slide deck.

## 3.4 Problem Solved

**Problem:** Indian skin in Gujarat faces constant sun, hard water, and humidity. Result: pigmentation, dullness, tan lines, uneven tone. Most customers want brighter skin but:
- Pharmacy brands (Cetaphil, ponds) don't address pigmentation actively.
- Instagram brands use weak actives (3% niacinamide) and call it a "glow serum".
- Luxury brands (Estée Lauder, Clinique) charge ₹3,500-₹7,000 for 30ml.

**Solution we offer:** A clinical-strength 10% vitamin C serum, formulated for Indian humidity, at ₹499. Visible brightening in 4 weeks. Backed by a transparent ingredient list and a founder's personal number.

## 3.8 Product #4 and Beyond (Months 7-12)

After the **3-step core routine** (Serum → Moisturizer → Sunscreen) is established, we expand only when the data says so:

- **Face wash** (cleanser) — ₹299 entry point, gateway product
- **Night cream with bakuchiol + retinol 0.3%** — ₹699
- **Under-eye cream with caffeine + peptides** — ₹449
- **Lip mask** — ₹299
- **Hair oil with rosemary + bhringraj** — ₹399
- **Body lotion with 3% AHA + shea** — ₹399

**Rule:** No product is launched unless we can answer "Which existing customer asks for this?" If we can't name 100 such customers, we don't make it.

## 3.9 The Routine Lock-in Strategy

The genius of this roadmap is that **once a customer buys the Glow Serum, she's a candidate for the moisturizer. Once she buys both, she's a candidate for sunscreen. Once she buys all three, she's a RANGAT routine customer for life.**

A customer buying 3 products at ₹499 + ₹399 + ₹599 = **₹1,497** in 6 months. That's our LTV target. With a CAC of ₹200-300, we have a 5x LTV/CAC ratio — the gold standard in D2C.
