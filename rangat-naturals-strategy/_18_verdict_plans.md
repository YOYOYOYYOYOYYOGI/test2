# SECTION 18: CEO VERDICT + 30/60/90 DAY PLAN

## 18.1 The CEO Verdict

If I were reading this strategy document as a potential investor, partner, or team member, here is what I'd want to know:

> **RANGAT NATURALS is a Gujarat-origin, founder-led, ingredient-transparent Indian skincare brand built for the modern Indian woman who wants effective skincare without paying luxury prices or following 12-step routines.**

> **We will launch in Ahmedabad with one hero product (10% Vitamin C Glow Serum at ₹499). We will get to 1,000 customers in 4 months, ₹12L monthly revenue by Month 12, ₹1.5-2 crore annual revenue run rate by Month 18, and become a household skincare brand from Gujarat in 3-5 years.**

> **The strategy works because:**
> 1. **We own a defensible white space** (active transparency + Indian climate testing + founder accessibility + routine simplicity + Gujarat origin). No competitor owns this combination.
> 2. **The unit economics work** — 60% gross margin, 25-30% marketing spend, profitable from Month 6+.
> 3. **The customer is real and accessible** — 6.2 lakh monthly searches for "vitamin C serum" in India, growing 18% YoY.
> 4. **The repeat engine is built in** — 40-50% reorder rate is achievable with the right WhatsApp + subscription engine.
> 5. **The execution roadmap is concrete** — every day, every rupee, every metric is defined.

> **The risks are:**
> 1. Founder burnout (mitigation: sustainability plan, hiring early)
> 2. CAC rises above ₹250 (mitigation: organic + referral flywheel)
> 3. Product quality issues (mitigation: 6-week human testing before launch)
> 4. Competitive copy-cats (mitigation: founder trust moat, hard to replicate)

> **The decision is clear: BUILD.**

## 18.8 The Decision Rules

When in doubt at any decision point, ask:

```
Does this help get a new customer?        → Yes → Do it.
Does this help retain an existing customer? → Yes → Do it.
Does this build brand trust?              → Yes → Do it.
Does this scale revenue profitably?       → Yes → Do it.
Is this a one-time vanity project?        → Probably → Skip.
Can a competitor copy this in 30 days?    → Yes → It's not a moat, deprioritize.
```

If a decision passes 3+ of these tests, execute it within 48 hours.
