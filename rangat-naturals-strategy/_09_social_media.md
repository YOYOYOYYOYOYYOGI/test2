# SECTION 9: SOCIAL MEDIA STRATEGY

## 9.1 The Strategic Goal

Social media is not "build followers." Social media is **"turn strangers into customers, one piece of content at a time."**

We measure social success in:
- Orders attributable to social (UTM tracked)
- CAC from social
- Repeat purchase rate by source
- Brand search lift (Instagram → Google search)

Followers are a vanity metric. Orders are the real metric.

### 9.3.4 Reel Production (DIY)

**Equipment:**
- Phone (iPhone 12+ or any flagship Android)
- Ring light (₹500 on Amazon)
- Phone tripod (₹400)
- Lavalier mic (₹600)
- Canva (for thumbnails)

**Shooting:**
- Vertical (9:16), 15-60 seconds
- First 1.5 seconds = hook (text + visual)
- Middle = content
- End = CTA ("Save this for later", "Comment 'GLOW' for link")

**Editing:**
- CapCut (free)
- Add captions (text-on-screen)
- Add trending audio
- Export at 1080p

## 9.4 YOUTUBE STRATEGY

### 9.4.1 YouTube Channel Purpose
YouTube is **long-form SEO**. A 10-minute YouTube video ranks on Google and drives organic traffic for 5+ years.

### 9.4.2 Content Pillars
1. **Skincare Education** — "How vitamin C works on Indian skin", "Layering actives"
2. **Founder Vlogs** — "Why I started RANGAT", "Day in the life"
3. **Product Deep-dives** — "What's inside Glow Serum", "How we formulated it"
4. **Reviews & Comparisons** — "RANGAT vs Minimalist", "Best ₹500 vitamin C serums"
5. **Behind-the-scenes** — "Inside our factory", "How an order is packed"

### 9.4.3 Upload Cadence
- Month 1-3: 1 video per week (8-15 min each)
- Month 4-12: 2 videos per week

### 9.4.4 SEO for YouTube
- Title: Include keyword ("Best vitamin C serum India 2026")
- Description: 200+ words, links to product
- Tags: 10-15 relevant tags
- Thumbnail: Custom (Canva), bold text + founder face + product
- Cards + End screens: Link to product page
