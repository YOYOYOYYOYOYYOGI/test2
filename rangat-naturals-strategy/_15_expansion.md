# SECTION 15: BRAND EXPANSION ROADMAP

## 15.1 The 4 Phases

| Phase | Geography | Duration | Revenue target | Customers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 — Gujarat Domination** | Gujarat only | Month 1-9 | ₹25-35L cumulative | 1,500-2,500 |
| **Phase 2 — National D2C** | Pan-India online | Month 9-18 | ₹1-2 crore annual | 10,000-15,000 |
| **Phase 3 — National Scale** | Pan-India + offline | Month 18-36 | ₹5-15 crore annual | 50,000-100,000 |
| **Phase 4 — International** | SE Asia, Middle East, UK | Year 3-5 | ₹50+ crore annual | 200,000+ |

---

## 15.2 PHASE 1: GUJARAT DOMINATION (Month 1-9)

### 15.2.1 Objective
Build a brand that **owns "skincare" in Gujarat before going national.** This means:
- Top-of-mind recall in Ahmedabad
- 1,500+ verified customers in Gujarat
- 4.5+ star rating on Amazon with 200+ reviews mentioning Gujarat
- Featured in 3+ Gujarati publications
- Recognized by 5+ Ahmedabad dermatologists

### 15.2.2 Strategy

| Lever | Action |
|---|---|
| **City coverage** | Ahmedabad → Surat → Vadodara → Rajkot → Bhavnagar → Jamnagar → Bharuch → rest of Gujarat |
| **Content** | 50% Gujarati + 30% Hindi + 20% English |
| **Distribution** | D2C site (35%) + Salons (25%) + Amazon (20%) + Events (15%) + WhatsApp (5%) |
| **Marketing** | 60% organic + 30% Meta ads + 10% influencer |
| **Team** | Founder + 1 helper + freelance designer + freelance video editor |

### 15.2.3 KPIs

| KPI | Target by Month 9 |
|---|---|
| Customers (cumulative) | 2,500 |
| Repeat purchase rate | 35% |
| AOV | ₹550 |
| Revenue (cumulative) | ₹30-35L |
| Instagram followers (Gujarat-dominant) | 25,000 |
| Reviews (total) | 500+ |
| NPS | 50+ |

### 15.2.4 Trigger to Move to Phase 2

> **All of the following:**
> - ✅ 1,500+ Gujarat customers
> - ✅ 4.5+ rating on Amazon with 200+ reviews
> - ✅ 35%+ repeat purchase rate
> - ✅ Profitable unit economics (LTV:CAC > 3:1)
> - ✅ 3+ SKUs with proven demand
> - ✅ Subscription product live
> - ✅ Founder has personal bandwidth beyond Gujarat (or hires team)

If these are met, we have the **machine** to scale. Without these, we don't.

## 15.3 PHASE 2: NATIONAL D2C (Month 9-18)

### 15.3.1 Objective
**Take the Gujarat machine and run it nationally through digital channels** (Amazon, D2C site, Meta ads, Google ads). No offline national expansion yet.

### 15.3.2 Strategy

| Lever | Action |
|---|---|
| **City coverage** | Top 10 metros + Tier-2 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata + Jaipur, Lucknow, Indore) |
| **Content** | 70% English + 20% Hindi + 10% Gujarati (Gujarati becomes heritage content) |
| **Distribution** | D2C site (40%) + Amazon (30%) + Nykaa (10%) + Flipkart (5%) + Meta/Google ads drive D2C + marketplaces |
| **Marketing** | 30% organic + 50% paid (Meta + Google) + 20% influencer |
| **Team** | Founder + Marketing manager + Operations + Customer support (4-6 people) |

### 15.3.3 New Investments

| Investment | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hire marketing manager | ₹40-60K/month | Scale ads, manage content team |
| Hire ops/logistics | ₹25-35K/month | Order fulfillment, inventory |
| Hire customer support | ₹20-30K/month | WhatsApp + email + DMs |
| New product launches (1-2 SKUs) | ₹2-3L | Broaden catalog |
| Influencer network expansion | ₹1-2L/month | Pan-India reach |
| PR agency / freelance PR | ₹25-50K/month | National press |

### 15.3.4 KPIs

| KPI | Target by Month 18 |
|---|---|
| Customers (cumulative) | 12,000-15,000 |
| Repeat purchase rate | 40% |
| AOV | ₹650 |
| Annual revenue run rate | ₹1.5-2 crore |
| Cities served | 50+ |
| Subscriptions | 1,500-2,000 |

### 15.3.5 Trigger to Move to Phase 3

> **All of the following:**
> - ✅ ₹1 crore annual run rate
> - ✅ Profitable on ad spend (Meta + Google ROAS > 2.5x)
> - ✅ Amazon top 500 in serum category
> - ✅ 10,000+ cumulative customers
> - ✅ Team of 5+ people
> - ✅ 3+ SKUs selling consistently
> - ✅ 5+ dermatologists recommending nationally

If these are met, we have the **operational muscle** to handle offline expansion.

## 15.4 PHASE 3: NATIONAL SCALE (Month 18-36)

### 15.4.1 Objective
**Build an omnichannel national brand** — D2C + marketplaces + offline retail + salons + pharmacies.

### 15.4.2 Strategy

| Lever | Action |
|---|---|
| **Distribution mix** | D2C (30%) + Marketplaces (25%) + Offline retail (20%) + Salons (15%) + Pharmacies (10%) |

| **Pharmacy** | 500+ chemist shops in top 10 cities |

| **Marketing** | TV/OTT ads (₹50L-₹2Cr/year), OOH in 5 cities, Meta/Google (₹3-5L/month), Influencer (₹2-3L/month) |
| **Team** | 15-30 people: marketing, sales, ops, customer support, R&D liaison, PR |

| New product launches (3-4 SKUs/year) | ₹10-20L/year |

| Manufacturing scale-up | Negotiate volume discounts with manufacturers |

### 15.4.4 KPIs

| KPI | Target by Month 36 |
|---|---|
| Customers (cumulative) | 50,000-100,000 |
| Annual revenue | ₹5-15 crore |
| Repeat purchase rate | 45% |
| AOV | ₹700 |
| Cities served | 100+ |
| Retail outlets | 500-1,500 |
| Subscriptions | 5,000-10,000 |
| Subscribers | 5-10% of customer base |

### 15.4.5 Trigger to Move to Phase 4

> **All of the following:**
> - ✅ ₹5+ crore annual revenue
> - ✅ 30,000+ customers
> - ✅ Brand known nationally (aided awareness > 20% in metros)
> - ✅ Profitable or near-profitable at EBITDA level
> - ✅ Team of 15+ people
> - ✅ 5+ SKUs in market
> - ✅ International regulatory readiness (formulations tested for target markets)

## 15.5 PHASE 4: INTERNATIONAL (Year 3-5)

### 15.5.1 Objective
**Take the brand global.** Start with markets that have:
- Large Indian diaspora (UK, US, Canada, UAE, Australia, Singapore)
- Growing skincare markets (SE Asia, Middle East)
- Indian-friendly regulatory environment (UAE, Singapore, UK are easier than US/EU)

### 15.5.2 Strategy

| Market | Approach | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **UAE / Dubai** | Amazon UAE + Noon.com + offline retail (Lulu, Carrefour) | Large Indian diaspora, premium positioning, English-friendly |
| **Singapore** | Online + retail at Watsons, Sephora | High skincare spend, English-speaking |
| **UK** | Amazon UK + Cult Beauty + Selfridges | Skincare-obsessed market, Indian brand appeal |
| **US** | Amazon US + DTC site | Largest market, but FDA compliance is heavy |
| **SE Asia (Malaysia, Indonesia)** | Local marketplaces + DTC | Huge skincare market, growing |
| **Australia** | Mecca + Adore Beauty | High skincare spend, Indian diaspora |

### 15.5.3 Compliance Per Market

| Market | Key requirement |
|---|---|
| **UAE** | MoHAP product notification |
| **Singapore** | HSA notification |
| **UK** | SCPN notification (post-Brexit) |
| **US** | FDA cosmetic registration (Voluntary Cosmetic Registration Program) |
| **EU** | CPNP notification, EU-compliant formulations |

### 15.5.4 International Marketing

- **Founders Reels in English** for global audience
- **Influencer partnerships** with global creators
- **International PR** in beauty publications (Allure, Vogue, Byrdie)
- **Localized packaging** (ingredient list in local language + English)
- **Multi-currency pricing**
- **Region-specific formulations** (e.g., SPF booster for Australia UV index)

### 15.5.5 Phase 4 KPIs (Year 3-5)

| KPI | Year 3 | Year 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Countries served | 3-5 | 8-10 |
| International customers | 5,000 | 30,000 |
| International revenue | 10-15% of total | 25-30% of total |
| International AOV | $30+ | $35+ |
| Team | 30-50 | 80-150 |

## 15.7 The Expansion Philosophy

> **"Don't expand to grow. Expand because customers are pulling you."**

If Mumbai customers are finding RANGAT on Amazon organically, that's a signal to expand. If no one in Mumbai is buying, no amount of advertising will fix that.

Customer-led expansion beats CEO-led expansion every time.

---

## 15.8 The Risk Map by Phase

| Phase | Biggest risks | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | Cash runs out, founder burnout, no repeat purchase | Founder does customer support, focus on reorder engine |
| **Phase 2** | CAC spikes, ad fatigue, founder can't scale | Hire marketing manager, build organic flywheel |
| **Phase 3** | Offline retail eats margin, inventory pile-up | Phased offline entry, demand-driven manufacturing |
| **Phase 4** | Regulatory failure, currency risk, supply chain | Local compliance partner, multi-currency pricing |
