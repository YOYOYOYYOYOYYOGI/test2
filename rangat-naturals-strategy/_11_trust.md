# SECTION 11: TRUST BUILDING FRAMEWORK

## 11.1 Why Trust Is the Hardest Part

For an unknown brand, **trust is more important than product.** Customers can buy a similar product from a known brand. To get them to buy from you, you need to remove the risk in their head:

> *"What if it's fake?"*
> *"What if it ruins my skin?"*
> *"What if I don't get a refund?"*
> *"What if they don't reply to my complaint?"*

Every element of our brand must answer one of these questions.

## 11.3 REVIEWS

### 11.3.1 How Reviews Build Trust

Reviews are **the #1 conversion driver** for skincare on Amazon. A product with 50+ 4-star reviews converts at 3-5x the rate of a product with 0 reviews.

### 11.3.2 How to Get Reviews (Ethically)

**1. In-box review request**
- Insert card: "Loved it? Leave a review on Amazon / Website. Get ₹50 off next order."

**2. WhatsApp follow-up**
- Day 7 message: "How's your skin feeling? Mind sharing a quick review? Reply with a photo + 2 lines and we'll send ₹100 off next order."

**3. Email follow-up**
- Day 7 email with review link + ₹50 off

**4. Amazon Vine / Early Reviewer**
- Sign up for Amazon Vine (after 5 reviews) — pays reviewers
- Cost: ₹5,000-15,000 per product for first 30 reviews

**5. Influencer gifting**
- Every gifting kit includes request for honest review
- Code unique to influencer tracks conversions

**6. Buyer persona outreach**
- Find 50 customers who've bought 2+ times → ask for video testimonial

### 11.3.3 What to NEVER Do

- ❌ Buy fake reviews (Amazon catches and bans)
- ❌ Incentivize ONLY positive reviews ("Pay ₹500 for 5 stars")
- ❌ Review-gate ("Only review if 4-5 stars")
- ❌ Use stock photos as reviews

### 11.3.4 Review Targets

| Channel | Month 3 | Month 6 | Month 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website reviews | 30 | 150 | 600 |
| Amazon reviews | 20 | 200 | 1,500 |
| Instagram UGC | 50 | 300 | 1,500 |
| Video testimonials | 5 | 30 | 100 |

## 11.6 INGREDIENT TRANSPARENCY

### 11.6.1 The Approach

We treat the ingredient list as a **marketing asset**, not a regulatory burden.

### 11.6.2 What We Do

1. **Active ingredient % on FRONT of pack** ("Vitamin C 10%")
2. **Full ingredient list on BACK** (INCI-compliant)
3. **"Why this ingredient" card** inside every box — explains each active in plain English/Gujarati/Hindi
4. **Website ingredient glossary** — every ingredient has a page
5. **"What's NOT in it" badge** — "Free from parabens, sulphates, mineral oil, synthetic fragrance"
6. **No "chemical-free" or "100% natural" claims** (they're false)

### 11.6.3 The Ingredient Education Series

Weekly Instagram carousel:
- Slide 1: "Today we're talking about niacinamide"
- Slide 2: "What is it?"
- Slide 3: "What does it do?"
- Slide 4: "What % should you use?"
- Slide 5: "Which RANGAT product has it?"
- Slide 6: "Save this for later"

This builds trust AND sells products.

## 11.8 CERTIFICATIONS & COMPLIANCE

### 11.8.1 Mandatory (Indian Law)

| Requirement | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **CDSCO Product Notification** | Register each cosmetic on Sugam portal before sale | ₹5,000-10,000 per product |
| **Manufacturing License** | Partner manufacturer must have valid license | Included in mfr cost |
| **Labelling compliance** | INCI list, batch number, MRP, mfr address, expiry, net weight | Packaging cost |
| **Import compliance** (if any imported ingredients) | Customs, FSSAI in some cases | Variable |
| **Shelf-life testing** | Stability test for claimed shelf life (usually 24 months) | ₹15-30K per product |

### 11.8.2 Voluntary (Trust Signals)

| Certification | Why | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Cruelty-free / Vegan** | Self-declaration or Leaping Bunny | ₹0-15K/year |
| **GMP-certified facility** | Already required | ₹0 |
| **Dermatologically tested** | Independent lab test on 25-50 people | ₹30-80K per product |
| **ISO 22716** | Cosmetics GMP standard | Already required of manufacturer |
| **Made in India** | Self-declaration | ₹0 |
| **Ayush license** (if making Ayurvedic claims) | Required if claiming "Ayurvedic" | ₹50K-1L |

### 11.8.3 What We Will Display on Packaging

- "Made in India 🇮🇳"
- "Cruelty-free 🐰"
- "Dermatologically tested" (after test)
- "Manufactured in GMP-certified facility"
- Batch number, MFG date, EXP date
- MRP, net weight
- Customer care number + email
- Founder's WhatsApp number (for first 6 months)
- QR code → product page with full ingredient breakdown

## 11.9 BEFORE/AFTER CLAIMS (Legally Appropriate)

### 11.9.1 The Law in India

The Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940 + ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) guidelines state:
- **"Before/After" claims must be genuine, not retouched.**
- **Claims must be substantiated.**
- **Individual results may vary** disclaimer is required.

### 11.9.2 How We Handle Before/After

1. **Only with written consent** from customer
2. **Same lighting, same angle, same camera** (or clearly noted)
3. **Real timeline** (e.g., "Day 1 vs Day 30")
4. **Disclaimer:** "Individual results may vary. This is [Name]'s personal experience."
5. **No skin condition cure claims** ("Cures acne" — illegal)
6. **Allowed language:** "Helps reduce appearance of pigmentation over 4-6 weeks" (cosmetic claim, not medical)

### 11.9.3 What We Will NEVER Show

- ❌ Photoshopped before/after
- ❌ "Cures eczema", "Cures acne", "Removes scars" (medical claims)
- ❌ Same person with very different skin tones
- ❌ Makeup-only before/after labeled as skincare

## 11.10 CUSTOMER SUPPORT

### 11.10.1 Channels
- WhatsApp (primary)
- Email (secondary)
- Instagram DMs (responded to)
- Amazon buyer-seller messaging (if sold on Amazon)

### 11.10.2 Response Time SLA
- WhatsApp: < 1 hour during 10 AM-9 PM
- Email: < 12 hours
- Instagram DM: < 6 hours

### 11.10.3 Founder-Powered Support
For first 1,000 customers, founder personally responds to most queries. This is the secret weapon.

### 11.10.4 Common Queries + Scripts

| Query | Response |
|---|---|
| "Is this suitable for sensitive skin?" | "Yes, we formulated it without fragrance or essential oils. If you have very reactive skin, do a patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours." |
| "Can I use this with retinol?" | "Yes, but use vitamin C in the morning and retinol at night. Different routines." |
| "How long until I see results?" | "Most customers see brighter skin in 4 weeks. Pigmentation takes 6-8 weeks." |
| "I got a rash, what do I do?" | "Sorry to hear that. Stop using immediately. Take a photo and share. We'll send a replacement or refund — your choice." |
| "Do you ship to [city]?" | "Yes, we ship pan-India in 4-6 days." |

## 11.11 RETURN & REFUND POLICY

### 11.11.1 Policy (Public)

> **RANGAT Promise:**
> 1. If you don't love the product, return it within 7 days for a full refund — no questions asked.
> 2. If you have a skin reaction, we'll refund + send a dermatologist-recommended alternative.
> 3. If your product arrives damaged, we replace immediately.

### 11.11.2 Implementation
- Display policy on website (prominent banner)
- Print on packaging insert
- Display in WhatsApp welcome message
- Show on Amazon listing

### 11.11.3 Refund Rate Target

Industry standard for skincare returns: 3-5%. Our target: < 2% (because of high product quality and founder-driven support).

If refund rate > 5%, investigate the SKU.
