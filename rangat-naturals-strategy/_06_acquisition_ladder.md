# SECTION 6: CUSTOMER ACQUISITION LADDER

## 6.1 The Principle

We don't "launch a marketing campaign." We **acquire customers one tier at a time, each tier funding the next tier.**

| Tier | Customers | Months | CAC | Funding source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | First 10 | Month 0-1 | ₹0-50 | Founder's network |
| Tier 2 | First 50 | Month 1 | ₹30-80 | Word of mouth + samples |
| Tier 3 | First 100 | Month 1-2 | ₹80-120 | Micro-influencers + salon |
| Tier 4 | First 500 | Month 2-3 | ₹100-180 | Reels + WhatsApp + exhibitions |
| Tier 5 | First 1,000 | Month 3-4 | ₹150-220 | First paid ads (small) |
| Tier 6 | First 5,000 | Month 4-6 | ₹180-260 | Scaling Meta + Amazon |
| Tier 7 | First 10,000 | Month 6-9 | ₹200-280 | Multi-channel, repeat purchase |

---

## 6.2 TIER 1: First 10 Customers (Month 0-1)

**Where they come from:** Founder's personal network. WhatsApp contacts. Family. Close friends.

**What to do:**
1. Founder makes a list of 50 people they genuinely know (not celebrities, not random Instagram followers — actual humans who will pick up the phone).
2. Founder sends each one a personal WhatsApp message **with their face, their name, their ask**. Not a marketing message. A personal message.
3. Offer: "I'm launching my skincare brand. This is my first batch. I'm giving you the first 10 bottles at ₹349 (₹150 off). I need your honest review after 2 weeks. If it doesn't work for your skin, I'll refund you personally."
4. Each recipient gets a **numbered bottle** (Bottle #1 through #10). This makes them feel special.
5. Founder follows up personally after 7 days to ask how their skin feels.

**Marketing approach:** Zero marketing. Zero ad spend. This is **founder sweat equity**.

**Offer:** ₹349 vs ₹499 MRP. 30% off. Free shipping.

**Content:** Founder films a 60-second video of themselves packing the box. Posts on Instagram. "These are going to 10 humans I know personally. Wish me luck."

**Conversion:** Expected 20-30% of 50 contacted → 10-15 orders.

**Repeat:** Each of these 10 customers becomes a WhatsApp contact. Founder messages at Day 30 asking if they want to re-order. Expected 50% reorder = 5 orders.

**Metric to track:** Reviews collected, photos shared by customers, personal NPS (do they recommend to a friend?).

## 6.3 TIER 2: First 50 Customers (Month 1)

**Where they come from:** Friends-of-friends. WhatsApp status. Apartment society word-of-mouth.

**What to do:**
1. The 10 Tier-1 customers are asked: "Know anyone else who'd want to try this? I'll send them a sample for free."
2. Founder makes 50 "sample kits" — each contains a 5ml mini serum, a "Why this works" insert card, and a ₹50-off coupon code valid for 14 days.
3. Founder personally hands/delivers samples to 50 women in their apartment complex, office, college, gym, or family.
4. The 50 samples → expected 30-40 conversions at ₹449 with the coupon.

**Marketing approach:** Still zero paid spend. Word-of-mouth + samples.

**Offer:** ₹449 (10% off) + sample in advance. Or full-size at ₹399 with the sample coupon.

**Content:** "I gave 50 free samples to women in Ahmedabad. Here's what happened." Founder-filmed vlog.

**Conversion:** Sample-to-buy rate in beauty is typically 60-70% if the sample is genuinely good. So 50 samples → 30-35 orders. Plus 10-15 from Tier 1 → **Total 50 customers**.

**Repeat:** Founder messages at Day 35, "Time to re-order. Want me to send another?" Expected 60% reorder (30 orders).

**Metric:** Sample cost per customer, sample-to-buy conversion rate, reorder rate.

## 6.4 TIER 3: First 100 Customers (Month 1-2)

**Where they come from:** First salon partnerships. First micro-influencer posts. First local press.

**What to do:**
1. **3-5 salons** in Prahlad Nagar, Bodakdev, Satellite give our Glow Serum samples to clients during facials. Parlour staff recommend. Client books order via WhatsApp/Instagram.
2. **5 micro-influencers** in Ahmedabad (5K-25K followers) are gifted 1 product each + given a unique code (e.g., "PRIYA15" for 15% off). They post organically. We pay nothing upfront.
3. **Gujarati-language Instagram Reels** by founder go semi-viral — 2-3 reels hit 50K+ views.
4. **First exhibition** at HL College fest or IIM-A cultural event — sell 20-30 bottles in person.

**Marketing approach:** Gifting + salon partnership. Cost: ₹3K-5K (samples only, no cash influencer fee).

**Offer:** ₹449 with first-order discount. Free shipping. Founder-signed thank-you card.

**Content:** Influencer UGC (with permission), salon "shelfie" photos, exhibition BTS.

**Conversion:** 30 salon customers + 25 influencer-driven + 10 founder-network + 35 random from Instagram = **100 customers**.

**Repeat:** Day 35 reminder. Expected 50 reorder = 50 orders.

**Metric:** Cost per influencer (₹), orders per influencer code, salon conversion.

## 6.5 TIER 4: First 500 Customers (Month 2-3)

**Where they come from:** Instagram Reels at scale. WhatsApp broadcast. First ₹15K-25K paid Meta ad.

**What to do:**
1. **Instagram strategy** at full swing: 5-7 Reels per week, 2-3 carousel posts, 1 live per week. Founder-led, ingredient-led, routine-led content.
2. **First paid Meta ad campaign:** ₹500/day for 30 days (₹15,000 total). Targeting women 21-32 in Ahmedabad + 50km radius. Objective: video views → website visits → purchases. Expected ROAS of 1.5-2x at this stage.
3. **WhatsApp broadcast list** at 500-1,000 numbers (collected from society groups, friends, exhibitions, salon clients). Broadcast offer once a week.
4. **Referral program** goes live: "Give ₹100, Get ₹100." Customer shares code with a friend, both get ₹100 off next order.
5. **Local press hits:** 2-3 stories in Ahmedabad Mirror, Gujarat Samachar supplement.

**Marketing approach:** Mix of organic (60%) + paid (40%).

**Offer:** ₹449 for first order, ₹100 off for referrals, free shipping above ₹399.

**Content:** Educational (ingredient breakdown), Reels (founder applying serum, before/after), testimonials (with consent), myth-busting ("Does vitamin C darken skin in sun?").

**Conversion math:**
- 100 from Instagram organic (Tier 3 carryover + new)
- 150 from Meta ads (₹15K spent, AOV ₹500, ROAS 2x)
- 100 from WhatsApp + referrals
- 100 from salons + exhibitions + college ambassadors
- 50 from Amazon (launched mid-Tier 4)
= **500 customers**

**Repeat:** Day 30 + Day 60 reminders. Bundle offer at Day 45 ("Buy serum + moisturizer ₹699"). Expected 200 reorders.

**Metric:** ROAS by ad set, cost per purchase by channel, referral rate.

## 6.6 TIER 5: First 1,000 Customers (Month 3-4)

**Where they come from:** Scaling paid ads. Amazon flywheel. Surat + Vadodara expansion. Influencer network.

**What to do:**
1. **Scale Meta ads** to ₹1,500-2,500/day. Run multiple ad sets (Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara; interest: skincare, minimalist, plum, mCaffeine; age 21-32).
2. **Launch on Amazon** with full A+ content, 4-5 reviews, sponsored product ads. Amazon is a machine for Tier-2/3 cities.
3. **Launch Nykaa** (limited catalog initially — only Glow Serum).
4. **Influencer network** scales to 30 micro-influencers across Gujarat (5-15K followers each, total reach 3-5 lakh).
5. **Launch RANGAT Bundle** (Serum + Moisturizer at ₹699).
6. **WhatsApp automation:** Use Interakt / Wati to send automated re-order reminders.

**Marketing approach:** 50% organic (Instagram + WhatsApp + referrals) + 50% paid (Meta + Amazon).

**Offer:** ₹449 first-order, ₹100 off for referrals, ₹199 off for bundle upgrade, free shipping above ₹399.

**Content:** Bundle-specific Reels ("Save ₹200"), customer transformation stories, ingredient deep-dives.

**Conversion math:**
- 400 from Meta ads (scaled, ₹45K spent, ROAS 2.2x)
- 200 from Amazon (organic + sponsored, ₹20K spent on Amazon ads)
- 150 from Instagram organic + referrals
- 100 from salons, exhibitions, college ambassadors
- 100 from Nykaa + WhatsApp + local
- 50 from new Surat + Vadodara push
= **1,000 customers**

**Repeat:** Bundle push. "You bought serum — try moisturizer at 20% off." Expected 350 reorders.

**Metric:** LTV:CAC ratio (target > 3:1), Amazon BSR rank, bundle attach rate.

## 6.7 TIER 6: First 5,000 Customers (Month 4-6)

**Where they come from:** National Meta ads. Amazon flywheel. Google Search + Shopping. PR hits. Influencer network expansion.

**What to do:**
1. **Meta ads scale to ₹5,000/day** (₹1.5 lakh/month). Test 5+ ad sets across India, scale winners. Retargeting site-visitors and IG engagers.
2. **Google Search ads** for "vitamin c serum", "niacinamide serum", "best serum for glowing skin in India" — high intent, ₹30K-50K/month.
3. **Amazon scaling:** Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Deals of the Day if invited.
4. **Influencer network:** 100 micro-influencers across India (5K-30K followers), gifting model.
5. **PR push:** Indian Express, Hindustan Times, Femina Women's supplement. Founder story pitches.
6. **Nykaa expansion:** 2-3 SKUs on Nykaa, Nykaa ads.
7. **Launch Product #3** (sunscreen). Cross-sell to existing 3,000 customers.

**Marketing approach:** 30% organic + 70% paid.

**Spend:** ₹3-4 lakh/month total marketing.

**Offer:** ₹449 first order, ₹349 festival/launch offer on sunscreen, 20% bundle save.

**Content:** National campaigns ("From Gujarat to your doorstep"), ingredient education, dermatologist collaborations, customer-of-the-month features.

**Conversion math:** 5,000 customers cumulatively by Month 6 = ~2,000 new in Months 4-6.

**Repeat:** Subscription model for serum + moisturizer at ₹399/month (15% discount). Expected 8-10% of customers convert to subscription = 200-400 subscribers.

**Metric:** LTV, CAC by channel, subscription conversion, ROAS by platform.

---

## 6.8 TIER 7: First 10,000 Customers (Month 6-9)

**Where they come from:** Repeat purchases dominate. Brand search volume rises. PR + word-of-mouth flywheel.

**What to do:**
1. **Brand search ads on Google** for "RANGAT NATURALS serum" — these convert at 8-12% because intent is high.
2. **Meta + Google retargeting** on 50,000+ site visitors and 100,000+ engagers.
3. **Amazon flywheel:** 100+ reviews, BSR top 100 in serum category.
4. **Influencer network** at 300+ creators, mostly gifting + revenue share (10% commission).
5. **Subscription push:** Aim for 1,500-2,000 subscribers.
6. **Loyalty program** launch: RANGAT Club. 1 point per ₹10 spent. 100 points = ₹100 off.
7. **Corporate gifting** push: pitch to 30 IT companies for Diwali / Women's Day hampers.

**Marketing approach:** 25% organic + 75% paid (but more efficient now, retargeting is cheaper).

**Spend:** ₹5-7 lakh/month. CAC drops to ₹150-200.

**Offer:** Loyalty rewards, subscription discounts, festival packs.

**Conversion math:** 5,000 new customers in Months 6-9, on top of 5,000 existing = 10,000 cumulative.

**Metric:** LTV by cohort, repeat purchase rate (target 35%), subscription %, NPS.

## 6.9 The Acquisition Flywheel Visual

```
[First 10] friends & family
       ↓ word of mouth
[First 50] sample-driven
       ↓ salon + micro-influencer
[First 100] Instagram organic
       ↓ first ₹15K Meta ad
[First 500] Reels + paid + referrals
       ↓ Amazon + bundles
[First 1,000] Multi-channel + retargeting
       ↓ subscriptions + national ads
[First 5,000] Repeat + scale
       ↓ brand search + loyalty
[First 10,000] Self-sustaining flywheel
```

At 10,000 customers, **40% of new orders come from existing customer recommendations** (referrals + repeat). The brand is now self-fueling.
