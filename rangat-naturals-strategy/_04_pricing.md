# SECTION 4: PRICING STRATEGY

## 4.1 The Unit Economics Reality

A new brand cannot compete on price against giants. We compete on **value density** (active per ₹).

But the unit economics MUST work. If we sell at a loss, we die. If we sell at 30% margin and spend 30% on ads, we break even. We need **60-70% gross margin** to have money left for ads, retargeting, packaging inserts, and (eventually) profit.

Below is the realistic breakdown for each price point. All costs are **per unit**, based on actual private-label quotes from manufacturers in Ahmedabad, Baddi (HP), and Bhiwandi (MH) as of mid-2026.

---

## 4.2 Price Point 1: ₹299 (Entry / Trial)

**Typical product:** Face wash (100ml), Lip balm (10g), Trial-size serum (15ml), Mini moisturizer (30g)

| Line item | Cost (₹) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Product (formula fill)** | 28-38 | Mass formula, simple actives |
| **Packaging (bottle/jar + label + outer box + shrink wrap)** | 22-30 | 100ml HDPE bottle with pump, or matte-finish tube |
| **Shipping to warehouse** | 5 | Bulk truck, ₹5 per unit at 5,000 unit MOQ |
| **Storage (1 month)** | 3 | Shared Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) or Shiprocket warehouse |
| **Payment gateway** | 9 | Razorpay ~3% of ₹299 = ₹9 |
| **Marketplace fee** (if Amazon) | 30 | Referral 10% + closing fee ₹25 + shipping subsidy |
| **Subtotal cost** | **97-115** | |
| | | |
| **Selling price** | **299** | |
| **Gross margin (D2C)** | **₹184-202 (61-68%)** | D2C on our own Shopify site, no marketplace fees |
| **Net margin (after marketplace)** | **₹154-172 (51-58%)** | If sold via Amazon |
| **Marketing budget allowed** (≤ 25% of SP) | **₹75** | Enough for first 1,000 customers |
| **Profit per unit (after marketing)** | **₹77-127** | This is what funds the next batch |

**Verdict on ₹299:** Good for **traffic-pull SKUs** (face wash, mini serum) and as a **first-order offer** to acquire new customers. Not the primary revenue driver. It exists to get a customer into our ecosystem.

## 4.7 The Bundle Strategy (Critical for LTV)

Bundles are how we **lock in 3-product customers** at one transaction.

| Bundle | MRP | Bundle price | Customer saves | Our margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **"Glow Starter"** — Glow Serum (30ml) + Hydra Moisturizer (50g) | ₹898 | ₹699 (22% off) | ₹199 | ~52% |
| **"RANGAT 3-Step Routine"** — Serum + Moisturizer + Sunscreen | ₹1,497 | ₹1,099 (27% off) | ₹398 | ~50% |
| **"Night Ritual"** — Night Cream + Lip Mask + Under-eye cream | ₹1,447 | ₹1,099 (24% off) | ₹348 | ~51% |

Bundle saves are critical because they (a) increase AOV from ₹500 to ₹900, (b) shift customer from 1-product trial to 3-product routine, (c) raise CAC efficiency 2x.

## 4.8 Discount & Promo Policy (CRITICAL)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Never discount on Amazon unless Amazon pays for it (Great Indian Festival etc.)** | Discounts train customers to wait for sales, killing margin |
| **Founder can approve first-100-customer freebies or ₹100-off vouchers** | First 100 customers are seed customers, not regulars |
| **Influencer codes give 15-25% off, not 50%** | 50% off signals "this isn't worth full price" |
| **Subscribe & Save gives 10% off + free shipping** | Locks customer into repeat purchase |
| **Festival/occasion discount capped at 20%** | Protects brand premium perception |

---

## 4.9 The Pricing Architecture Summary

```
₹299  →  Traffic-pull SKU (face wash, mini trial)
₹399  →  Repeat-purchase SKU (moisturizer, body lotion)
₹499  →  Hero SKU (serum) — 60% of revenue target
₹599  →  Premium SKU (sunscreen, night cream)
₹699  →  Bundle or advanced SKU
₹999+ →  Avoid until brand has 50,000+ customers
```

**Rule of thumb:** AOV across all customers should target ₹650. We achieve this by 60% of customers buying 1 product and 40% buying 2+ (driven by bundles and cross-sell).
