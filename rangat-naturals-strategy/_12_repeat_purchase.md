# SECTION 12: REPEAT PURCHASE & LTV ENGINE

## 12.1 Why Repeat Purchase Is THE Metric

A new skincare brand with 1,000 customers can either:
- **A)** Spend ₹200 to acquire 1 new customer every day → 365 new customers/year
- **B)** Get 30% of existing customers to buy twice → 300 orders/year from same base

Option B is 5x cheaper than Option A. And it's the foundation of a brand.

In India, the average skincare reorder cycle is 60-75 days for serums, 90-120 days for moisturizers, 30-60 days for face washes.

**Our target:** 40-50% repeat purchase rate within 90 days. This is achievable with the right engine.

## 12.6 SUBSCRIPTIONS

### 12.6.1 Subscribe & Save Model

**What it is:**
Customer signs up for auto-delivery every 30/45/60 days. Gets 10-15% off + free shipping.

**Products available for subscription (start):**
- Glow Serum (every 60 days)
- Hydra Moisturizer (every 90 days)
- Face wash (every 60 days)
- Sunshield (every 90 days)

**Pricing:**
- One-time: ₹499 + ₹50 shipping
- Subscription: ₹424 (15% off) + free shipping

### 12.6.2 Subscription Tool

- **Loop Subscriptions** (Shopify app, ₹1,500/month)
- **Recharge** (alternative, ₹2,000/month)
- **Native Shopify Subscriptions** (basic, free with Shopify)

### 12.6.3 Subscription Conversion Target

- **Month 3:** 5% of customers subscribed (~10 of 200)
- **Month 6:** 10% subscribed (~150 of 1,500)
- **Month 12:** 15-20% subscribed (~1,500 of 10,000)

### 12.6.4 Subscription Communication

- **D-3:** "Your next Glow Serum ships in 3 days. Pause/skip anytime."
- **D0:** "Shipped! Track here."
- **D+5:** "Should arrive in 2 days."
- **D+14:** "How's the new bottle treating you?"

### 12.6.5 Subscription Pause / Skip Rules

- Pause anytime, no fee
- Skip one delivery
- Cancel anytime
- 90-day minimum to avoid churn (but no penalty for early cancel — better to keep goodwill)

## 12.10 WIN-BACK (For Lapsed Customers)

### 12.10.1 Lapsed Definition

- 90 days no purchase → "Slipping"
- 180 days no purchase → "Lapsed"
- 365 days no purchase → "Churned"

### 12.10.2 Win-Back Sequence

**Slipping (D90):**
> "Hey [Name]! It's been 3 months since your last order. Want to try our new [Product]? 20% off: WELCOMEBACK20. — [Founder]"

**Lapsed (D180):**
> "[Name], we'd love to have you back. ₹200 off your next order + free shipping. Code MISSYOU200. Valid 14 days. — [Founder]"

**Churned (D365):**
> "[Name], a lot has changed in a year. We've launched [new products]. ₹300 off if you give us another chance. Code TRYAGAIN300. — [Founder]"

### 12.10.3 Win-Back Channel Mix

- WhatsApp (personal)
- Email (automated)
- Instagram retargeting ad (₹50/day, lapse list custom audience)

## 12.11 LTV CALCULATION

### 12.11.1 LTV Formula

```
LTV = AOV × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan × Gross Margin
```

### 12.11.2 Conservative LTV Estimate (Year 1)

**Cohort: 1,000 first-time customers**

| Variable | Conservative | Realistic | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| AOV | ₹500 | ₹550 | ₹600 |
| Orders per customer (Year 1) | 1.5 | 2.0 | 2.5 |
| Gross margin | 55% | 60% | 65% |
| **LTV per customer** | **₹413** | **₹660** | **₹975** |

### 12.11.3 LTV:CAC Ratio

If CAC is ₹200 and LTV is ₹660, **LTV:CAC = 3.3:1** — healthy and scalable.

If we can hit optimistic (LTV ₹975), LTV:CAC = 4.9:1 — excellent.
