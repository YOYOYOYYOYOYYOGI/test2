# SECTION 7: LOW-BUDGET STRATEGY

## 7.1 The Budget Reality

Most Indian D2C founders start with personal savings of ₹1-3 lakh and treat every rupee as if it were their last. That's exactly how we plan — because **it is.**

Below are four budgets, each showing exactly where every rupee goes. We assume the founder can do the marketing themselves (shoot Reels on phone, talk to salons, talk to influencers). If founder can't do it, add ₹15-20K/month for a freelance marketing person.

---

## 7.2 THE ₹10,000 BUDGET (Bootstrapping — Month 1 only)

> **Goal:** 20-30 customers + Instagram to 500 followers + product-market fit signal.

| Item | Cost (₹) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| First batch of product (10 bottles) | 1,500 | Enough for samples + first sales |
| Packaging inserts (thank-you cards, founder note) | 200 | Personal touch |
| Free samples (50 mini-samples of 5ml each, manufactured alongside main batch) | 1,500 | Seeds word-of-mouth |
| WhatsApp Business automation (free plan) | 0 | Customer support |
| Canva Pro (1 month) for design | 500 | Instagram posts, packaging design |
| Domain + Shopify trial (3 months free) | 0 | D2C site |
| Free shipping for first 10 orders (₹50-80 per order) | 800 | Encourage trial |
| Founder's phone data for content creation | 500 | 30-day validity |
| **Total** | **₹5,000** | (Buffer: ₹5,000 for misc) |

### What you get for ₹10K
- ✅ 10 paying customers (₹449 × 10 = ₹4,490 revenue)
- ✅ 50 sample recipients (10-15 will convert → 15 customers)
- ✅ Instagram with 500 followers
- ✅ 20-30 pieces of content
- ✅ First 5 reviews

### What NOT to spend on at this stage
- ❌ Paid ads (organic only)
- ❌ Influencer fees (gifting only)
- ❌ Professional photography (phone is enough)
- ❌ Office/warehouse (use founder's bedroom)

## 7.6 The Budget Hierarchy — Where Money Goes As You Grow

| Phase | Monthly budget | Largest line items |
|---|---|---|
| **₹10K (Bootstrapping)** | Founder time + 10 free samples | Founder sweat equity |
| **₹25K (Launch)** | Inventory + first paid ads | Product, Meta test |
| **₹50K (Growth)** | Inventory + scaled Meta + Amazon | Ads + Amazon |
| **₹1L (Scale)** | Ads dominate (50%+ of budget) | Meta + Google + Amazon + Influencers |
| **₹3L+ (National)** | Ads + team + marketplaces | Meta + Amazon + team salaries |
| **₹10L+ (Established)** | TV/OOH/celeb (Phase 3 only) | Mix of paid channels |

### The Golden Rule

> **At every budget level, 40-50% should go to INVENTORY, 30-40% to CUSTOMER ACQUISITION, 10-15% to BRAND/PACKAGING, and 5-10% should be BUFFER.**

If your marketing spend is more than 50% of revenue, you are buying customers you can't afford. If it's less than 15%, you're not acquiring enough.
