# SECTION 16: SALES TARGETS

## 16.1 The Realism Principle

These targets are **conservative**. Most Indian D2C founders overestimate. We will not.

Assumptions:
- AOV starts at ₹500, climbs to ₹700 by Month 12
- CAC starts at ₹0 (founder network), rises to ₹200 by Month 12
- Repeat purchase rate: 25% (M3) → 40% (M12)
- Gross margin: 60% throughout (after marketplace fees)
- Marketing spend: 30% of revenue (M3) → 25% (M12)

## 16.12 The Founder's Salary at Each Stage

| Stage | Founder salary | Why |
|---|---|---|
| M1-3 | ₹0 (reinvest everything) | Survival mode |
| M4-6 | ₹30,000/month | Minimum living wage |
| M7-12 | ₹60,000-₹1,00,000/month | Founder can pay themselves |
| Year 2 | ₹1,50,000-₹2,50,000/month | Reasonable founder salary |
| Year 3 | ₹3,00,000-₹5,00,000/month | Market-rate salary |

Founders often pay themselves last. This is correct. Customers, team, and investors come before founder salary in Year 1.

---

## 16.13 The Reality Check

These targets assume:
- ✅ Founder works 60-80 hours/week
- ✅ At least one helper by Month 3
- ✅ Marketing manager by Month 6
- ✅ Realistic market conditions (no COVID-style disruption)
- ✅ Products deliver as promised (no major quality issues)
- ✅ Founders respond to customer feedback and iterate

If any of these break, targets should be revised **downward**, not upward. Conservative estimates protect the brand.
