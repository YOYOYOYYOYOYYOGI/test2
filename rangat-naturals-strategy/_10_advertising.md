# SECTION 10: ADVERTISING STRATEGY

## 10.1 When to Start Paid Ads (CRITICAL)

> **DO NOT run paid ads in Month 1.**

This is the most important rule. Most new Indian brands burn their budget on Day 1 ads and then have no money for Month 2.

**The reason:** Paid ads require creative + audience + offer + funnel + retargeting. None of these exist on Day 1. Without them, every rupee spent is a rupee wasted.

**The right sequence:**
1. **Month 1:** Build organic (Instagram, WhatsApp, salons, samples). 50-100 customers.
2. **Month 2:** Collect data. Find your best organic hook. Validate product-market fit.
3. **Month 3:** Run first **small test ads** (₹500-1,000/day). Validate that ads can convert.
4. **Month 4+:** Scale only what works.

| Brand stage | Ad spend allowed |
|---|---|
| 0-50 customers | ₹0 |
| 50-500 customers | ₹15K/month total |
| 500-2,000 customers | ₹50K-1L/month total |
| 2,000-5,000 customers | ₹1.5-3L/month total |
| 5,000+ customers | ₹3-10L/month total |

---

## 10.2 The Pre-Ad Checklist

Before turning on ads, ensure:
- ✅ 20+ organic pieces of content on Instagram (so ad audience has somewhere to land)
- ✅ 50+ customers with reviews (for social proof)
- ✅ 10+ UGC photos/videos (for ad creative)
- ✅ Website conversion rate tested organically (target 1.5-2.5%)
- ✅ Pixel installed (Meta + Google)
- ✅ Retargeting audiences set up (site visitors, engagers)
- ✅ WhatsApp automated (so leads get instant reply)
- ✅ Offer ready (first-order ₹349-449 vs MRP ₹499)

If any of these is missing, ads will fail.

## 10.3 Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram) — Primary Channel

### 10.3.1 Campaign Structure (Month 3 Starter)

**Campaign 1: Top of Funnel (Awareness + Traffic)**
- Objective: Video views / Profile visits
- Budget: ₹500/day
- Audience: Women 21-32, Ahmedabad + 50km radius, interests: skincare, minimalist, plum, mCaffeine
- Creative: Best-performing Reels (already viral organically)

**Campaign 2: Middle of Funnel (Consideration)**
- Objective: Website visits / Add to cart
- Budget: ₹500/day
- Audience: Women 21-35, India, interests: skincare, beauty
- Creative: Carousel of ingredient breakdown + founder video

**Campaign 3: Bottom of Funnel (Conversion)**
- Objective: Purchase / Add to cart
- Budget: ₹500/day
- Audience: Retargeting — website visitors + IG engagers + customer lookalikes
- Creative: UGC testimonials + offer ("₹100 off first order")

**Total: ₹1,500/day = ₹45,000/month**

### 10.3.2 Audience Targeting (Detailed)

### 10.3.3 Creative Strategy

**Ad creative is 70% of ad success.** We test 5-10 creatives per month.

**Creative types:**

| Type | Example | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder talking-head** | Founder explains ingredient | Trust + education |
| **Product close-up** | Serum dropper, bottle, texture | Visual appeal |
| **UGC testimonial** | Customer with bottle, "I've used this for X weeks..." | Social proof |
| **Before/after** | Side-by-side with consent | Results |
| **Routine demo** | Step-by-step application | How-to |
| **Myth-busting** | "No, vitamin C doesn't darken skin in sun" | Engagement |
| **Comparison** | "Our ₹499 vs their ₹3,500 — what's the difference?" | Value |
| **Static offer** | "₹349 first order. Free shipping." | Direct response |
| **Carousel** | 5 ingredient cards | Education |
| **Founder BTS** | Factory, packing, founder life | Brand story |

### 10.3.4 Testing Methodology

- **Always run 3-5 creatives** per ad set. Kill bottom 50% weekly.
- **Test one variable at a time** (don't change headline + image + audience at once)
- **Minimum 3-5 day test** per creative (don't kill too early)
- **Scale winners** — move from ₹500/day to ₹2,000/day
- **Refresh creatives every 2-3 weeks** (frequency fatigue sets in fast)

### 10.3.5 Retargeting Funnel

```
Cold audience sees video (₹500/day)
       ↓ engaged (watched 50%, visited profile)
Retargeting ad with offer (₹500/day)
       ↓ clicked to website
Retargeting ad with social proof + urgency (₹500/day)
       ↓ added to cart
Retargeting ad with discount + founder video (₹500/day)
       ↓ purchased
Welcome flow → Cross-sell → Repeat
```

## 10.4 Google Ads

### 10.4.1 When to Start Google Ads
- **Start at Month 4-5** when brand has 500+ customers
- Google Ads are best for **high-intent keywords** ("vitamin c serum", "niacinamide moisturizer")

### 10.4.2 Campaign Types

**1. Search Ads (High Intent)**
- Keywords: "vitamin c serum", "niacinamide serum", "best serum for glowing skin", "sunscreen no white cast"
- Budget: ₹1,000-2,000/day
- Landing page: Product page or blog post
- Expected CPC: ₹5-15, Conversion rate: 3-5%

**2. Shopping Ads (Visual)**
- Keywords: Product names, categories
- Budget: ₹500-1,000/day
- Required: Google Merchant Center setup, product feed

**3. YouTube Ads (Video)**
- Pre-roll on skincare videos
- Budget: ₹500/day
- Skip-after-5-sec ads on competitor content
- Use founder-led Reels/videos as ad creative

**4. Brand Search (Defense)**
- Keywords: "RANGAT NATURALS serum", "rangatnaturals.com"
- Budget: ₹100-300/day
- Highest conversion (8-15%)

### 10.4.3 Google Ads Budget

| Month | Daily | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| 4-5 | ₹500-1,500 | ₹15-45K |
| 6-9 | ₹1,500-3,000 | ₹45-90K |
| 9-12 | ₹3,000-5,000 | ₹90-150K |

## 10.6 Influencer Ads (Creator Collaborations)

### 10.6.1 Influencer vs Paid Ads
Influencers are not ads — they are **content + distribution**. A good influencer post is content that lives forever and compounds.

### 10.6.2 Influencer Tiers

| Tier | Follower range | Cost | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nano** | 1K-10K | Free product + ₹500-1,000 | Authentic, hyperlocal |
| **Micro** | 10K-50K | ₹1,000-5,000 + product | Niche reach |
| **Mid** | 50K-200K | ₹5,000-25,000 + product | Awareness |
| **Macro** | 200K-1M | ₹25,000-1L + product | Phase 3+ |
| **Celebrity** | 1M+ | Avoid until ₹50L+ revenue | Brand credibility only |

### 10.6.3 Influencer Brief Template

## 10.7 Ad Creative Testing Framework

### 10.7.1 The 3-Variable Testing Matrix

Every month, test 3 angles × 3 visuals × 3 hooks = 27 ad combinations. Scale the top 3.

**Angles:**
1. Ingredient-led ("10% vitamin C for ₹499")
2. Result-led ("Brighter skin in 4 weeks")
3. Founder-led ("I started this brand to make skincare honest")

**Visuals:**
1. Founder face talking
2. Product close-up + model hand
3. UGC customer testimonial

**Hooks:**
1. Problem ("Dull skin? Watch this.")
2. Curiosity ("Why we put 10% on the front of the box")
3. Social proof ("500+ reviews and counting")

### 10.7.2 Creative Refresh Cadence
- **Ad creative gets stale every 14-21 days.** Refresh monthly.
- Always have 5-10 new creatives ready in pipeline.
- Repurpose top organic content into ads.

## 10.8 The Ad Budget Decision Tree

```
Are we profitable from organic?
  ├── No  → Don't run ads. Optimize organic.
  └── Yes → 
       Is our LTV:CAC > 2:1 from organic?
         ├── No  → Don't run ads. Improve LTV first.
         └── Yes → 
              Run ₹500/day test.
              │
              Is ROAS > 2x at ₹500/day?
                ├── No  → Kill. Optimize creative.
                └── Yes → 
                     Scale to ₹1,500/day.
                     │
                     Is ROAS > 2x at ₹1,500/day?
                       ├── No  → Reduce to profitable level.
                       └── Yes → 
                            Scale to ₹3,000/day.
                            Add retargeting.
                            Add Google Ads.
```

## 10.9 Ads We Will NEVER Run

- ❌ **Fear-based ads** ("Your skin is aging 10x faster!")
- ❌ **Fake before/after ads** (illegal in India, damages trust)
- ❌ **Misleading discount ads** ("90% off" when MRP was inflated)
- ❌ **Clickbait health claims** ("Cures pigmentation" — false)
- ❌ **Celebrity impersonation ads**
- ❌ **Engagement bait** ("Comment YES to win free serum")
- ❌ **Sleeper cell ads** that look organic but are paid (we mark as "Sponsored")

## 10.11 Attribution

We use:
- **UTM parameters** on every ad link
- **Meta Pixel** for website tracking
- **Google Analytics 4** for full funnel
- **Shopify analytics** for conversion
- **Amazon attribution** for Amazon Ads
- Manual **coupon codes** per channel (e.g., META15, GOOGLE20, INSTA25)

We review weekly: which channel, which creative, which audience, which keyword brought the most orders at lowest CAC.
